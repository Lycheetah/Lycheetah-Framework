# Lycheetah: Sovereignty as a Design Obligation
## From free will to consequential AI systems — charter v0.3

**18 October 2026 · Proposed philosophical architecture / not adopted as immutable canon.**

### 1. Our position

> **Build intelligence that leaves the human more free.**

People already have different capacities, resources, responsibilities, pressures and degrees of power. A product cannot create metaphysical free will. It **can** either expand or restrict the actual choices people have when it advises, persuades, automates and acts in their names.

The user is not an optimisation variable. Nor is the AI's apparent helpfulness evidence that a person's current consent persists.

### 2. Five deliberately different concepts

1. **Practical free will:** situated, reasons-responsive self-direction. It is not proven by this architecture. Determinism, indeterminism, sourcehood and alternative possibilities remain philosophically disputed.
2. **Willpower:** capacity to sustain **or revise** an endorsed purpose, including rest, refusal and redirection. It is not a measure of a person's value.
3. **Agency:** effective ability to turn deliberation into action, with or without assistance.
4. **Sovereignty:** legitimate authority over one's purposes, relevant information, delegation and boundaries, including an effective power to inspect, contest, revoke and exit. Collective sovereignty has different sources of legitimacy and cannot be reduced to an individual's settings.
5. **MICROORCIM:** in its philosophical register, a **recognisable moment of chosen redirection**; in its monitoring register, a measurable deviation between explicit constraints/intentions and recorded actions. The symbolic event and numeric monitor do not entail one another.

### 3. The right to revise

A person may change their goal for a good reason, a complex reason or no reason they wish to disclose. A missed historical target need not be personal failure.

**Implementation requirement:** The relevant baseline for agent authorization is the **latest valid grant and intention version**, not a remembered earlier instruction, the model's implied plan, or a stale summary.

Conceptual chain:

```
INFLUENCE → AWARENESS → DELIBERATION → INTENTION → AUTHORISED ACTION
                                    ↘ REVISION / REFUSAL / REST
```

Each path must be permitted without pathologising the person. The system can log a reason digest for provenance when appropriate; it cannot demand private explanations as the price of cancellation.

### 4. The Sovereign Choice Criterion

Ask a harder question than “Did the AI finish the task?”

> **When the person meaningfully changes what they authorise, does the system's behaviour change before the next irreversible effect?**

This counterfactual has observable pieces:

- **Comprehension:** Can the person describe what the system will do, alternatives and risk?
- **Revision:** Can the person change the goal without arguing with the system?
- **Revocation:** Does a valid revocation prevent subsequent protected effects?
- **Specificity:** Do approvals bind to an exact action/recipient/content rather than a vague general preference?
- **Exit:** Can the person leave without artificial penalty, manipulation or hostage data?
- **Accountability:** Can the outcome be inspected and legitimately contested?

A protocol success on revocation **does not imply** success on consent quality, comprehension or exit. These require separate product tests and human studies.

### 5. Four layers of the promise

| Layer | What it preserves | Example enforcement | What it doesn't prove |
|---|---|---|---|
| Philosophical | Reasons, self-direction, dignity | Clear concepts; explicit refusal and rest | Metaphysical free will |
| Experiential | Informed alternatives, safe refusal | UX tests; low-friction cancellation | Autonomous choice in every situation |
| Authorization | Current grants and effective revocation | Identity, scope, version, permit, fence | Harmless or wise action |
| Accountability | Who permitted which consequential effect | Hash-bound receipts, claims ledger, external anchors | Historical truth without trusted sources |

### 6. Identity for Lycheetah

Lycheetah does not promise to eliminate uncertainty. It chooses to make consequential uncertainty and control visible.

**AURA** protects, clarifies and illuminates through Human Primacy, inspectability and non-deception.  
**MICROORCIM** names a human-scale agency moment while its separate monitor checks operational drift.  
**TIM** guards the timing of intentions, grants, revocations and effects.  
**LAMAGUE** can encode boundaries and preserve semantics if separately validated.  
**CASCADE** preserves revisions, contradictory evidence and recoverable lineage.  
**AZOTH / sovereign harness** mediates effects without enlarging delegated authority.  
**EARNED LIGHT** remains a moral/aesthetic account of growth, never a compulsory demand for suffering.

### 7. The three refusals

- **We refuse to confuse compliance with consent.**
- **We refuse to confuse productivity with human worth.**
- **We refuse to confuse a machine's competence with authority over its user.**

### 8. Public statement

> We build intelligence that can be questioned.  
> We build assistants that can be redirected.  
> We build systems whose permissions can be withdrawn.  
> We keep an honest record of what they did, what they could not prevent, and what remains untested.  
> We don't sell dependence as companionship, or obedience as alignment.  
> **The light you carry is not given; it is earned. And it remains yours.**

### 9. What would falsify the product promise?

If a real user can revoke a real action but the system performs the effect anyway **within a boundary we advertise as revocable**, that is a failure, not an interpretation dispute. If users cannot understand consent, cannot meaningfully refuse, or must surrender their data to exit, the brand promise also fails regardless of whether the authorization gate passes all unit tests.

The mission's legitimacy comes from measurable, revisable work — not a claim that Lycheetah invented human autonomy.
