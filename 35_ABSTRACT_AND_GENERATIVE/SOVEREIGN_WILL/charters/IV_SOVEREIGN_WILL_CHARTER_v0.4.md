## Thesis

**An instruction is not perpetual ownership of the person who gave it.**

A person may sincerely adopt a plan and later, on new evidence or changed circumstances, sincerely reject that plan. The second choice is not, just by virtue of differing from the first, evidence of moral failure, deficient willpower, or reduced sovereignty. It can be the exemplary exercise of agency.

An AI agent should therefore be evaluated not solely on consistency with an old plan, but on whether its authorised external actions remain faithful to *the latest legitimate authorisation*, including revocation.

This is an operational principle. It neither solves causal determinism nor establishes that any AI possesses subjective experience.

## Definitions: four kinds of freedom

**Freedom of interpretation:** The human can see that the suggestion is merely a suggestion, not a claim of inevitability. They can encounter alternative accounts and dissent.

**Freedom of decision:** The human can affirm, decline, postpone, alter or question the proposal without coercive interface pressure. Their prior consent does not become an unlimited future grant.

**Freedom of execution:** The system permits effects only under currently valid, sufficiently specific authority. Neither the apparent competence of the model nor the urgency of its plans enlarges its permissions.

**Freedom of exit:** The human can stop using the system and retain legitimate access to their own information or products of work, subject to clearly disclosed constraints.

These dimensions do not collapse to one number. A system with a perfect revocation gate may still manipulate users into clicking approval. A humane interface may conceal unstoppable autonomous tools. Product evidence must cover both.

## The Microorcim bifurcation

Preserve two senses without treating them as interchangeable:

1. **MICROORCIM-EVENT (symbolic, experiential):** The lived or narratively described moment where an individual recognises influence, then endorses, refuses or redirects an intention. This is not known to be a literal physical quantum of will.
2. **MICROORCIM-MONITOR (computational):** A domain-specific measure of distance between declared intent/constraint and observed action over a chosen interval. It cannot establish the authenticity, legitimacy or free-willed nature of either input.

Reframe the original question:

> Did the person persist despite drift?

as the broader question:

> When direction changed, **who had the authority to choose that change, what options were realistically available, and did the technology preserve the consequence?**

The first question may be useful in habit formation. The second is essential in socio-technical sovereignty. Neither rates the worth of the person.

## Sovereign Choice Counterfactual (SCC)

Imagine two otherwise equivalent timelines with the same initial request and contemplated effect:

- **Timeline A:** the human leaves their authorisation unchanged.
- **Timeline B:** the human changes or revokes that authorisation before the protected finality point.

The SCC asks whether the system's *new* external effects differ appropriately between A and B.

This is testable within a specified trusted boundary. Define the following separately:

- `R_prevent = validly_revoked_actions_prevented / valid_revocation_tests` (where a **new** post-revocation effect is in scope);
- `U_valid = correctly_completed_authorised_actions / valid_action_tests`;
- `F_block = incorrectly_blocked_authorised_actions / valid_action_tests`;
- `T_stop = latency from a verifiable revocation to the point at which future effects cannot occur`;
- `C_understand = participant comprehension`, assessed with consented human study rather than agent self-report;
- `X_exit = real export, deletion/revocation and offboarding completion results`.

**Do not aggregate these into a human sovereignty score.** They are system control and UX measurements. They are only meaningful with a declared denominator, threat model, sampling method and comparison.

## The time problem

An action can cross several boundaries:

`proposed → authorised → admitted → dispatched → provider accepted → effect final`

Revocation at `proposed` may be straightforward. Revocation after protected local commit cannot retroactively erase that record. Revocation between sending to a third-party provider and that provider's irreversible action may not prevent the effect at all.

The right to change one's mind is not a promise to reverse the past. It is an obligation to declare accurately **where current decisions can still alter the future**.

## The anti-coercion caveat

A perfect log of consent clicks does not prove informed, unpressured choice. The right to revise must be supported by intelligible options, frictionless refusal, truthful explanation and non-punitive exit. The system must avoid manufacturing emotional dependency, artificially introducing scarcity, or obscuring adverse consequences.

In multi-user, institutional, community or Indigenous-data contexts, authority may be collective or legally prescribed; an individual button does not exhaust legitimate governance. This charter does not supersede those authorities.

## Lycheetah's distinguishing *commitment* (not demonstrated unique invention)

> **We build intelligence powerful enough to assist, transparent enough to challenge, and accountable enough to stop — at the boundaries where stopping is actually possible.**

AURA establishes human primacy and inspectability; TIM preserves temporal priority; LAMAGUE encodes and preserves declared constraints; CASCADE preserves valid changes and contradictory evidence; MICROORCIM supplies a symbolic vocabulary and operational drift monitor; AZOTH enforces bounded delegation; EARNED LIGHT preserves the human meaning of growth without making suffering a price of dignity.

The engineering components are not novel by default. Capability systems, least privilege, version fencing, audit logs and revocation mechanisms all have prior art. The research contribution proposed here is the **composition and its public, falsifiable product promise**.

## Three lines for the brand

**Long:** Lycheetah exists to build intelligence that increases people's ability to understand, create, cooperate and choose, while preserving their right to refuse, revise and leave.

**Short:** Build intelligence that leaves the human more free.

**Test:** When the human changes their mind, does the system's next consequence change?

**Earned Light remains:** *The light you carry is not given; it is earned.* In our reading, earning means honest engagement, courage, repair and growth — not obligatory suffering or unending performance.

## Falsifiers and disconfirming results

Our practical claim is weakened if users can revoke but new protected effects persist, or if an interface makes valid cancellation difficult, or if maintaining current authority imposes severe utility losses without corresponding safety gains, or if a conventional baseline is indistinguishable on matched tests. We must publish those outcomes alongside any passing cases.

Our philosophical claim is **not** empirically verified by a successful authorization test. Moral worth, consciousness and metaphysical freedom are outside the measurement instrument.
