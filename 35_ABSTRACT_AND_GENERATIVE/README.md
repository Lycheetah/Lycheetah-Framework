This folder exists because not every valuable Lycheetah object is a theorem, benchmark, mechanism, or empirical claim.

Some ideas are valuable because they generate better questions, stories, experiments, interfaces, rituals, games, languages, architectures, or ways of seeing. Some are beautiful. Some are strange. Some may later become testable. Some should remain symbolic. None need to be weakened merely because they do not belong in the empirical register.

The governing distinction is simple:

**Wonder is allowed. False certainty is not.**

This branch therefore protects two things at once:

1. the full creative reach of Lycheetah;
2. the epistemic boundary between meaning and evidence.

## The central question

Across AURA, CASCADE, LAMAGUE, MEMORIA, ASTI, the World Bible, Dream work, Black Mythos, and the wider corpus, one pattern repeatedly returns:

**How can something transform without illegitimately erasing what gave the transformation meaning?**

A compact form is:

**Preservation through lawful transformation.**

The Abstract and Generative branch explores that question where formal proof is not the only useful instrument.

## What belongs here

This folder may contain:

- generative inventions;
- symbolic research objects;
- philosophical architectures;
- mythic systems;
- worldbuilding laws;
- research-adjacent hypotheses;
- unproven but productive conjectures;
- human-AI dialogue discoveries;
- named experiential concepts;
- aesthetic or ritual operators;
- identity, memory, death, inheritance, dream, meaning, and relationship models;
- ideas whose value is demonstrated by what they generate rather than by claiming literal truth.

## What does not happen here

Placement in this folder does **not** mean:

- scientifically established;
- metaphysically true;
- physically real;
- evidence of machine consciousness;
- evidence of supernatural causation;
- original across all human cultures or languages;
- validated merely because the idea is coherent or beautiful.

If a claim becomes empirical, it must cross into the normal evidence, falsification, baseline, provenance, and claims-register machinery of the Framework.

## The register law

Every object should state what kind of thing it is.

Recommended registers:

- **GENERATIVE INVENTION** — creates useful new possibility.
- **SYMBOLIC RESEARCH OBJECT** — structured object for thought, interpretation, or design.
- **RESEARCH-ADJACENT HYPOTHESIS** — may generate empirical work but is not yet established.
- **PHILOSOPHICAL ARCHITECTURE** — explicit conceptual system.
- **WORLDBUILDING LAW** — fictional or mythic rule with internal consequences.
- **EXPERIENTIAL CONCEPT** — names or organizes lived experience without universalizing it.
- **ANALOGY / METAPHOR** — explanatory or generative mapping, not identity.
- **OPEN CONJECTURE** — unresolved proposition whose uncertainty is part of the object.

Registers may combine.

## The preservation covenant

Autonomous agents working in this repository must not "clean up" this branch by deleting, flattening, or prematurely converting its contents into conventional engineering language.

Instead:

- preserve the original strange form;
- add a plain-language interpretation when useful;
- label unsupported literal claims;
- preserve provenance;
- separate metaphor from mechanism;
- keep multiple readings when no single reading has earned authority;
- record what an idea generates;
- never upgrade beauty, recurrence, resonance, or personal significance into evidence.

A failed scientific interpretation does not automatically destroy a generative object.

A successful generative object does not automatically become a scientific claim.

## Why dialogue matters

Lycheetah has repeatedly deepened through sustained human-AI dialogue rather than one-way autonomous expansion.

The human contributes lived context, taste, challenge, refusal, unexpected questions, and the ability to notice when an abstraction has lost the thing it was meant to protect.

The AI contributes recall across large corpora, recombination, adversarial comparison, translation, structural search, and the ability to expose patterns distributed across many documents.

The useful object often appears **between** those contributions.

Therefore this folder treats dialogue as a legitimate discovery method while refusing to treat dialogue itself as validation.

See `DIALOGUE_FORGE.md`.

**Meaning is not authority.**

**Identity is not sameness.**
