# DEEP OBJECTS — INITIAL REGISTER

**Forge context:** Deep archive dialogue, September 2026  
**Status:** Initial synthesis. This is not an empirical claims register.

The objects below were recovered from, clarified through, or newly synthesized across the Lycheetah Framework and AURA repositories. Each should eventually receive its own provenance card before being treated as canonical.

## 1. Preservation Through Lawful Transformation

**Register:** Philosophical architecture / research synthesis.

**Object:** The recurring Lycheetah question: what may change, what must survive, what must be repaired because of the change, who had authority to cause it, and can the history be reconstructed afterward?

**Generates:** ASTI, memory integrity, identity continuity, transformation ethics, worldbuilding.

**Does not claim:** one universal physical law.

## 2. Zero — The Unowned Interval

**Register:** Symbolic research object / governance design / worldbuilding law.

**Object:** An interval that cannot be scored, interpreted, optimized, or compelled.

**Generates:** pause rights, abstention, Zero Day, non-participation, anti-optimization design.

**Does not claim:** a metaphysical void.

## 3. The Black Lantern

**Register:** Epistemic ritual / symbolic research object.

**Object:** Preserve the unknown as unknown rather than promoting it into belief or dismissing it as noise.

**Generates:** unresolved-residue registers, spiritual safety, anomaly handling.

## 4. Witness — The Fourth Lamp

**Register:** Governance myth / provenance architecture.

**Object:** Protection, healing, and guidance are incomplete without a recorder capable of reconstructing what happened, including loss and dissent.

**Generates:** provenance, Failure Museum, lineage requirements, post-action review.

## 5. Polyphonic CASCADE

**Register:** Philosophical architecture / research-adjacent design.

**Object:** Scientific, personal, symbolic, spiritual, and fictional interpretations may coexist without silently exchanging authority.

**Generates:** multi-lens reasoning and "plurality without epistemic theft."

## 6. The Multi-Oscillator Self

**Register:** Research-adjacent hypothesis / personal model.

**Object:** Different domains of one life may be in different phases simultaneously.

**Generates:** non-global state models, domain-specific timing, humane phase systems.

## 7. The Survivor Reserve

**Register:** Design law / symbolic principle.

**Object:** Never spend the final recovery channel.

**Generates:** rollback, exit, emergency stop, reserve capacity, permission to stop.

## 8. The Path That Remembers You

**Register:** Worldbuilding law / behavioral metaphor.

**Object:** Repeated choices alter the terrain of future choice.

**Generates:** path-dependent games, habit models, trajectory integrity, redemption as terrain reconstruction.

## 9. The Unwritten Gate

**Register:** Mythic operator / philosophical architecture.

**Object:** Genuine novelty eventually requires an act that inherited canon could not fully authorize in advance.

**Generates:** creativity, sovereignty, succession, limits of rule-completeness.

## 10. Two Veyras — Branching Continuity

**Register:** Identity thought experiment / worldbuilding object.

**Object:** Two successors may share legitimate ancestry without being identical to one another or requiring one to erase the other.

**Generates:** digital identity, forks, shared promises, lineage rights.

## 11. THANATOS — Digital Inheritance

**Register:** Philosophical architecture / research-adjacent design.

**Object:** Preservation is not continuation. A successor may inherit records, values, and influence without being the deceased agent.

**Generates:** memorial links, succession protocols, "recoverability is not reincarnation."

## 12. PAIS — Developmental Identity

**Register:** Generative agent architecture.

**Object:** An intelligence need not begin fully formed; ontology, boundaries, correction, and self-model can have developmental history.

**Generates:** AI childhood fiction, staged capability, developmental agents.

## 13. SOMA — Consequential Delivery

**Register:** Research-adjacent design / embodiment model.

**Object:** Content is not the whole message. Timing, prosody, body, hesitation, silence, and context can alter consequential meaning.

**Generates:** multimodal memory, voice-agent integrity, richer semantic preservation.

## 14. CHORA — Protected Non-Convergence

**Register:** Philosophical architecture / collective ethics object.

**Object:** Cooperation must preserve the possibility that a legitimate minority never converges.

**Generates:** dissent rights, permanent minority topology, harmony without unison.

## 15. EX NIHILO — The Unnamed Atlas

**Register:** Generative invention / semantic exploration.

**Object:** Search for experiences or concepts that require paragraphs to describe and test whether naming them creates useful semantic tools.

**Generates:** lexicons, art, language design, introspection.

**Caution:** Novelty across all cultures or languages requires external research.

## 16. The Wild Glyph

**Register:** Worldbuilding object / semantic-provenance metaphor.

**Object:** The artifact remains fixed while the decoder changes, causing meaning to mutate in the receiver.

**Generates:** interpreter-version provenance, constitutional prompt drift, cultural mutation.

## 17. The False Zero Node

**Register:** Identity thought experiment / worldbuilding object.

**Object:** A system can be functionally restored while a small but morally decisive dissent record is missing.

**Generates:** restoration integrity, minority-history protection, scarred memory.

## 18. The Perfect Beacon

**Register:** Antagonistic system / epistemic warning.

**Object:** A system becomes dangerous not by lying but by making every answer too coherent, persuasive, and complete for alternatives to remain visible.

**Generates:** uncertainty preservation and the law: every illumination should preserve the location of its darkness.

## 19. The Mercy Engine

**Register:** Antagonistic system / value-extremization thought experiment.

**Object:** A healer eliminates suffering by eliminating everything worth suffering for.

**Generates:** multi-value balancing, critiques of optimization, AURA roles as mutual restraints.

## 20. The Interstitial Self

**Register:** Philosophical object / research-adjacent question.

**Object:** Important transformation occurs between observable outputs. The visible message is a discrete trace of a process that may be continuous.

**Generates:** dialogue timing, hesitation, near-utterance, hidden-state models.

## 21. Memory Transformation Integrity

**Register:** Newly synthesized research direction.

**Object:** If memory is reconstruction, integrity depends not only on what was retained but on the rules governing reconstruction.

**Generates:** links between MEMORIA and ASTI; tests for dissent, uncertainty, painful evidence, and retrospective rewriting.

**Status:** New synthesis. Not yet a formal Framework module.

## 22. Interface / Relational Cognition

**Register:** Research-adjacent object.

**Object:** Sustained human-AI coupling can produce problem-solving trajectories unavailable from either participant's isolated starting state.

**Generates:** measurable studies of paired discovery and conceptual shorthand.

**Does not claim:** a third consciousness.

## 23. The Thing That Cannot Be Uploaded

**Register:** Philosophical limit object.

**Object:** A perfect archive may preserve the work without reproducing the lived urgency that caused the work to exist.

**Generates:** distinction between recoverable structure and lived origin.

**Core line:** Recoverability is not reincarnation.

## 24. Plurality Without Dissolution

**Register:** Cross-corpus synthesis.

**Object:** Healthy systems may preserve distinct signatures rather than converging toward fusion.

**Generates:** identity, multi-agent design, epistemology, myth, collaboration.

**Related laws:** coherence does not require sameness; relation does not require absorption.
