# Crossovers: combine mechanisms, then test the result

**Status: PROPOSED product and game briefs; no prototype implied.**

The corpus is a lens, not a finite idea menu. Combine two mechanisms, name the tension between them, and build a small encounter that proves the tension can be experienced.

[Catalogue home](README.md) · [Build contract](07_BUILD_CONTRACT_AND_SEQUENCE.md)

## LYC-B027 · The Unnamed Atlas

**Form:** crossover · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Map a place whose names are useful hypotheses rather than commands that make it real.

- **Source to inspect:** [35_ABSTRACT_AND_GENERATIVE/DEEP_OBJECTS.md](../../35_ABSTRACT_AND_GENERATIVE/DEEP_OBJECTS.md). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** An invented landscape, discoveries, user-chosen labels and route constraints.
- **Interaction:** Explore → name a feature → use the label to remember a pattern → discover an exception → rename with history → uncover a new path.
- **Artifact:** A player-authored atlas with original observations beneath every later label.
- **First build:** One island, six discoveries and two routes opened by understanding rather than combat.
- **Acceptance:** Renaming never erases the earlier observation; two players can use different labels and solve the same route; the atlas remains navigable without mythic terminology.
- **Limits:** Combine Anamnesis, MEMORIA and original fiction. Naming is a game interface, not metaphysical authority or a cultural borrowing licence.

## LYC-B028 · The False Beacon

**Form:** crossover · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Detect a guide whose confident advice conflicts with the evidence on the ground.

- **Source to inspect:** [papers/agent-safety/frontier-controls/README.md](../../papers/agent-safety/frontier-controls/README.md). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** A synthetic route, a fallible guide, observable hazards and a player-controlled notebook.
- **Interaction:** Hear advice → inspect a clue → test a low-risk claim → record a discrepancy → choose whether to follow, challenge or ignore the guide.
- **Artifact:** An expedition replay comparing advice, evidence and actual outcomes.
- **First build:** One fifteen-minute route with truthful, mistaken and deliberately deceptive fictional messages.
- **Acceptance:** Confidence is never the only clue; a sceptical player has a viable route; every deception has discoverable evidence; the final explanation connects cause and consequence.
- **Limits:** Combine AURA, CASCADE and agent-safety themes as fiction. Do not impersonate a real authority, diagnose gullibility or claim a detector for real deception.

## LYC-B029 · The Scarred Archive

**Form:** crossover · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Recover an archive without erasing the traces that explain why it broke.

- **Source to inspect:** [21_MEMORIA/](../../21_MEMORIA/). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** Fictional fragments, competing reconstructions, protected originals and repair choices.
- **Interaction:** Find a fragment → propose a reconstruction → compare contradictions → preserve a scar or repair a gap → unlock a story from the recorded difference.
- **Artifact:** A branching archive in which every reconstruction retains its source fragments.
- **First build:** Eight fragments, three plausible reconstructions and an ending shaped by what was preserved.
- **Acceptance:** The player can revisit discarded interpretations; a repair cannot overwrite its evidence; more than one coherent ending remains possible.
- **Limits:** Combine MEMORIA, CHRYSOPOEIA and Mythos. Do not advertise fictional reconstruction as recovery of actual traumatic or forgotten memories.

## LYC-B030 · Living Siege

**Form:** crossover · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Make the long tower-defence campaign about an evolving base, champion movement and divergent tower chains.

- **Source to inspect:** [LYCHEETAH_EPIC/](../../LYCHEETAH_EPIC/). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** Large readable maps, mostly available base towers, hero loadouts, wave seeds and a persistent campaign save.
- **Interaction:** Expand the base → plan a single path or a short fork that rejoins → place a niche party → survive a wave → choose an evolution → explore a region → prestige with an explicit benefit.
- **Artifact:** A campaign save with tower XP, hero progression, completed regions and inspectable damage calculations.
- **First build:** One large map, six base towers with two divergent evolutions each, one movable hero and a boss encounter. Treat these as proposed acceptance scope for a later game batch.
- **Acceptance:** The whole field fits the phone with overlay menus; towers pivot and attacks read clearly; at least two builds win; save/resume works; bosses require changed decisions; reward and offline progress limits are explicit.
- **Limits:** Combine TRIAD adaptation, HARMONIA synergies, CASCADE route discovery and Epic fiction as authored game systems. No gambling, forced retention or performance claim. Keep this brief aligned with the actual game owner before implementation.

## LYC-B031 · Recovery Race Lab

**Form:** tool / crossover · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Show why a valid rollback plan can damage a later legitimate update.

- **Source to inspect:** [papers/agent-safety/compensation-race-benchmark/README.md](../../papers/agent-safety/compensation-race-benchmark/README.md). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** Two synthetic resources, original values, a repair permit and a scheduled second writer.
- **Interaction:** Step through precheck → inject a later update → repair → compare weak, sequential and atomic strategies → inspect exactly which values changed.
- **Artifact:** A replay of the event schedule and before/after resource states.
- **First build:** An offline visualisation of the four public deterministic fixtures; no private causal-repair mechanisms.
- **Acceptance:** The stale atomic case performs zero repair writes; the clean case restores both targets; weak and sequential failures remain visible; changing the schedule changes the result for an explained reason.
- **Limits:** Use only the public benchmark. The mitigation is conventional transaction/concurrency discipline, not novelty, a real-provider proof or a production recovery service.
