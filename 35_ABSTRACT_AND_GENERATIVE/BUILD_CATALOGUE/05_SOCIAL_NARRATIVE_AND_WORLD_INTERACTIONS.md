## LYC-B024 · Glyph Forge

**Form:** interaction · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Invent a small symbolic language and see whether another person can use it.

- **Source to inspect:** [03_LAMAGUE_L1/](../../03_LAMAGUE_L1/). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** Original glyphs, explicit meanings, composition rules and a chosen puzzle message.
- **Interaction:** Define → compose → hand the message to a second reader → compare interpretation → repair ambiguous rules → try a fresh message.
- **Artifact:** A vocabulary sheet, grammar and record of misunderstandings.
- **First build:** Twelve original glyphs and five messages, with plain text alternatives.
- **Acceptance:** An unfamiliar reader can attempt the puzzle without lore; ambiguity is recorded as failure; glyphs have accessible labels and a text fallback.
- **Limits:** A playable grammar is not a universal language or evidence that the reader shares an agent’s internal state.

## LYC-B025 · Many Crowns

**Form:** game · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Negotiate a fictional council decision where allies remember whose interests were ignored.

- **Source to inspect:** [32_TIANXIA/](../../32_TIANXIA/). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** Fictional factions, visible demands, resources and independently authored consequences.
- **Interaction:** Listen → propose → bargain → vote or refuse → live with the consequences → renegotiate after the world changes.
- **Artifact:** A council history and a world state with traceable political consequences.
- **First build:** Three factions and one scarce-resource dispute with at least three viable settlements.
- **Acceptance:** A majority win can still have visible costs; no single slider erases dissent; a settlement affects the next encounter and can be reconsidered.
- **Limits:** The game explores tradeoffs. It does not establish the legitimacy of any real governance system or collapse cultures into one rule.

## LYC-B026 · Maker Trials

**Form:** interaction · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Turn learning into making something that another person can inspect and use.

- **Source to inspect:** [14_MYSTERY_SCHOOL/MODELCRAFT/README.md](../../14_MYSTERY_SCHOOL/MODELCRAFT/README.md). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** A task, worked example, rubric, time allowance and a learner-controlled artifact.
- **Interaction:** Study a small example → attempt a fresh problem → inspect feedback → revise → test transfer with another problem later.
- **Artifact:** A private portfolio artifact with revision history and a learner-written reflection.
- **First build:** One local HTML-building trial and one later transfer task; no grades about identity.
- **Acceptance:** The rubric exists before the attempt; feedback names a specific repair; successful transfer is checked with a fresh problem; skipping and returning remain available.
- **Limits:** This is a curriculum experiment. Learning-effectiveness claims need an appropriate study; existing lesson structures are not proof of outcomes.
