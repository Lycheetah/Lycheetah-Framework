# Adventure, inheritance and long progression

**Status: PROPOSED product and game briefs; no prototype implied.**

The long game needs paths worth choosing, enemies that ask different questions, and discoveries that alter a build. Bigger numbers need readable causes and strategic consequences.

[Catalogue home](README.md) · [Build contract](07_BUILD_CONTRACT_AND_SEQUENCE.md)

## LYC-B019 · Living Dungeon

**Form:** game · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Explore a dungeon whose route changes when you discover evidence about its hidden structure.

- **Source to inspect:** [01_CASCADE_L4/](../../01_CASCADE_L4/). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** A seeded map, clues, revisable hypotheses and named route conditions.
- **Interaction:** Explore → propose what a symbol means → test it → discover a contradiction → revise → open a different route. Wrong hypotheses create recoverable detours.
- **Artifact:** An expedition journal that retains clues, false starts and unlocked routes.
- **First build:** One floor, three theories, two correct routes and one deliberate false clue.
- **Acceptance:** The player can trace every route change to a clue; the false clue has a discoverable counterexample; no irreversible soft lock follows an early guess.
- **Limits:** Fictional belief revision drives the map. Game correctness is not evidence that CASCADE determines truth in open real-world problems.

## LYC-B020 · Inheritance Roguelike

**Form:** game · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Lose a run while keeping discoveries and consequences that change the next expedition.

- **Source to inspect:** [21_MEMORIA/](../../21_MEMORIA/). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** A hero, route seed, discovered techniques and explicitly selected inherited artifacts.
- **Interaction:** Travel → take a risk → record a discovery → fall or return → choose what the next hero inherits → face a changed world.
- **Artifact:** A lineage ledger separating inherited knowledge from current power.
- **First build:** One biome, two hero roles and five discoveries that unlock different routes rather than flat damage alone.
- **Acceptance:** Inheritance creates a new choice within the next run; a fresh save remains viable; death does not erase the player’s understanding; the ledger can explain each inherited effect.
- **Limits:** A lineage is fictional save data, not transfer of consciousness. Avoid grief-based retention or pressure to keep an avatar alive for the app.

## LYC-B021 · The Last Lantern

**Form:** game · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Guide an expedition by protecting enough reserve to return, while temptation grows around you.

- **Source to inspect:** [05_MICROORCIM_L5/](../../05_MICROORCIM_L5/). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** A fictional lantern reserve, travel costs, route hazards and optional rescues.
- **Interaction:** Plan → travel → discover a reward or rescue → inspect the return cost → continue, reroute or go home → buy a new capability.
- **Artifact:** A route journal and a family of reserve-management builds.
- **First build:** A branching route with three optional objectives, visible return estimates and one uncertain hazard.
- **Acceptance:** At least one generous and one cautious strategy can succeed; uncertain costs are labelled; return is a real playable choice rather than a hidden failure.
- **Limits:** Reserve mechanics concern fictional travel. They do not diagnose a person’s resilience, agency or fatigue.

## LYC-B022 · Ruins of Recognition

**Form:** game · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Find repeated patterns in an invented civilisation and learn when resemblance misleads you.

- **Source to inspect:** [07_ANAMNESIS_L0/](../../07_ANAMNESIS_L0/). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** Original fictional symbols, environmental clues and competing puzzle interpretations.
- **Interaction:** Observe a motif → compare ruins → try an interpretation → notice a difference → assemble the rule → uncover another site.
- **Artifact:** An atlas of discovered patterns with confirmed and rejected interpretations.
- **First build:** Three ruins, one shared rule and one false resemblance; commission original symbols.
- **Acceptance:** The false resemblance can be disconfirmed from available clues; the correct rule transfers to a new puzzle; colour alone never carries a solution.
- **Limits:** Use invented cultures for the prototype. Named living traditions require specific sourcing, permission where appropriate and human review.

## LYC-B023 · Champion Expeditions

**Form:** game · **Build status:** PROPOSED

Build a niche party and take it through meaningful paths, bosses and camp upgrades.

- **Source to inspect:** [LYCHEETAH_EPIC/](../../LYCHEETAH_EPIC/). This is a lineage pointer, not a statement that the proposed product already exists.
- **Inputs:** Hero roles, tower or unit choices, route seeds and explicit account-level unlocks.
- **Interaction:** Choose a party → scout the route → fight a readable encounter → pick a consequential reward → reach a boss → return with an upgrade that changes future choices.
- **Artifact:** A persistent campaign with party loadouts, discovered regions and explained progression.
- **First build:** One full region, three champions, three enemy roles and one boss with two phases.
- **Acceptance:** Two distinct parties can finish the region; a reward changes a later decision; the campaign resumes correctly; no level requires a paid hero or endless grinding.
- **Limits:** This is a prospective game brief. Existing tower-defence work has its own repository and ownership; this catalogue does not mark it complete or authorise a rewrite.
