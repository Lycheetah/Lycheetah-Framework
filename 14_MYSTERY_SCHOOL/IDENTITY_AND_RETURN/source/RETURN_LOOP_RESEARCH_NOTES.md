# RESEARCH BASIS — RETURN LOOP v0.3

This system is design work, not a validated intervention.

External evidence consulted while drafting:
- Li, Hew & Du (2024), *Gamification enhances student intrinsic motivation, perceptions of autonomy and relatedness, but minimal impact on competency: a meta-analysis and systematic review*. Educational Technology Research and Development.
  - Useful design signal: autonomy and relatedness matter; public absolute rankings can create discomfort for low-ranked learners.
- International Review of Education (2025), *Gamification in education and training: A literature review*.
  - Useful design signal: game elements do not automatically produce healthy motivation; meaningful choice and autonomy matter.
- UK Department for Science, Innovation & Technology (2026), *Growing up in the online world* consultation evidence summary.
  - Useful risk signal: respondents and stakeholders identified streaks, daily-login bonuses, intermittent reinforcement, infinite scroll, notifications and other persuasive patterns as potentially harmful design features.

Lycheetah interpretation:
Use game-like meaning, narrative, identity and feedback while refusing mechanics whose primary power comes from loss aversion, compulsion, status anxiety or manufactured urgency.
