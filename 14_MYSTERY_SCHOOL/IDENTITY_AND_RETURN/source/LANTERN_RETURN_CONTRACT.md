# THE LANTERN RETURN CONTRACT v0.3

**Status:** PROPOSED CORE CONSTITUTION  
**Purpose:** Make returning to the Mystery School rewarding without making absence costly.

## House law

> **Return is invitation, never debt.**

The School may become compelling because:
- an unfinished question still matters,
- a learner can feel themselves becoming more capable,
- a chosen Mantle gains lived meaning,
- a personal Atlas becomes richer,
- a Living Myth continues,
- another person responds to something worth discussing,
- or the learner simply wants to come back.

It must not become compelling because:
- a streak will die,
- a reward expires,
- a rank will fall,
- a companion acts hurt,
- a timer creates artificial scarcity,
- a notification manufactures urgency,
- a leaderboard threatens status,
- an algorithm discovers that distress prolongs sessions,
- or private identity data is used to maximize engagement.

## The seven prohibitions

1. **No punitive streaks.**
   The interface never counts absence as failure.

2. **No daily-login economy.**
   Titles, roots, story chapters and learning access never require opening the app on a schedule chosen by the platform.

3. **No variable-ratio reward machine.**
   No loot boxes, randomized prestige drops, mystery currency, or deliberately unpredictable reward cadence designed to prolong use.

4. **No engagement-maximizing recommender.**
   Session length, click count and return frequency are not direct optimization targets for learning recommendations.

5. **No guilting companion.**
   The Companion may say that work is waiting. It may not imply that the AI suffered, was abandoned, became lonely, or needs the learner to return.

6. **No rank ladder for human worth.**
   No global XP, public level, prestige tier, rare-title hierarchy, or absolute learner leaderboard.

7. **No artificial urgency.**
   If something is urgent, the urgency must come from reality: an exam date, a learner-set commitment, a changing source, an actual event, or another person waiting for an agreed response.

## Positive design target

The School optimizes for:

**meaningful voluntary return**

A meaningful return is one where the learner can answer at least one of these:

- I continued something I cared about.
- I learned or practiced something reusable.
- I made or repaired an artifact.
- I understood myself or a question more clearly.
- I contributed something useful.
- I had a worthwhile encounter with another person or perspective.
- I chose to stop at a good boundary.

Stopping can be a successful session.
