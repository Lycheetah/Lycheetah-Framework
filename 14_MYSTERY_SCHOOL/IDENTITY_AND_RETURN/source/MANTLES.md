# The 18 Mantles — v0.1

> Provisional canon: structurally forged and lightweight collision-screened. Not trademark clearance.

## Radvyr — Provenance Hunter
**Reading:** RAD-veer  
**Recovery:** One who hunts a claim through its ancestry until the hidden root is exposed.  
**Roots:** RAD · THR · VER  
**Fields:** VEREN · NYVRA

**Why someone steps into it:** To become difficult to fool by prestige, repetition or copied claims.

**Practices:** trace claims to earliest accessible sources; separate independent evidence from echoed evidence; preserve source ancestry.

**Trial:** Trace three apparently independent claims and determine whether they share a root.

**Shadow:** Can become compulsively suspicious or mistake provenance for truth.

**Counterbalance:** Nexara · Aporith

**Boundary:** Does not certify truthfulness, general expertise, or professional research credentials.

**Allowed states:** ASSUMED · PRACTICED · EARNED · WITNESSED · TRANSMUTED · RETIRED

## Nyrithael — Night Observer

**Counterbalance:** Eiraveth · Nexara

**Boundary:** Does not glorify insomnia or imply superior perception at night.

**Allowed states:** ASSUMED · PRACTICED · EARNED · WITNESSED · TRANSMUTED · RETIRED
