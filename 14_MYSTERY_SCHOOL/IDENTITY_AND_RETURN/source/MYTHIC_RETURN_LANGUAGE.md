# MYTHIC LANGUAGE FOR THE RETURN LOOP

The mythology should remember the constitution.

## The School remembers the question, not the absence.
No streak punishment.

## The Lantern may be left low.
A good session can end.

## Winter is part of the garden.
Rest does not erase progress.

## No Name Owns Its Bearer.
Identity remains editable and retireable.

## The Ninth Door Opens Outward.
Leaving is legitimate.

## A Road That Cannot Be Refused Is Not a Road.
Recommendations remain optional.

## Roots Are Not Coins.
Semantic roots cannot become a spendable engagement currency.

## The Lycheetah Does Not Chase You Home.
The system does not pursue a learner with guilt.

## Return When the Question Has Teeth Again.
Curiosity, not platform anxiety, is the desired pull.
