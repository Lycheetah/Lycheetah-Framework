# Semantic Root Lexicon — 72 Roots

These are **Lycheetah-authored semantic roots**. Similarity to words in natural languages is not a historical etymology claim.

## AELUM — Illumination
*luminous, open, revealing*

- **AEL** — illumination: bringing something into clearer view
- **ORA** — revelation: making a hidden structure legible
- **LUM** — steady light: sustained attention without glare
- **EIR** — gentle clarity: clarity that does not coerce
- **SERA** — calm radiance: patient visible presence
- **ALEN** — opening: making room for perception
- **IRAE** — spark: first emergence of a useful distinction
- **AUR** — orientation: turning toward what is treated as luminous

## NYVRA — Night / Hiddenness
*nocturnal, quiet, watchful*

- **NYR** — night: wakefulness in quiet or darkness
- **VAE** — veil: a boundary between visible and hidden
- **DUS** — dusk: transition into reduced certainty
- **SEL** — silence: attention made possible by reduced noise
- **MUR** — shadow: partial visibility rather than evil
- **THR** — tracking: following subtle traces
- **EVE** — afterlight: attention that begins after ordinary activity ends
- **NOI** — nocturnal presence: belonging in the hours others overlook

## VEREN — Evidence / Science
*precise, hard-edged, empirical*

- **VER** — tested claim: a proposition exposed to correction
- **RAD** — provenance root: where a claim, source or dependency begins
- **MET** — measurement: operationalized contact with a variable
- **BAS** — baseline: the rival ordinary explanation or comparator
- **REP** — replication: recovery of an effect under renewed test
- **CAU** — causal leverage: change produced through intervention
- **CAL** — calibration: alignment between instrument and what it claims to measure
- **INV** — invariant: what survives controlled variation

## QIRAE — Mystery / Unresolved
*liminal, spacious, unresolved*

- **QIR** — unexplained remainder: what survives after known mechanism is exhausted
- **APO** — open problem: a question preserved without forced closure
- **LIM** — threshold: edge between explanatory regimes
- **REM** — remainder: residue not yet accounted for
- **MIR** — mystery: unresolved depth without supernatural promotion
- **EID** — unknown form: pattern glimpsed but not yet securely classified
- **HAL** — horizon: limit of current observation or theory
- **ORAQ** — oracle-risk: a prediction that must be timestamped before outcome

## THAEN — Sacred / Devotion
*reverent, resonant, voluntary*

- **THAE** — sacred orientation: chosen relation to what one treats as sacred
- **DEV** — devotion: voluntary enduring commitment
- **VOW** — vow: self-authored promise
- **RIT** — ritual: repeated symbolic act with declared meaning
- **COV** — covenant: mutual or sacred promise with boundaries
- **PIL** — pilgrimage: movement undertaken for meaning
- **REV** — reverence: careful regard without automatic truth status
- **ALT** — altar-space: a deliberately set-apart place or practice

## NULEN — Non-devotion / Open Absence
*clear, spare, unforced*

- **NUL** — non-assumption: leaving a metaphysical slot unfilled
- **ABS** — absence: what is not present without inferring why
- **SKE** — skepticism: withholding assent pending sufficient reason
- **FRE** — freedom from imposed frame: permission not to adopt a worldview
- **QUI** — quiet null: choosing no declaration
- **UNS** — unsettled: not yet committed
- **ZER** — zero-point: starting without prestige or rank
- **OPEN** — open stance: remaining revisable without devotional commitment

## KORAE — Form / Body / Craft
*grounded, tactile, constructive*

- **KOR** — structure: arrangement that can bear load or meaning
- **FOR** — form: shape made usable
- **TAL** — craft: skill embodied through repeated making
- **MAK** — making: bringing an artifact into existence
- **BOD** — embodiment: knowledge expressed through body or material
- **STR** — strength: capacity to withstand relevant pressure
- **MEND** — repair: restoring function without erasing history
- **ASH** — surviving remainder: what remains useful after breakage or failure

## NOERA — Mind / Meaning
*interior, reflective, semantic*

- **NOE** — knowing: relation between mind and understanding
- **SEN** — sentience question: capacity for felt experience
- **MIRR** — reflection: mind observing its own representations
- **QUAL** — subjective quality: what experience is like from within
- **MEM** — memory: stored continuity that may or may not remain authoritative
- **SEM** — meaning: recoverable semantic content
- **INT** — interpretation: a model applied to an event or symbol
- **OTH** — other-mind boundary: evidence and uncertainty about another interior

## NEXEN — Relation / Community
*relational, connective, plural*

- **NEX** — dependency: a relation through which one thing changes another
- **KIN** — belonging: chosen or inherited relation among people
- **BRI** — bridge: translation or connection across difference
- **CONS** — consent: permission that can be scoped or withdrawn
- **PEER** — peer witness: attestation by another participant
- **THD** — thread: a connection explicitly carried across contexts
- **TRU** — trust: reliance under vulnerability
- **CARE** — care: attention to another's flourishing without ownership
