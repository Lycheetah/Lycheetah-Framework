# NOTIFICATION POLICY v0.3

## Default
Nonessential notifications should be sparse and user-controlled.

## Allowed categories
- learner-scheduled reminder,
- real source/evidence update,
- agreed peer response,
- real deadline,
- user-requested recurring practice,
- system/security issue.

## Disallowed copy
- "We miss you."
- "Your Mantle is fading."
- "Don't lose your progress."
- "Everyone else is advancing."
- fake countdowns,
- streak rescue,
- emotionally simulated abandonment by the Companion.

## Good examples
> A source attached to your saved question was updated.

> Mara replied to the argument you asked them to witness.

> You asked to revisit this experiment today.

## Quieting
Every notification class can be disabled.
Winter Mode disables all nonessential classes.
