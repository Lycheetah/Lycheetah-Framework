# Transmutation Protocol v0.1

## Eligibility
A learner may enter Transmutation when at least two meaningful roots/titles are legitimately present and the learner chooses to.

No minimum XP exists.

## Inputs
- active Mantles,
- retired Mantles if intentionally included,
- earned/witnessed roots,
- declared devotion only if explicitly included,
- chosen mystery/question,
- meaningful revisions or failures,
- optional personal phonetic preferences.

## Forbidden automatic behavior
The system must not:
- assign a personal name without acceptance,
- include a religious root the learner did not opt into,
- infer devotion from journal text,
- convert private trauma into a public epithet,
- imply professional credentialing,
- rank personal transmutations by rarity or prestige.

## Generation
1. Collect legitimate roots.
2. Build 6–18 candidate semantic splices.
3. Generate 2–6 phonetic forms per splice.
4. Apply phonetic gates.
5. Apply collision/cultural screen.
6. Show candidate + recovery side by side.
7. Allow edit/reject/combine.
8. Re-run recovery check after human edit.
9. Human explicitly accepts.
10. Mint `PERSONAL_CANON` receipt.

## Recovery test
A reviewer given the name and root ledger should be able to reconstruct the approved plain meaning without inventing a materially different claim.

## Core ceremony
- What did you once believe that you no longer believe?
- What survived that change?
- What mystery are you still unwilling to pretend you have solved?
- What do you refuse to surrender?
- What remains revisable?

**You inherit the language. You earn the roots. You forge the name.**
