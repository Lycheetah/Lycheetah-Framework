# Lycheetah Naming Forge v0.1

**Status:** PROPOSED SYSTEM / PROVISIONAL CANON  
**Purpose:** A recoverable, phonetic, mythic identity system for the Mystery School.  
**Core law:** **No mystery without recovery.**  
**Identity law:** **The game may reward the practice. It may never pretend the practice proves the whole person.**

## 1. What this system is

The Naming Forge turns real practices of inquiry into playable identities.

It does not issue social rank. It provides a language for:
- what a learner declares,
- what they are practicing,
- what they have demonstrably done,
- what others have witnessed,
- and what personal name they eventually forge from the history.

The permanent common identity is **STUDENT**. A learner may wear no other title at all.

## 2. Core pipeline

`Archetype -> Semantic roots -> Phonetic profile -> Candidate name -> Echoform gate -> LAMAGUE recovery -> Collision screen -> Human choice -> Receipt -> Living identity -> Transmutation`

### Echoform function
Sound should carry emotional posture before definition arrives.

### LAMAGUE function
Meaning must remain recoverable after compression.

Together:

`meaning <-> name`

A title is **loss-bounded identity compression**.

## 3. Nine Fields

The Nine Fields are not ranks and not curriculum Wings. They are thematic axes a person may combine.

- **AELUM — Illumination**: What can be made clearer without pretending clarity is truth?
- **NYVRA — Night / Hiddenness**: What becomes visible when ordinary noise falls away?
- **VEREN — Evidence / Science**: What survives measurement, rivalry and repetition?
- **QIRAE — Mystery / Unresolved**: What remains when explanation honestly stops?
- **THAEN — Sacred / Devotion**: What do I freely orient the sacred part of myself toward?
- **NULEN — Non-devotion / Open Absence**: What do I refuse to assume merely because a sacred answer is available?
- **KORAE — Form / Body / Craft**: What can be built, embodied, repaired or tested in matter?
- **NOERA — Mind / Meaning**: What counts as knowing, experience, memory or another mind?
- **NEXEN — Relation / Community**: What exists between minds that no mind owns alone?

## 4. Title states

- **DECLARED** — Self-authored identity or devotion. No external proof implied.
- **ASSUMED** — A mantle consciously chosen as a practice target.
- **PRACTICED** — Relevant practices have been performed; no claim of mastery.
- **EARNED** — A defined School gate has been passed.
- **WITNESSED** — A valid artifact, peer, system, or authorized reviewer supports the defined behavior.
- **TRANSMUTED** — A personal name forged from multiple legitimate roots/titles and accepted by the bearer.
- **RETIRED** — Historical identity preserved but no longer actively worn.

Important distinctions:
- Devotion is normally **DECLARED**, never School-certified.
- A Mantle may be **ASSUMED** before it is earned.
- An earned title records a bounded act, not a whole-person virtue.
- **RETIRED** preserves history without forcing continued identity.
- **TRANSMUTED** names are personal canon, not universal rank.

## 5. Witness grammar

- **SELF** — First-person declaration; authoritative only for self-description.
- **ARTIFACT** — A saved work product demonstrates the defined operation.
- **PEER** — Another participant attests to a specified behavior.
- **SYSTEM** — A deterministic or preregistered gate verified a bounded criterion.
- **REVIEWER** — A designated human reviewer checked the evidence.
- **NONE** — No witness claimed.

No witness type may silently imply more than its scope.

## 6. Phonetic law

### One claw. One breath. One echo.

A canonical name should:
1. have a memorable force point,
2. contain enough open sound to breathe,
3. leave a lingering sonic identity,
4. survive ordinary speech and low-light ceremonial speech,
5. remain usable in music/audio,
6. avoid becoming a parody of fantasy naming.

Every proposed name is tested:
- spoken normally,
- whispered,
- called across a room,
- sung over four notes,
- in the phrase `I am <NAME>`,
- in the phrase `<NAME> by Mantle`.

These are authored aesthetic constraints, not universal psycholinguistic laws.

## 7. Nine canon gates

Every proposed School-canon name must pass:

1. Semantic recoverability
2. Phonetic beauty
3. Speakability
4. Singability
5. Distinctiveness
6. Archetypal force
7. Cultural/collision screen
8. Cringe resistance
9. Transmutation compatibility

A failure on a critical gate rejects or returns the candidate for revision.

## 8. The 72 playable identities

The system now has **18 Great Mantles** and **54 specialized Forms**. Great Mantles remain broad identities; Forms are narrower practices beneath them. None is a higher rank.

See `MANTLES.md`, `forms.json`, `IDENTITY_HIERARCHY.md`, and `MANTLE_TREE_72.md`.

These are **provisional canon**: architecturally complete and lightweight collision-screened, but **not** trademark-cleared or cross-linguistically validated.

## 9. Transmutation

Transmutation is not XP.

A learner becomes eligible when several legitimate roots/titles form a meaningful tension or pattern and the learner chooses to enter.

The system asks:
- What title no longer fits?
- Which one cost the most to earn?
- What survived failure?
- What mystery grew larger?
- What do you refuse to surrender?
- What are you still willing to revise?

AI may propose candidate forms from legitimate roots. AI may **not** choose the final identity.

The bearer may reject all candidates, edit one, combine several, or leave the slot empty.

Acceptance creates **PERSONAL CANON**.

## 10. Devotion

Sacred orientation is structurally different from earned achievement.

A learner may declare:
- a named religion,
- devotion to God or a deity/tradition,
- agnosticism,
- atheism,
- non-devotion,
- a custom sacred description,
- or nothing.

The School does not verify sincerity or theological correctness.

Religious inquiry should distinguish:
- devotional,
- experiential,
- theological,
- historical,
- philosophical,
- empirical,
- mystery/open.

## 11. Public identity

The deep profile may store many facets. Public identity stays compact and user-selected.

Examples:

`Student · Qiralith · Godward`

`Student · Radvyr · Nyrithael`

No public title is mandatory.

## 12. The point of a Mantle

A Mantle should change practice.

- A **Radvyr** traces claims to roots.
- An **Oraeth** timestamps predictions before outcomes.
- A **Qiralith** records unexplained remainder without inflating it.
- An **Aporith** protects unresolved questions.
- An **Orivane** builds methods that others can actually use.

The game succeeds when the practice escapes the interface and appears in real thinking.

## 13. Sovereignty invariants

- No title grants human superiority.
- No founder or AI may define a learner's identity without consent.
- No title buys access to a “better mind.”
- No sacred identity is School-certified.
- Leaving or retiring a title is allowed.
- No streak is required to retain earned history.
- A title must expose what it does **not** imply.
- The Ninth Door opens outward: exit remains legitimate.
- The Lycheetah has no throne.

## 14. Versioning

Definitions are versioned.

A receipt binds:
- title definition version,
- gate version,
- witness type,
- evidence pointer,
- boundary text.

If a meaning changes materially, old receipts preserve the version under which they were earned.

Commons proposals never silently become School Canon.


## 16. Great Mantle -> Form rule

A Great Mantle supplies the broad character. A Form supplies a narrower practice. Forms may be changed without erasing earned history. Personal Transmutation may combine roots across multiple Great Mantles and Forms.
