# SUCCESS METRICS v0.3

The product team must not treat time-on-platform as the primary definition of success.

## Better product questions
- Did the learner complete the thing they intended?
- Can they reuse the method later?
- Did they produce an artifact they value?
- Can they explain why a title was earned?
- Did recommendations preserve choice?
- Did they successfully dismiss or ignore recommendations without penalty?
- Can they export and continue elsewhere?
- Can they return after absence without repair work caused by the platform?
- Do users voluntarily retire titles that no longer fit?
- Does the Companion widen the learner's world beyond the interface?

## Candidate metrics

### Meaningful Return Rate
Percentage of return sessions in which the learner marks at least one outcome as worthwhile.

Do not optimize this through pressure.

### Voluntary Completion
Learner ends a bounded session after completing or intentionally stopping a chosen task.

High voluntary stopping is not a failure.

### Capability Reuse
A method learned earlier is reused in a later different context.

### Exit Integrity
Export, dismissal, Winter Mode and account-exit paths function as described.

### Recommendation Rejection Health
Skipping recommendations does not reduce access or trigger escalating persuasion.

### Return After Absence
Measure whether people can recover context successfully after long gaps.
Do not measure absence itself as failure.

## Never use alone as success
- DAU
- session length
- notifications opened
- number of title unlocks
- content consumed
