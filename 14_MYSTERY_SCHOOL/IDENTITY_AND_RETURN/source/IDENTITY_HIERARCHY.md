# Identity Hierarchy v0.2

## Student
Permanent zero-point identity. Everyone remains Student.

## Great Mantle
A broad, playable archetype of inquiry. There are 18 in v0.2.

## Form
A specialization under a Great Mantle. There are three Forms per Mantle in v0.2, for 54 Forms.

A Form is not an upgrade. It answers: **how do you currently practice this Mantle?**

Examples:
- `Radvyr -> Radovael` means Provenance Hunter practiced as Source Diver.
- `Thaevara -> Rithaevyn` means Devotee practiced through deliberate ritual.
- `Qiralith -> Cauverith` means Mechanism Walker practiced through intervention testing.

## Epithet
A short earned phrase generated from a bounded act, e.g. `Keeper of the Broken Claim`.
Epithets may coexist with Mantles/Forms and need not use the constructed title tongue.

## Transmutation
A bearer-authored personal name forged from roots legitimately present across Mantles, Forms, declared devotion and lived history.

## Public identity
Recommended compact pattern:
`Student · <Form or Mantle> · <optional declared devotion/epithet>`

Example:
`Student · Vaenythra · Godward`

## Choice law
The system should offer depth without forcing identity work. A learner can remain simply `Student`.
