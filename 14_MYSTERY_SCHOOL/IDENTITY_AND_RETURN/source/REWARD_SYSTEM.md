# REWARD SYSTEM v0.3 — NON-PREDATORY PROGRESSION

## What counts as a reward

### 1. Access through understanding
A deeper instrument becomes usable because prerequisite concepts are now meaningful.

This is not time-gated.

### 2. Semantic roots
Roots are added to the learner's available naming vocabulary when their meaning has genuinely entered the learner's work.

Roots are not currency.
They cannot be spent.
They cannot be purchased.

### 3. Mantle states
ASSUMED -> PRACTICED -> EARNED -> WITNESSED

State changes require defined acts, not engagement volume.

### 4. Epithets
Short phrases tied to specific history:
- Keeper of the Broken Claim
- One Who Withheld the Score
- Witness of the Fork

An epithet must have a receipt.

### 5. Transmutation
A rare reflective event created by semantic convergence, not accumulated XP.

No rarity colors.
No legendary tier.
No loot probability.

### 6. Story continuity
The Living Myth changes because the learner changed.

Story is not withheld as payment for return frequency.

### 7. Capability evidence
The strongest reward:
> **I can now perform this operation without needing the School to do it for me.**

### 8. Contribution
A method or artifact becomes useful to another person.

Contribution quality is reviewed through evidence and use, not likes.

## Forbidden currencies

The core School should not use:
- global XP,
- gems,
- coins,
- energy,
- daily tokens,
- login chests,
- prestige points,
- purchasable progression,
- randomized title rarity.

Local scores may exist inside a bounded learning instrument where the number has a real interpretation.

Example:
A forecasting calibration score is allowed.
"Knowledge Power 8,240" is not.

## Surprise

Surprise is allowed when learner-triggered and non-coercive.

Example:
**Open a Wild Fragment**
- explicitly requested,
- no scarcity,
- no daily limit designed to form habit,
- no premium probability,
- contents do not determine prestige.

Serendipity is not a slot machine.
