# Mantle Tree v0.2

**18 Great Mantles + 54 Forms = 72 playable inquiry identities.**

Great Mantles are broad characters of inquiry. A Form is a specialization beneath a Great Mantle. A learner may wear a Great Mantle without choosing a Form, wear a Form publicly by itself, change Forms, or later Transmute roots across different Mantles into Personal Canon.

Forms are not higher ranks. They are narrower practices.

## Radvyr — Provenance Hunter
*One who hunts a claim through its ancestry until the hidden root is exposed.*

### Radovael — Source Diver
**Reading:** RAD-oh-vayl  
**Recovery:** One who descends through citation, retelling and prestige until the earliest accessible source is reached.  
**Roots:** RAD · VER · THR

**Trial:** Take one widely repeated claim and reconstruct its oldest accessible evidential chain.

**Shadow:** Confusing the oldest source with the best source.

### Nexathyr — Dependency Tracker
**Reading:** NEK-sah-theer  
**Recovery:** One who follows what a claim depends upon and notices when one broken root weakens many branches.  
**Roots:** NEX · RAD · INV

**Trial:** Build a dependency map and identify the smallest upstream change that forces downstream review.

**Shadow:** Treating every relation as causal.

### Veradhael — Lineage Keeper
**Reading:** VEH-rah-dhail  
**Recovery:** One who preserves the lineage of evidence so later minds can see how a conclusion became authoritative.  
**Roots:** VER · RAD · MEM

**Trial:** Reconstruct a claim history including support, revisions and superseded versions.

**Shadow:** Archival prestige: mistaking a well-documented claim for a true one.
