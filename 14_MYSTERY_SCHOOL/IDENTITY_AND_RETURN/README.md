**Mac × Vaelora** · supplied AI co-author attribution: OpenAI GPT-5.6 Sol.  
**Current source:** Identity Return v0.3 · provisional design, not a deployed system.

STUDENT is the common identity. Eighteen Great Mantles and fifty-four Forms name
practices; none ranks human worth. Identity can be retired, and leaving does not erase
work. A title's evidence applies only to its bounded act.

Those gates check source data and explicit design commitments. They do not establish
healthy real-world engagement, globally unique names, cultural permission, production
privacy or learner capability. Existing provisional canon/collision labels are inherited
source statuses, not new certification. Two historical source logs are summarized here;
their full originals and hashes are preserved in the private intake receipt.

Payment cannot buy spiritual rank, a title's evidence or easier worthiness. Return is
voluntary; skipping loses nothing. No private words enter the Commons automatically.
