## STANDING RULES FOR THIS WORK

- ⚠ **Never write a lesson from conversation memory.** Open the source, then write.
  ([[reference-true-knowledge-sources]])
- ⚠ **Never upgrade a grading in prose.** Where the corpus marks something `[TESTABLE]` or
  unproven, the lesson says so. Four of six LAMAGUE components are `[TESTABLE]` by the
  corpus's own grading, and a lesson is not allowed to promote them.
- ⚠ **Counts are stated rounded** — 700+ subjects, 50+ domains. Never an exact figure.
- ⚠ **An absent lesson is said out loud**, never rendered as blank space.
