# CHAPTER FIVE CONTINUITY RECEIPT
## The Road That Counted the Living

**Date:** 2026-08-02 NZST  
**Human author, world origin and final canon authority:** Mackenzie Conor James Clark  
**Narrative forge:** Mackenzie Conor James Clark × Caelorynth  
**Status:** EDITORIAL RECEIPT · not a declaration of final canon

## Judgment

The existing Prologue and Chapters One–Four remain causally coherent. They are still too
compressed for the eventual finished novel, but they do not require destructive revision
before this listening proof. Their sacred scenes remain protected.

Chapter Five treats the previous ending as the hinge into the world portrayal established
on 2026-08-02:

- there is one material reality joined by folded relational routes, not a disposable
  fantasy dimension behind the School;
- the Mystery School is one fallible surviving institution, not the centre or sole owner
  of ancient truth;
- the Testament of Three Shadows survives as creature relation, road custom, work song,
  disputed memory and political consequence rather than an inserted lecture;
- the City of Broken Names protects ordinary life in real ways, making Concordance order
  morally tempting even as it converts living exceptions into errors;
- love and fellowship enlarge available choices without commanding them;
- the rescue distinguishes a voluntary or protective boundary from a prison;
- Tarin's cost is named before he chooses it, refusal remains possible, and no character
  treats suffering as proof of loyalty;
- the Waybearer can loosen Mara's Bind and choose a route she does not control.

## Protected continuity carried forward

- Mara's brass ruler and archival attention;
- the damaged Lycheetah's ordinary black-cat form and single antler;
- Tarin's repair craft and portable argument;
- Ember's observation / inference / desire method;
- Veyra's obligation to repair the School and preserve a safe return;
- the seven missing School members;
- Celyn's red-black coat, broken crown and luminous ninth fragment;
- the black-glass road, faceless statues and nine towers.

## Reversible new commitments to test by listening

- Celyn and Tarin knew one another in the city;
- Tarin's mother remained there and refused the official report of his death;
- six missing School members are held for correction while one is recorded as an
  authorised ninth-tower custodian;
- one of the seven helped open the School route from inside;
- the red-road labour song carries an unfinished-choir inheritance;
- a living three-shadowed creature still walks the road and makes choices independent of
  what any tradition claims it symbolizes.

These are strong continuation candidates, not irreversible canon. The future opening
rewrite should expand ordinary life, relationships and causal recovery around the
existing sacred scenes. It should not rewrite tonight's listening proof merely to make
the earlier compression invisible.
