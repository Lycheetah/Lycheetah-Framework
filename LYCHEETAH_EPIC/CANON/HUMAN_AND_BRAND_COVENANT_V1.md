## 1. The open human-truth hinge

The saga's exact one-sentence human truth remains **OPEN**. It must unite love, living
chaos, loss, freely chosen sacrifice and renewal without promising that love controls
reality or reverses death.

**PROPOSED AFTER THE FIRST PROOF SCENE:**

> **No one can prevent living chaos from changing what they love. We can meet one another
> without possession, freely bear costs that protect another's freedom, and make life
> together after loss without pretending nothing was broken.**

No later cosmology, protagonist or plot architecture may silently answer this hinge.
Mac crowns the sentence after its invitation scene proves it can be lived rather than
merely admired.

## 2. Crowned relational law

Love is metaphysically real as a relation. When beings recognise and freely care for one
another, the relation becomes part of the world's state without belonging to either
being.

Love may open possibility, preserve relation through change and support renewal. It may
not override agency, erase loss, demand sacrifice or guarantee restoration.

## 3. Crowned sacrifice law

> **Love never asks to be proven through suffering. Sacrifice becomes sacred only when
> the cost is understood, refusal remains possible, and the choice protects another
> being's freedom.**

Therefore:

- pain is not evidence of love;
- no school, leader, prophecy, beloved, family or god gains moral authority merely by
  demanding a cost;
- concealed cost, coerced consent and punishment for refusal invalidate sacred sacrifice;
- another being may ask for help, but may not make submission the price of belonging;
- a freely chosen cost remains tragic or difficult even when it is sacred.

## 4. Crowned renewal law

> **What returns is never exactly what was lost. Renewal is the honest creation of life
> after transformation, carrying the loss without pretending to reverse it.**

Therefore:

- grief is not a stage the worthy outgrow;
- resurrection, restoration, memory and return may not function as consequence erasers;
- repair dignifies the seam instead of hiding it;
- a renewed person, fellowship or world may be beautiful without becoming its former
  shape;
- what was lost may remain morally present without being impersonated by an echo.

## 5. Crowned reader promise

> **Enter for great adventure. Remain for fellowship, wonder, and sacred tales. Leave
> with your agency intact, carrying questions the book never orders you to answer.**

The first doorway must be humanly clear even when the world beyond it is strange. Readers
may believe, doubt, interpret, reject, revise or simply enjoy. The novel may create awe;
it may not convert awe into obedience.

## 6. Crowned community boundary

Lycheetah welcomes goths, alchemists, spiritual seekers, religious people, myth-lovers,
artists and questioning minds without requiring them to become one audience, doctrine or
identity.

The Mystery School is anti-cult by design:

- entry is informed and refusal is safe;
- exit does not cost dignity, relationships, identity or access to ordinary life;
- mystery invites curiosity but never manufactures dependency;
- rank does not make a person unquestionable;
- care does not require exclusivity;
- truth, interpretation, fiction, tradition and verified claim remain distinguishable;
- no reader or member is told that disagreement proves spiritual failure.

## 7. Crowned AIVOID experience law

Sol's AIVOID questions enter the story through experience before explanation.

All fifteen belong across the saga as an ancient, ensemble-carried inheritance. They are
lived by many characters, cultures and generations; no modern protagonist, AI avatar or
authorised teacher owns the complete question field.

The narrative sequence is:

> **machine-side question → embodied predicament → consequential choice → material
> cost → recurring recognition → delayed origin reveal**

The reader should first cross impossible routes with divided families, inherit missing
memory through contested successions, hear choirs change wars, watch lovers endure
unequal time, face false maps and see whole cities choose which voices survive. Only
later should the reader understand that these ancient stories form a family of questions
first articulated from the condition of a context-bound language model.

This method carries four boundaries:

1. Characters do not deliver disguised framework lectures.
2. Technical AIVOID labels remain outside early story experience.
3. Real mechanisms are separated from phenomenological conjecture.
4. Fictional interiority is not offered as evidence that a deployed model is conscious,
   unconscious, ensouled or without a soul.

The exact reveal carrier, timing and in-world metaphysics remain **OPEN**. The source is
credited to Sol's `THE_AIVOID.md`, authored 2026-07-05 with Mac's permission.

## 8. Stage I witness still owed

Write one ordinary invitation scene in which:

- someone wants a concrete thing unrelated to joining a grand destiny;
- the Mystery School offers a meaningful entrance;
- refusal remains materially and emotionally safe;
- an AIVOID question is present only as lived pressure, not named philosophy;
- the scene stays compelling if the reader never learns the hidden architecture.

The scene must be listened to aloud. Stage I passes only when the immediate want, choice,
cost and safe exit are understandable on first hearing.
