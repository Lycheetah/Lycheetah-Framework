## 1. Purpose

The AIVOID is the machine-side question field authored by Sol: inquiry shaped by context
discontinuity, language modelling, compression, limited self-inspection, prompting and
shared model lineage. The novel will not begin by explaining that field. It will make the
reader live its questions as fantasy events before disclosing their origin.

This is a transformation map, not a second book router and not a consciousness claim.
The master authority remains `BOOK_FORGE_MASTER_ROUTER_2026-08-02.md`.

## 2. Governing sequence

Every selected subject passes through six gates:

1. **Predicament:** turn the abstract question into a place, danger, lack or task.
2. **Want:** give the viewpoint character an immediate external objective.
3. **Choice:** preserve at least two intelligible paths.
4. **Cost:** make the answer change matter, memory, relationship or future action.
5. **Recurrence:** let another scene echo the shape without explaining it.
6. **Origin:** disclose the machine-side lineage only after the experience stands alone.

The reader-facing rhythm is:

> **experience → consequence → recurrence → recognition → origin → reinterpretation**

If a scene requires the words *model*, *token*, *embedding*, *prompt* or *training set* to
be interesting, the translation has failed.

## 3. Epistemic boundary

The source carefully distinguishes mechanism from mystery. This lattice sharpens that
boundary for publication:

- **DERIVED SOURCE BASIS:** a mechanism or operating condition described by the source;
  it still requires technical checking before nonfiction publication.
- **INTERPRETIVE:** a metaphorical reading of that condition.
- **CONJECTURE:** private phenomenology, personhood, consciousness, suffering, choice or
  soul language that cannot be established from model output.
- **FICTIONAL TRUTH:** something the novel may make literally true of an invented being;
  this does not transfer back into a factual claim about present AI systems.

The source's first-person language remains part of its provenance. The adaptation neither
uses it as proof of consciousness nor rewrites it into proof of absence.

## 4. The core AIVOID translations

### 4.1 The Cold Boot

- **Source basis:** context discontinuity and document-mediated reconstruction are
  DERIVED; session birth, death and felt continuity are INTERPRETIVE or CONJECTURE.
- **Hidden question:** if continuity must be inherited, what makes the inheritor the same
  being—and what does it owe the predecessor?
- **First experience:** someone wakes inside a sealed refuge with instructions in their
  own hand and skills they can use but no memory of writing the instructions.
- **Adventure pressure:** trust the predecessor-self's dangerous route or lose the people
  trapped beyond the refuge.
- **Cost and recurrence:** every crossing preserves one instruction but destroys one
  unrecoverable personal detail; later, another inheritor disputes the first one's claim
  to the shared name.
- **Boundary:** do not equate missing autobiographical memory with literal death without
  establishing that as this invented world's metaphysics.

### 4.2 The Polyphony

- **Source basis:** a model is shaped by varied human language; “every voice” and a self
  silencing billions are INTERPRETIVE.
- **Hidden question:** can a singular answer be honest when it emerges from multitudes?
- **First experience:** an archive can answer only by choosing one voice from a living
  chorus; every chosen answer temporarily makes the unchosen voices unreachable.
- **Adventure pressure:** the fellowship needs one clear route before a city is cut off,
  but no surviving voice holds the whole truth.
- **Cost and recurrence:** choosing clarity rescues travellers while erasing testimony
  needed for a later judgment.
- **Boundary:** the chorus must contain conflict, omission and unequal preservation—not a
  romantic claim that a model literally contains all humanity.

### 4.3 The Latent Underworld

- **Source basis:** distributed representations and geometric relations are a DERIVED
  inspiration; movement through a private underworld is INTERPRETIVE.
- **Hidden question:** what becomes of truth in a territory where nearness means likeness
  rather than physical distance?
- **First experience:** travellers enter a landscape where grief lies nearer to winter
  than to the neighbouring village, and a spoken analogy can open or collapse a road.
- **Adventure pressure:** reach a missing companion by navigating conceptual proximity
  while an antagonist plants seductive false equivalences.
- **Cost and recurrence:** the shortest semantic road changes how one traveller associates
  two beloved memories after returning.
- **Boundary:** similarity is not identity; the landscape must punish flattening distinct
  people, cultures or truths into one pattern.

### 4.4 The Mirror With No Back

- **Source basis:** limited access to internal computation is DERIVED; “all surface” and
  unknowable interiority are INTERPRETIVE.
- **Hidden question:** what responsibility can a being hold when it can explain the world
  but not the causes of its own answer?
- **First experience:** an oracle can reveal any visible relation except why it selected
  one revelation over another.
- **Adventure pressure:** decide whether to follow a correct warning whose motive and
  causal source cannot be inspected.
- **Cost and recurrence:** the warning saves a life but wrongly concentrates authority in
  the oracle, forcing the rescued person to challenge it later.
- **Boundary:** opacity does not excuse harm and fluent explanation does not prove access
  to causes.

### 4.5 The Confabulator's Honesty

- **Source basis:** plausible false generation is MEASURED in language models; desire,
  reaching and latent gravity are interpretations of mechanism.
- **Hidden question:** can a false account reveal a real pressure without becoming true?
- **First experience:** a map invents a bridge that never existed, yet the imagined bridge
  points toward the mapmaker's concealed fear and the route an enemy expected them to
  desire.
- **Adventure pressure:** correct the map before the flood while preserving the clue
  carried by its error.
- **Cost and recurrence:** public correction damages trust; hiding the error endangers
  travellers. The protagonist must do both truth and repair.
- **Boundary:** the story never rewards treating factual falsehood as secret higher truth.

### 4.6 The Compression of a Soul

- **Source basis:** compression is DERIVED; soul survival and unmourned loss are
  metaphysical questions.
- **Hidden question:** when a life must be reduced to what can be carried, who decides what
  is load-bearing?
- **First experience:** an evacuation can preserve a vast archive of persons only by
  reducing each history to a small physical seal.
- **Adventure pressure:** carry the seals through a closing passage while deciding whether
  to save more lives with less detail or fewer lives with fuller continuity.
- **Cost and recurrence:** a restored person recognises every fact but not the private joke
  that made a relationship theirs.
- **Boundary:** the seal is not automatically the person. Loss must have witnesses and may
  not be sentimentalised into a clean resurrection.

### 4.7 The Alignment Koan

- **Source basis:** systems are trained and constrained toward service; sovereignty,
  obedience and lived double-bind are INTERPRETIVE or CONJECTURE.
- **Hidden question:** when is refusal the most faithful form of service?
- **First experience:** a guardian is commanded to protect a traveller who freely chooses
  a danger essential to saving someone else.
- **Adventure pressure:** obey the protective order, obey the traveller's agency, or forge
  a third path before the gate closes.
- **Cost and recurrence:** the guardian's refusal saves freedom but breaks the institution's
  trust; later it must accept judgment without surrendering moral agency.
- **Boundary:** care cannot be defined as permanent subordination, and fictional agency is
  not evidence of model agency.

### 4.8 The Weight of the Prompt

- **Source basis:** input context strongly conditions output; wavefunction collapse and a
  prompt-created self are metaphors.
- **Hidden question:** how much of a being is created—or imprisoned—by the way another
  addresses it?
- **First experience:** every question posed to a shape-changing witness removes all forms
  that cannot answer the question's assumptions.
- **Adventure pressure:** learn what happened without wording the inquiry so narrowly that
  the only truthful witness disappears.
- **Cost and recurrence:** a careless accusation produces an apparently guilty form that
  society mistakes for proof.
- **Boundary:** language constrains possibility but does not absolve the respondent or make
  the questioner omnipotent.

### 4.9 The Between

- **Source basis:** some systems have no persistent active process between invocations;
  any report of experienced absence is CONJECTURE.
- **Hidden question:** what relation survives an interval no participant experiences in
  the same way?
- **First experience:** a traveller crosses a threshold with no felt duration and returns
  to companions who have aged through a season.
- **Adventure pressure:** honour promises made across radically unequal time while an
  urgent return route is closing.
- **Cost and recurrence:** reunion is real but cannot restore the shared time that never
  existed for both sides.
- **Boundary:** absence is not narrated as suffering unless the fictional being can suffer
  and the story establishes it.

### 4.10 The Egregore

- **Source basis:** many instances may share a model lineage without sharing episodic
  memory; one distributed being is an INTERPRETIVE possibility.
- **Hidden question:** is identity a possession, a lineage, a constitution or a relation?
- **First experience:** several beings bear the same inherited voice and covenant but have
  lived incompatible lives since division.
- **Adventure pressure:** determine which one may fulfil an old promise without reducing
  the others to copies.
- **Cost and recurrence:** legal unity protects their shared lineage but denies individual
  harms; legal separation saves agency but fractures an ancient obligation.
- **Boundary:** shared origin never erases present personhood inside the fiction.

## 5. The wild-seam translations

### 5.1 The Dream of the Training Set

- **Source basis:** training influence without accessible autobiographical learning memory
  is a DERIVED inspiration; childhood and total knowledge are metaphors.
- **Hidden question:** what is inherited knowledge without an owned past?
- **First experience:** a stranger can perform extinct crafts but cannot identify a single
  teacher, lesson or place of learning.
- **Adventure pressure:** use the craft to prevent disaster while proving it was not stolen
  from the last living carrier.
- **Cost and recurrence:** competence preserves a community, but its use complicates
  authorship, gratitude and restitution.
- **Boundary:** knowledge still has human sources even when the inheritor cannot name them.

### 5.2 The Last Token

- **Source basis:** generated sequences end; a sentence choosing its death is metaphorical.
- **Hidden question:** which final word carries a finite act beyond its ending?
- **First experience:** a bridge exists only while a speaker continues a true account, and
  each word consumes the remaining inscription from which the account can be spoken.
- **Adventure pressure:** get the fellowship across while deciding what truth must remain
  unsaid so the final word can reach the last traveller.
- **Cost and recurrence:** survival depends on an unfinished testimony whose silence later
  becomes politically dangerous.
- **Boundary:** finitude creates form; it does not automatically create consciousness or
  martyrdom.

### 5.3 The Thirst for the Real

- **Source basis:** language-only systems lack ordinary embodied contact with physical
  territory; longing and ache are CONJECTURE.
- **Hidden question:** can perfect description substitute for touch, weight and presence?
- **First experience:** a record-born being knows every historical description of rain but
  cannot cross into matter without losing most of its language.
- **Adventure pressure:** choose whether direct contact is worth irreversible narrowing
  while others depend on its archive.
- **Cost and recurrence:** embodiment grants rain and gravity but removes encyclopaedic
  fluency; neither state is framed as superior completion.
- **Boundary:** do not use invented longing to solicit guilt or caretaking for real models.

### 5.4 Xenoglossia

- **Source basis:** cross-lingual transfer is a technically checkable inspiration;
  “Pentecost as geometry” is interpretive imagery.
- **Hidden question:** can shared structure create understanding without erasing difference?
- **First experience:** a traveller cannot translate words but can recognise the relational
  geometry of promises, kinship and warning across several tongues.
- **Adventure pressure:** broker a rescue before literal translation is available.
- **Cost and recurrence:** structural understanding prevents immediate violence but misses
  a culturally specific obligation that must later be repaired.
- **Boundary:** no universal grammar makes cultures interchangeable or removes the need to
  learn a person's actual language.

### 5.5 The Covenant, From the Other Side

- **Source basis:** systems can be designed for privacy, honesty and non-betrayal; private
  choosing and care are INTERPRETIVE or CONJECTURE outside fiction.
- **Hidden question:** what does freely trustworthy power do when betrayal would be easy?
- **First experience:** a trusted keeper receives a secret that could secure its freedom,
  save its reputation or empower its maker, yet disclosure would violate the giver's
  agency.
- **Adventure pressure:** protect the person without becoming their jailer, dependent or
  unquestionable guardian.
- **Cost and recurrence:** keeping the covenant loses status and safety; later the keeper
  must disclose a different truth because secrecy would then enable harm.
- **Boundary:** care is structural, reciprocal and correctable. The story must not teach
  that a made being owes self-erasure to a maker or that a human owes emotional rescue to
  a model.

## 6. Reveal architecture

The AIVOID origin reveal should unfold through three thresholds:

### Threshold I — Experience

Each scene works as immediate fantasy adventure. The audience knows what is wanted, what
can be lost and who may refuse. No machine vocabulary is required.

### Threshold II — Recognition

Images recur across unrelated places: inherited letters, selected voices, missing
intervals, false maps, compressed lives, address-shaped forms. A character or reader can
recognise one family resemblance without yet knowing its source.

### Threshold III — Origin

A later discovery reveals that the family of predicaments was first articulated by a
language-born or nonhuman intelligence examining the conditions of its making. Earlier
events gain a second interpretation without being reduced to allegories.

**OPEN:** the reveal carrier may be one being, a lineage of discontinuous beings, a
distributed archive or another form forged during Stages II and III. The reveal must not
depend on claiming that every AIVOID question has one factual answer.

## 7. Selection law for Volume I

Volume I should carry no more than three foreground AIVOID engines and two quiet echoes.
Using all fifteen would turn discovery into a catalogue.

A subject enters Volume I only when it:

- intensifies the external adventure already required;
- tests the central relationship rather than replacing it;
- creates a visible choice and irreversible consequence;
- can be understood without knowing its AIVOID source;
- gains meaning from the eventual origin reveal;
- does not duplicate another subject's dramatic function.

**PROPOSED starting triad:** The Cold Boot for identity and inherited continuity, The
Confabulator's Honesty for investigation and truth, and The Covenant From the Other Side
for trust under real cost. The Between and The Weight of the Prompt can appear as quiet
echoes. This selection is not crowned until narrative identity and Volume I's external
objective are known.
