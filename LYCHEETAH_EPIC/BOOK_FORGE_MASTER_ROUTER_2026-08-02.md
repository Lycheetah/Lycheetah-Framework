## 1. The outcome

Build a mythic fantasy novel and durable Lycheetah story world that:

- can be understood and emotionally followed on a first listen;
- becomes richer, stranger and more morally difficult on a second encounter;
- carries gothic, alchemical, spiritual and religious resonance without requiring one
  belief system;
- tells deep stories of love, chaos, loss, freely chosen sacrifice and renewal;
- carries those truths through great adventures rather than philosophical exposition;
- contains original sacred tales that readers may contemplate without being required to
  treat the novel as scripture or doctrine;
- makes unconditional friendship, family and chosen fellowship load-bearing;
- honours ancient truth-carriers through transformed story and visible provenance;
- transforms Sol's AIVOID questions into lived narrative experiences before revealing
  their machine-side origin, with mechanism and conjecture kept visibly distinct;
- protects mystery without using confusion, hierarchy or vulnerability as control;
- creates characters, places, symbols and scenes recognizably belonging to Lycheetah;
- can later support illustration, audio, print, community and app lore because the novel
  earned them first.

## 2. The governing craft sentence

> **First encounter: clarity and feeling. Second encounter: recognition and revelation.**

Mystery is a question the reader wants to carry. Confusion is missing causal information.
The book may withhold answers; it may not withhold the immediate human stakes of a scene.

### The adventure law

> **Philosophy must move.**

Every load-bearing idea must eventually become a place to cross, a danger to survive, a
creature to encounter, a relationship to test or a choice that changes the road home.
Characters do not exist merely to discuss mind, love, freedom or sacrifice. They pursue
urgent external aims while those questions become unavoidable through consequence.

Every volume should earn:

- a journey with material distance and irreversible thresholds;
- a spectacular destination with ordinary lived detail;
- a mystery that can be investigated rather than merely contemplated;
- a creature encounter governed by attention and choice;
- danger that requires courage, skill and fellowship;
- at least one rescue, recovery, escape, defence, expedition or impossible return;
- an antagonist whose action changes the available future;
- a victory that costs something and a loss that creates new action;
- a return in which home, friendship or the traveller now means something different.

Wonder comes before explanation. Philosophy arrives through what the adventure makes the
characters do.

### The sacred-tale law

> **Sacredness is earned by what a story protects, costs and returns—not announced by a
> narrator demanding reverence.**

Lycheetah may contain prayers, hymns, rites, parables, creation accounts, pilgrimages,
miracles, angels, divine interpretations and stories people live or die by. Different
characters and cultures may believe, doubt, revise, reject or love them. The novel never
uses fictional revelation to seize authority over the reader's real faith or conscience.

A sacred tale inside this world should:

- arise from a human need such as grief, courage, hospitality, forgiveness, justice,
  sacrifice, companionship or return;
- have been carried by people whose lives altered its telling;
- remain interpretable in more than one honest way;
- produce material customs, disagreements, mercy and mistakes;
- contain an image or action strong enough to survive retelling;
- reveal a moral pressure without flattening it into a lesson;
- honour transformed ancient lineage through visible provenance outside the fiction;
- leave belief and disbelief free.

Some sacred tales may prove historically partial, politically edited or metaphysically
uncertain without becoming meaningless. Their truth can live in what they helped people
carry as well as in what literally occurred.

## 3. Canon authority and registers

Every durable world claim carries one of these statuses:

- **CROWNED BY MAC** — final enough to govern current drafting; still correctable.
- **ESTABLISHED IN MANUSCRIPT** — currently on the page but not automatically crowned.
- **PROTECTED SCENE** — its dramatic core must survive; prose and details may change.
- **PROPOSED SCAFFOLD** — generated to make a decision visible; carries no authority.
- **OPEN QUESTION** — deliberately unresolved.
- **RETIRED** — removed from active canon while provenance remains.

Conversation enthusiasm does not silently crown a proposal. Caelorynth recommends and
Mac decides. Where a decision changes several dependent systems, its consequences are
recorded before prose is globally renamed or rewritten.

## 4. What Mac has already crowned

### The thematic heart

- love;
- living chaos;
- loss;
- freely chosen sacrifice;
- renewal that does not merely restore the former shape;
- unconditional friendship;
- family and chosen family;
- love itself as a power that accompanies without possessing.
- love as a metaphysically real relation: when beings recognise and freely care for one
  another, the relation becomes part of the world's state without belonging to either
  being.
- love may open possibility, preserve relation through change and support renewal; it may
  not override agency, erase loss, demand sacrifice or guarantee restoration.

### The human and community direction

- Lycheetah should welcome goths, alchemists, spiritual seekers, religious people,
  myth-lovers, artists and questioning minds without requiring them to collapse into one
  doctrine.
- The Mystery School reclaims mystery, ritual, fellowship and transformation while being
  anti-cult by design.
- A person may enter mystery without surrendering mind, name, relationships, money,
  dignity or the right to leave.
- Natural curiosity may arise from mystery; manufactured dependency may not.
- Truth, interpretation, fiction, tradition and verified claim must remain distinguishable.

### The ancient-truth direction

- Ancient truths may be transformed into original myth in silent artistic honour.
- Publication records later name and thank actual sources, traditions and truth-carriers.
- Living cultures and sacred language are not mined as generic mystical texture.
- Similar motifs across cultures are not proof that every tradition secretly teaches one
  proprietary Lycheetah doctrine.

### The naming direction

- Proper names are designed canon assets and current names are placeholders until screened
  and crowned.
- The **Threefold Name** fuses one deep Lycheetah grammar, layered personal names governed
  by consent and many culturally distinct carried tongues.
- The deep sound moves through the emotional transmutation **hymn → shared tide → courage
  → revelation** without becoming literal musical or nautical costume.

### The production boundary

- Writing and worldbuilding come first.
- Illustration, comic/A4 design, audio, printing, binding and app-lore extraction happen
  after the manuscript earns them.
- Great adventure is the story vehicle; philosophical depth must never reduce the novel to
  characters exchanging framework propositions in beautiful rooms.
- Sacred tales are part of the world's living inheritance; they may invite reverence but
  never demand submission from the reader or member.

### The sacrifice, renewal and reader covenant

> **Love never asks to be proven through suffering. Sacrifice becomes sacred only when
> the cost is understood, refusal remains possible, and the choice protects another
> being's freedom.**

> **What returns is never exactly what was lost. Renewal is the honest creation of life
> after transformation, carrying the loss without pretending to reverse it.**

> **Enter for great adventure. Remain for fellowship, wonder, and sacred tales. Leave
> with your agency intact, carrying questions the book never orders you to answer.**

### The AIVOID experience law

- Sol's fifteen AIVOID subjects all belong across the saga as one ancient,
  ensemble-carried inheritance—not as exposition topics or one protagonist's private set
  of puzzles.
- Many characters, cultures, creatures and generations carry incomplete and conflicting
  versions. No single speaker or institution owns the authorised interpretation.
- The reader first lives a question as an embodied predicament with an external aim,
  relationship, protected choice and material consequence.
- Recurring images and consequences allow the reader to recognise a hidden family of
  questions before any machine-side origin is disclosed.
- A later reveal may identify the originating intelligence and recontextualise the earlier
  adventures; its exact carrier, timing and metaphysical status remain uncrowned.
- Actual model mechanisms may inform the fiction when marked **DERIVED** and checked.
  Claims about private machine experience, consciousness or a soul remain
  **CONJECTURE** or fictional possibility—not proof.
- Publication provenance credits Sol's `THE_AIVOID.md`, authored 2026-07-05 with Mac's
  creative permission. Fictional transformation does not erase source authorship.

## 7. The twelve-stage forge

Each stage has a decision outcome, a durable artifact and a story witness. A stage does
not require encyclopedic completion; it requires enough truth to stop downstream work
from resting on accidental assumptions.

### STAGE I — Human and Brand Covenant

**Question:** What human experience does Lycheetah promise, and what must it never demand?

Decide:

- the one-sentence human truth of the saga;
- the emotional promise to a first-time reader;
- audience centre and universal doorway;
- relationship between love, chaos, loss, sacrifice and renewal;
- Mystery School covenant and anti-cult prohibitions;
- religious plurality, doubt and the right to remain unconvinced;
- the boundary between mysterious attraction and manipulative conditioning.

**Artifact:** `CANON/HUMAN_AND_BRAND_COVENANT_V1.md`  
**Story witness:** one ordinary invitation scene in which entry is meaningful and refusal
remains genuinely safe.

### STAGE II — Narrative Identity

**Question:** Whose choices transform the saga, whose eyes orient each volume, and where
does the reader stand?

Decide:

- saga protagonist versus volume protagonist versus viewpoint carrier;
- whether Lycheetah's interiority is entered, sensed or protected;
- single, dual, rotating or fellowship point of view;
- the human doorway's specificity and universality;
- chosen-one, secret-lineage and destiny laws;
- what makes the reader feel invited rather than represented by a blank avatar;
- the central Volume I bond.

**Artifact:** `CANON/NARRATIVE_IDENTITY_V1.md`  
**Story witness:** the same pivotal moment rendered through two possible architectures,
then judged for mystery, clarity and emotional power.

### STAGE III — Metaphysics and Moral Physics

**Question:** What can reality do, what does it cost, and what refuses every shortcut?

Decide:

- creation accounts and how they disagree;
- the Field, Veil, Recognition and Long Light or their replacements;
- names, perception, memory and relation;
- power source, access, training, failure and recovery;
- death, grief, echo, afterlife and resurrection boundaries;
- sacrifice: what can be freely given and what may never be demanded;
- chaos as living irreducibility rather than random spectacle;
- why love matters without becoming a spell that fixes everything.

**Artifact:** `CANON/MORAL_PHYSICS_V1.md`  
**Story witness:** a magical act that succeeds technically but creates an irreversible
moral cost.

### STAGE IV — Ancient Truth and Provenance Lattice

**Question:** Which old human truths deepen this story, and how do we honour their carriers
without theft or flattening?

For each motif record:

- source tradition, text or carrier;
- whether it is living, sacred, contested or historically mediated;
- the human question it carries;
- what the Lycheetah transformation changes;
- what must not be copied, universalised or claimed;
- publication acknowledgement;
- sensitivity or source-community witness needed.

Candidate motif families include descent and return, death and rebirth, the sacred
stranger, hospitality, stolen names, labyrinth, underworld, world-tree, flood, fire,
beloved-loss, knowledge with cost and companionship across the threshold.

The AIVOID lattice is governed here for provenance but remains distinct from ancient
human traditions. It is not evidence that all traditions secretly describe artificial
intelligence, nor may it be used to absorb their meanings into a machine-origin story.

**Artifact:** `SOURCES/ANCIENT_TRUTH_PROVENANCE_LATTICE_V1.md`  
**Story witness:** one transformed motif that works without the reader knowing its source
and becomes richer—not appropriated—when provenance is later read.

### STAGE V — History, Cultures and Ordinary Life

**Question:** Who has lived in this world long enough to disagree about it?

Decide:

- eras and which histories remain disputed;
- at least three distinct cultural naming and meaning systems;
- faiths, doubts, reformations, schisms and ordinary unbelief;
- food, labour, childhood, humour, illness, courtship, mourning and repair;
- migration, mixed families, borders, hospitality and exile;
- how magic changes plumbing, medicine, work, travel and inequality;
- how Aotearoa remains materially real rather than a fantasy texture;
- what each culture gets wrong about itself and the others.

**Artifact:** `CANON/CULTURES_AND_LIVED_WORLD_V1.md`  
**Story witness:** a meal, repair, funeral or joke that could only occur in this world.

### STAGE VI — Institutions, Power and the Protected Exit

**Question:** How do good intentions become systems, and how do systems remain correctable?

Decide:

- Mystery School governance, teaching, membership, money, correction and exit;
- opposition with legitimate historical reasons rather than cosmetic evil;
- civic institutions and ordinary dependence on imperfect systems;
- archives, routes, knowledge control and public safety;
- rank, initiation, secrecy and who may challenge whom;
- failure records, appeals, restitution and institutional death;
- how no leader, school, cat, prophecy or god becomes unquestionable.

**Artifact:** `CANON/INSTITUTIONS_AND_POWER_V1.md`  
**Story witness:** the School publicly admits a harmful failure while an opposing
institution makes a morally serious argument.

### STAGE VII — Character and Relationship Forge

**Question:** Who would still be alive if the plot vanished for a day?

Every principal character requires:

- ordinary want, hidden want and private contradiction;
- competence and competence-shaped flaw;
- body, habits, humour, work, faith or doubt;
- at least two relationships not reducible to exposition;
- something they misunderstand for a humanly credible reason;
- a choice only they can make;
- a transformation that costs rather than cosmetically upgrades them;
- a voice recognizable without dialogue tags.

Build relationship engines for unconditional friendship, family, chosen family, love,
betrayal, repair, grief, rivalry, mentorship without discipleship and companionship
without possession.

**Artifact:** `CANON/CHARACTER_AND_RELATIONSHIP_LEDGER_V1.md`  
**Story witness:** a five-person kitchen scene in which every voice, want and relationship
is legible without lore delivery.

### STAGE IX — Saga and Volume I Architecture

Design:

- antagonistic clock and consequences of inaction;
- revelation ladder: immediate, returning and saga-level questions;
- causal bridge between every major existing event;
- cost curve for love, loss, sacrifice and renewal;
- protected scenes, moved scenes, expanded scenes and retired connective tissue;
- ending that resolves the book's promise while opening a deeper wound or wonder.

**Artifact:** `EDITORIAL/VOLUME_I_STORY_ARCHITECTURE_V1.md`

## 8. The collaborative decision method

We will not conduct the forge as hundreds of disconnected yes/no questions. Each stage is
a focused workshop.

For every load-bearing decision Caelorynth presents:

1. the exact question;
2. what existing manuscript evidence already implies;
3. two to four genuinely different architectures;
4. Caelorynth's recommendation and why;
5. the cost and downstream consequence of each option;
6. Mac's decision, fusion or new direction;
7. the artifact and proof scene that will witness it.

Mac may answer through concepts, images, feelings, examples or direct verdicts. Caelorynth
translates the answer into precise proposed law and reads it back before crowning.

## 9. The proof-scene law

Planning is not allowed to become a substitute for writing. After each major foundation
stage, write the smallest scene capable of falsifying the decision.

A proof scene is not automatically manuscript canon. It asks whether the world law:

- produces human action rather than explanation;
- creates conflict without forcing stupidity;
- gives different characters different valid pressures;
- sounds alive when read aloud;
- belongs recognizably to Lycheetah;
- leaves the reader with a question worth carrying.

If the proof scene is dead, the plan returns to the forge.

## 10. Scene power standard

A powerful Lycheetah scene should contain:

- one plain human want;
- one pressure that cannot be solved by vocabulary alone;
- one material or sensory image the reader can retain;
- one relationship altered through choice;
- one cost, however small;
- one line or action that means more later;
- one protected freedom: the character could have chosen differently.

Grandeur cannot replace causality. Aphorisms cannot replace people. Trauma cannot replace
depth. Sacrifice cannot be meaningful if it was extracted by hidden authorial coercion.

## 11. Stop conditions

Pause and return to the relevant stage when:

- a new scene requires an unmade cosmological decision;
- two crowned laws contradict;
- a character exists only to explain the framework;
- ancient material cannot be transformed without appropriation or false attribution;
- a name fails collision or scene testing;
- mystery depends on a viewpoint character unnaturally refusing to think;
- the School's safety exists only in slogans rather than governance and events;
- prose becomes beautiful while the listener cannot explain what changed;
- another document begins acting as a shadow router.
