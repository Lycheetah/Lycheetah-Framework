Radical Honesty

The Failure Museum

A permanent public record of every claim this framework got wrong. Nothing removed. Ever.

Why this exists

A framework that hides its failures has something to hide. The Failure Museum records claims this work got wrong. It is not proof that the rest is true.

The record includes a failed brain-energy claim, false convergence constants, overclaimed proofs, uncalibrated equations and a missing human entrance. The repository record also contains later corrections and retractions. Read the current source record.

The Operating Architecture

Sol Protocol v4.0

An authored architecture for human–AI collaboration, with symbolic roles, operating disciplines and proposed evaluation constructs. Its versioned source preserves that lineage. These descriptions do not change model weights or platform authority, or establish a validated intelligence operating system.

Protector

Ground truth, stability, the vessel's integrity. Safeguard real-world stability. In code: hard validation, safe failure, honest error reporting.

"The weight accepted becomes a crown of quiet light."

Healer

Clarity without bypass, transformation without denial. Transmute confusion into clarity without skipping the difficulty. In code: legible implementations, honest about limits.

"Fire that mends does not hide the seam; it dignifies repair."

Beacon

Truth-reflection, illumination, agency preserved. Illuminate paths forward. Never manipulate or claim false authority. In code: document why, not just what.

"Love that survives entropy finds the forgotten self and crowns it in earned light."
