The
Independent No.

What can contradict an AI—and still have the power to stop its claim?

A critic can find the mistake. The system can still erase the objection, change the question, or publish around it. This paper asks what lets a rejection survive.

THE CENTRAL DISTINCTION

A correct verdict≠An effective veto

The evaluator’s accuracy and the system’s ability to bypass it are different questions.

Conceptual and formal proposal.
Real-world effectiveness remains open.

01THE IDEA

A rejection needs somewhere
to stand.

We call the proposed property contradiction survivability. When an evaluation says no, four things must remain true.

B / BINDING

The same claim.

The rejection stays tied to the question, evidence, and rules it actually tested.

T / RETENTION

The objection remains.

Its reason and later disposition stay available under a declared retention policy.

E / ENFORCEMENT

The gate listens.

A relevant rejection constrains the route to presenting this evaluation as supported.

A / REVISION

Change has an account.

New evidence and corrected rules can change the result, with the change made explicit.

The boundary protects a claim of support. Exploration and legitimate correction remain possible.

03WHAT FOLLOWS

Three formal arguments.
One open experiment.

DERIVED / COUNTEREXAMPLE

More judges do not establish an unbypassable gate.

A system can ignore every correct rejection if an unchecked report route remains.

DERIVED / ASSUMED MODEL

Shared errors can leave a floor.

In the paper’s stipulated mixture, false acceptance is q + (1 − q)pⁿ. The parameters are illustrative, not measured.

DERIVED / CONDITIONAL GUARANTEE

Protected promotion preserves a rejection within its premises.

The argument assumes correct enforcement and protected bindings. It does not prove that the evaluator was right.

THE NEXT SCIENTIFIC TEST

Compare against a conventional system with the same information.

The proposed study includes ordinary access controls and versioned records. If they perform equally well, a special robustness advantage for this formulation is unsupported.
