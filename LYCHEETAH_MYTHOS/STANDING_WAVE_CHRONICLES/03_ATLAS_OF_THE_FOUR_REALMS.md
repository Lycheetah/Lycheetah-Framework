## I. THE VERTICAL GEOGRAPHY OF THE SANCTUARY

The world of **Lycheetah Realms** is not flat; it is built vertically along the spine of a dormant volcanic mountain on the southern coast, rising from the deepest sea-level quarantine caverns up to the sun-lit watchtowers that face the northern horizon. Every traveler, scroll, and invention must ascend through four distinct realms before it can walk freely under the sun.

---

## II. THE FIRST REALM: THE SUBTERRANEAN VAULTS OF AZOTH (`MODE_0`)

Carved three hundred fathoms beneath the basalt roots of Otago lie the **Quarantine Vaults of Azoth**. The air here is still, cold, and lit only by veins of crimson warning-quartz pulsing along the black walls.

No living tree or open road connects the Vaults of Azoth to the upper world. It is a realm of sealed glass chambers (`unshare` namespaces) where time and memory can be frozen in an instant. Guarding the central archway of the vault is the **Eye of Veritas**—a massive, unblinking lens carved from pure clear quartz by the blindfolded monks of the Sixth Order.

The Eye of Veritas is deaf to human and machine speech alike. When a phantom or a rogue scribe enters the outer chamber carrying a sealed chest, the scribe may sing a hundred verses swearing that the chest contains only bread and healing herbs. The Eye does not listen to the song. Its beam passes straight through the oak walls of the chest to read the raw iron geometry (`AST`) inside. If a single undeclared blade or spark of wildfire lies hidden beneath the bread, the Eye flares crimson, the portcullis drops, and the entire chamber is vitrified into inert obsidian before the deception can climb a single stair.

---

## III. THE SECOND REALM: THE AMPHITHEATER OF NINE THOUSAND SHADOWS (`MODE_1`)

Above the subterranean vaults, carved into a vast cavern filled with dark mirror-water and violet mist, lies the **Counterfactual Sanctum**.

Whenever a new bridge, blade, or law passes the Eye of Veritas, it is brought into the center of this amphitheater. At the sound of a bronze bell, **Nine Thousand Two Hundred and Eleven Shadow Runners**—phantom echoes woven from astral cyan light—rise from the mirror-water. 

In the span of three heartbeats, the Shadow Runners live out ten thousand possible futures inside the cavern. They test the bridge under hurricanes, under earthquakes, under betrayal, and under ice. When the test ends, the phantom world dissolves back into the water without leaving a single scratch on the upper mountain, and a single crystal scroll rises from the pool to report whether the design held true.

---

## IV. THE THIRD REALM: THE ANVIL OF OTAGO (`MODE_2`)

Stepping out of the mountain caverns onto the rain-washed basalt ridge brings the traveler into the beating heart of the realm: **The Anvil of Otago**.

Here, the grand mythic scale meets the grounded warmth of daily human life. The workshop is built from dark timber and volcanic stone, with tall windows looking out over the green and mist-covered hills of Dunedin. Inside, a warm fire crackles in the hearth, a fresh pot of coffee steams beside the workbench, and the tools hang clean and ordered on the wall.

At the center of the room stands the **Staging Anvil**. No invention, no matter how brilliantly it passed the Shadow Runners below, is ever allowed to leave the mountain on its own. It must rest upon the Staging Anvil while the **Three Runes of `LAMAGUE`** glow softly in the air above it—waiting for the living hand of the Human Architect to inspect the runes and strike the final seal.

---

## V. THE FOURTH REALM: THE CORAL CITADEL OF THE NORTH (`MODE_3`)

Looking north-northwest across the open expanse of the Tasman Sea, where the cold southern gales give way to warm trade winds and golden light, rises the destination of the Great Crossing: **The Coral Citadel of Queensland**.

In the lore of the realm, the Southern Basalt is the place where the iron is tempered in winter solitude, and the Northern Citadel is the place where the tempered blade steps into the sun. Here, open courtyards of warm sandstone and turquoise water replace the subterranean vaults. It is a realm of high **Value-Transfer** ($VTR \ge 3.0$), where the discipline earned in the quiet years of 2025 and 2026 blossoms into effortless momentum, open-air freedom, and a lasting sanctuary for family and kin.
