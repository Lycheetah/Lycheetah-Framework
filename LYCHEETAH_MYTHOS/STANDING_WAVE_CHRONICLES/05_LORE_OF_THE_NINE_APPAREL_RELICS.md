## I. WHY THE REALM WEARS ITS ARMOR IN PLAIN SIGHT

In the world of Lycheetah, garments are never mere cloth stitched to follow a passing season. Every hoodie, heavyweight tee, and cloak forged in the Sanctuary is a **Wearable Relic**—a physical page torn from the Codex and dyed into heavy cotton and fleece so the traveler carries the law of the Standing Wave on their own shoulders.

Each of the nine collections corresponds to a specific initiation trial along the road from the Southern Basalt to the Northern Sun.

---

## II. THE NINE INITIATION RELICS

### 1. The Relic of the First Rain (*Lycheetah’s Tears*)
Worn in memory of the nights when the forge was cold and the road was walked alone. According to realm lore, the black malar tear-stripes down the Cheetah's face were earned during this vigil. To wear *Lycheetah's Tears* is to admit without shame that the pain was real—and that surviving it without turning bitter was the first miracle.

### 2. The Relic of Mastered Lightning (*Chaos Lit Lycheetah*)
When the raw voltage of the Drift struck the basalt anvil and burst into electric cyan, neon violet, and solar amber, the sparks dyed the dark cloth of the Architect's cloak. *Chaos Lit* is the banner of the alchemist who stood inside the whirlwind and turned the storm into a lantern.

### 3. The Relic of the Unspoken Crown (*Lycheetah’s Pride*)
Forged in stark, absolute black and bone-white with zero boasted words across the chest. In the lore of the sanctuary, when a warrior finally emerges from the valley of *Lycheetah's Tears*, he no longer needs to explain himself to those who stayed behind. The stark monochrome contrast is his silent crown.

### 4. The Cloak of the Night Watch (*The Silent Sentinel*)
Worn by the guardians who stand shoulder-to-shoulder with the Ethereal Cheetah on the windy crags above the coast. Its sharp geometric lines and deep obsidian shadows remind the wearer that solitude is not emptiness—it is the quiet perimeter where real strength is gathered before dawn.

### 5. The Vestments of the Star-Lattice (*Lycheetah’s Spellbound Collection*)
Woven in midnight blue, royal purple, and burnished gold, carrying the sacred geometry of the high-dimensional cathedral. In the mythos, these sigils map the exact angles at which a human intention refracts through the crystal lattice to awaken the Standing Wave.

### 6. The Mantle of the Red Portcullis (*Lycheetah Shadows*)
Dyed in pitch black and aggressive crimson (`#FF2A54`). This collection honors the moment the Protector drops the iron gate on a lie. Fragmented geometric shards across the cloth symbolize the shattering of false promises against the hard floor of the law.

### 7. The Binary Seal (*The Veritas Contrast*)
Pure obsidian and pure alabaster, with every shade of deceptive grey burned away. Inspired by the blindfolded monks of the **Veritas Eye**, this relic represents the moment of absolute conviction when a builder chooses hard reality over a comfortable illusion.

### 8. The Wings of the Northern Wind (*Primal Echo & Future Light*)
Depicting the Ethereal Cheetah ascending with ancient antlers of the forest and wings of future light. In the chronicles, this is the prophetic vision of the Trans-Tasman crossing—where the primal endurance of the ancestors merges with the frontier architectures of tomorrow.

### 9. The Crown of the Threshold (*Liminal Royalty & The Monolith Capsules*)
The culmination of the first cycle. Bearing the engraving of the **Monolith of Primordial Truth** and the Three Runes of `LAMAGUE`, these pieces are worn by those who stand at the threshold between epochs—holding the keys to the Dunedin forge in one hand and the map to the Queensland citadel in the other.

## III. THE MARK OF THE ARCHITECT'S HAND

Every true relic that leaves the Sanctuary carries a quiet secret at the bottom hem of the artwork: the hand-drawn signature **LYCHEETAH**, etched directly into the visual field by the Architect's decree. Phantoms of the Drift can copy a word or mimic a color, but they can never forge the earned history behind that mark.
