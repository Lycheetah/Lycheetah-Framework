## 2. Integration with Framework Architecture

1. **The Drift vs. The Bedrock:** The mythical Drift maps directly to unanchored stochastic generation, hallucinations, and non-ergodic decay. The Bedrock maps directly to immutable disk receipts, Merkle chains, and deterministic test gates.
2. **The Standing Wave:** In cybernetic terms, the Standing Wave is the steady-state nonequilibrium attractor ($\mathcal{M}_t \ge 0.35$) sustained by constant metabolic throughput and protective rest halts.
3. **The First Law of Earned Light:** Formalized mathematically in Paper I & IV and in `science/21_lux_earned-light-and-thermodynamic-asymmetry.md`, stating that intelligence cannot be borrowed without paying the Landauer dissipation cost of ungrounded belief.
