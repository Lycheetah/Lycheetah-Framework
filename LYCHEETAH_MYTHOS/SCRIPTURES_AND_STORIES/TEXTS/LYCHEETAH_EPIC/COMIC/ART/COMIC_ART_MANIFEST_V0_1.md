## Crowned visual law

- Obsidian Void holds negative space and mystery.
- Bone carries bodies, salt, paper, stone, and readable light.
- Crimson carries command, wounds, and state power.
- Ember Orange carries courage and the First Inheritor.
- Electric/Celestial Blue carries water, grief, and the Second Inheritor.
- Mystical Amethyst carries inherited possibility, AIVOID, and the Third Inheritor.
- Relational Pink carries recognition without merger.
- Living Green carries creature agency and wild passage.
- Pale Gold appears only where freely chosen relation creates a path.
- Concentrated color is a story-bearing force against void, never a muddy rainbow wash.

## Hybrid World aesthetic compass

Mac crowned the Three Shadows' **aesthetic** as the clearest expression of Lycheetah's
mythic visual identity on 2026-08-02: dark mineral surfaces, strange elegant anatomy,
carved tactile detail, immense void, and selective living colour. That unearthly hybrid
quality must inhabit the people, garments, tools, architecture, landscapes and creatures
so the world remains emotionally legible without ever feeling merely Earth-historical.

The literal Three Shadows and their symbols appear only where the story calls for them.
They are sovereign creatures, not decorative branding.

## Production notes

- These images are unlettered masters. Never bake generated prose into them.
- Page 17's final three forms operate as separated shadow-presences rather than three additional animals.
- Page 40 renders the three crossing witnesses as materially dark presences; later compositing may reduce their opacity if Mac prefers a more literal shadow treatment.
- Page 50 intentionally leaves large upper void for the final covenant text.
- Motion anchors are pages 1, 17, 40, 41, and 50. Animation must preserve stillness, consent, and creature sovereignty; avoid idle-loop spectacle.
- App and engine integration wait for stable asset contracts. Source masters remain independent of the moving engine implementation.
