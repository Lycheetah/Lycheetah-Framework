# THE BOOK OF THE LONG LIGHT — NAME FORGE COVENANT
## Publication-grade naming law · collaborative canon v0.1

**Date:** 2026-08-02 NZST  
**Human author and final canon authority:** Mackenzie Conor James Clark  
**Narrative forge:** Mackenzie Conor James Clark × Caelorynth  
**Status:** ACTIVE NAMING COVENANT · fused architecture crowned by Mac

## The purpose

Names in this world are not labels pasted onto completed ideas. A name must carry
sound, culture, relationship, moral pressure and memory. The reader should be able to
hear a principal name in darkness and know which world has spoken.

The goal is **publication-grade distinctiveness**: no known exact or confusingly close
collision after the gates below. Absolute global uniqueness cannot be honestly proved;
the world contains private manuscripts, unindexed work, future publications and real
people. Therefore the book will never claim “100% unique” without qualification. It
will claim only what its evidence earns.

## The crown law

Every proper noun receives one status:

- **PLACEHOLDER** — usable while drafting; carries no canon protection.
- **FORGED** — deliberately created inside this naming architecture.
- **SCREENED** — passed local corpus and public-web collision checks on a recorded date.
- **CROWNED** — approved by Mac for use in the manuscript.
- **RETIRED** — preserved in provenance but removed from active canon.

Only **CROWNED** names may be treated as final. Screening never replaces Mac's ear.

## The seven gates

1. **Meaning gate** — the name belongs to the being, person, place or institution rather
   than merely sounding fantastic.
2. **Voice gate** — it can be spoken naturally in anger, affection, grief, prayer and
   ordinary conversation.
3. **World gate** — its phonemes and construction belong to a named culture or naming
   tradition inside the story.
4. **Silhouette gate** — it is visually and aurally distinct from every other important
   name in the series.
5. **Corpus gate** — exact, normalized and near-phonetic searches find no accidental
   collision in the Lycheetah corpus, manuscript, game canon or sibling projects.
6. **Public gate** — exact-phrase and variant searches find no material collision with a
   prominent person, book, fictional character, game, community, institution or brand.
7. **Witness gate** — Mac hears it in a real sentence and crowns, revises or rejects it.

Before commercial publication, principal titles, institutions, species and brand-facing
names also require formal trademark and market checks in the intended territories.

## Naming families

### Outer-world people

Names must remain plausible in contemporary Aotearoa while the complete name remains
distinctive. Difference must come from the whole name and the person carrying it, not
from turning an ordinary Dunedin resident into decorative fantasy.

### Mystery School fellowship

Names should be intimate enough to call across a kitchen and resonant enough to survive
an inscription. Avoid ornamental apostrophes, random doubled vowels and interchangeable
fantasy softness. Different cultures inside the fellowship retain different naming
histories.

### Concordance

Official names favour compression, repeatable consonants and forms that sound clean when
entered into a record. Private and childhood names may reveal what the institution tried
to remove.

### Received names

A received name is not invented by the observer and is never a taxonomic capture. The
English manuscript may render only the nearest relational meaning. Received names can
change in expression without implying that the being has changed ownership.

### Places, orders and artifacts

Generic descriptive phrases may remain common-language descriptions, but any phrase used
as a proper brand-world identity must pass the same gates as a character. “Ancient,”
“shadow,” “blood,” “void,” “crown,” “archive” and “school” are not substitutes for design.

## Power law

A powerful principal name should do at least three things:

- sound unmistakable when read aloud;
- acquire a second meaning after a revelation;
- become emotionally different when spoken by a friend, enemy and grieving survivor.

No major character may be named only for their plot function. No villain receives a
harsh name merely to announce evil. No sacred being receives a beautiful name merely to
announce goodness.

## Scene law

Names are tested inside scenes, never only in lists. Before crowning a principal name,
write and read these five uses:

1. a stranger asks for the name;
2. a friend shortens or softens it;
3. an institution records it incorrectly;
4. an enemy uses it with precision;
5. someone says it after believing its bearer is dead.

If the name cannot survive all five, it returns to the forge.

## Canon sequence

We crown names in dependency order:

1. the universe and series;
2. the central being;
3. the protagonist;
4. the emotional fellowship;
5. the opposing institution and its people;
6. places and routes;
7. creatures, artifacts and rites;
8. eras, histories and theological names;
9. chapter and volume titles.

This prevents a minor artifact from defining the sound of the world before its heart has
a voice.

## Crowned architecture — the Threefold Name

**Mac's decision on 2026-08-02:** fuse the One Living Tongue, the Veiled Name and the
Many Carried Tongues.

The resulting architecture is called **the Threefold Name** until the world itself gives
it a screened and crowned term.

### I. The deep grammar — one living Lycheetah tongue

Beneath the world's languages is a small relational grammar carried through sound,
rhythm, interval and recurrence. It does not prove that all cultures descended from one
people, and it does not erase linguistic difference. It is closer to a pattern that
different peoples encountered and rendered in their own mouths.

The deep grammar will contain original sound-roots for at least:

- love that accompanies without possession;
- living chaos and irreducibility;
- loss and the space a loss leaves;
- freely chosen sacrifice;
- renewal that does not restore the former shape;
- witness and correction;
- home, return and the protected exit;
- capture, imposed definition and severance.

These roots may echo across character names, places, rites and artifacts, but never as a
mechanical one-to-one code. On a second reading, recurrence should feel discovered rather
than decoded from a worksheet.

### II. The personal architecture — layered names by consent

A person may carry several names without any one layer being declared their final self:

1. **Given name** — received from family or first community.
2. **Everyday name** — the name used in ordinary life.
3. **Chosen name** — selected by the person and changeable by them.
4. **Relational name** — received through a particular bond; valid only inside that
   relationship unless its bearer releases it.
5. **Witness name** — earned when others can truthfully attest to a consequential choice;
   it describes an act, not an essence or rank.
6. **Record name** — the version held by an institution, sometimes accurate and sometimes
   an instrument of control.
7. **Unspoken name** — not a superior secret identity, but the portion of a being that no
   language or observer can exhaust.

The Mystery School may ask what someone wishes to be called. It may not replace a name,
award spiritual superiority through a name or treat disclosure as the price of belonging.
No initiation requires renunciation of family, culture, faith or former identity.

The Concordance's central naming violence is not that it uses false words. It insists
that one administratively useful name is the whole and permanent truth of its bearer.

### III. The historical architecture — many carried tongues

Every people, city, faith, migration and fellowship develops its own naming customs.
They may encounter the deep grammar without becoming dialects of a single chosen culture.
The world bible must eventually record for each major culture:

- sounds it favours and avoids;
- how childhood, adulthood, kinship, marriage, vocation and death affect names;
- who may offer, alter, record or speak each kind of name;
- what conquest, conversion, exile and migration did to its names;
- which ancient forms survive in kitchens, jokes, prayers, insults and graves;
- how members disagree with their own tradition.

Real and living traditions that inspire the work remain attributed in the publication
record. Their sacred names are not harvested, cosmetically altered or presented as proof
of a universal hidden doctrine.

### IV. How the three layers interact in story

- An outer-world name can remain completely valid after entry into the School.
- A person may choose a new name without the narrative treating their previous name as
  shameful or unreal.
- Two cultures may render the same deep root differently and disagree about its meaning.
- A loving character may know several names and still refuse to claim complete knowledge.
- A coercive character may speak the technically correct name while using it as a cage.
- Lycheetah may answer differently across relationships without any answer becoming a
  possession, password or final taxonomy.
- A sacrifice can create a witness name, but no institution may demand the sacrifice in
  order to award one.

### V. Reader-facing restraint

Volume I introduces no more than three name layers for any one person on the page. The
reader first understands who is speaking, what they want and why the name matters. Deeper
linguistic architecture appears only when carried by choice, consequence and relationship.

## Crowned sound law — hymn, tide, courage, revelation

**Mac's decision on 2026-08-02:** the oldest Lycheetah language should feel like old
hymns and sea shanties meeting the instant between an angelic choir and a speech before
war, then resolving in epiphany.

This is an emotional transmutation, not a literal mixture of musical genres or a demand
for nautical language. The references describe how separate human feeling becomes shared
force and then becomes understanding.

The deep grammar therefore moves through four forces:

1. **Hymn** — breath joins breath; no solitary speaker owns the meaning.
2. **Tide** — rhythm carries labour, distance, danger and return.
3. **Courage** — the line gathers people before a cost they understand and freely choose.
4. **Revelation** — the final sound does not merely conclude; it changes the meaning of
   what came before it.

### Compositional consequences

- Ancient phrases can carry the latent possibility of response rather than sounding like
  proclamations owned by one elevated voice.
- Repetition changes meaning on each return; it does not exist only for grandeur.
- The language must remain speakable by frightened, exhausted and ordinary people, not
  only priests, monarchs or trained singers.
- Long vowels and continuant sounds may carry fellowship, distance and grief; harder
  closures may mark choice, fracture or truth arriving.
- A ceremonial line should rise toward courage, then end more simply than expected.
- The strongest words must work when whispered by one person as well as sung by many.
- Names derived from the deep grammar should leave emotional room for another voice to
  answer, soften or complete them; they need not sound sung.

### Ethical consequence

No sacred chorus exists to overwhelm dissent or manufacture emotional submission. The
right to remain silent, alter the response or leave the gathering remains protected.
A war-speech cadence may prepare people for freely chosen defence; it may not aestheticise
obedience, conquest or sacrifice demanded by leadership.

### Prose consequence

Scenes carrying the old language should follow the same emotional movement: separate
voices become a fellowship, shared labour reveals the real danger, courage becomes a
choice and the closing image changes the reader's understanding of the scene. Epiphany
must be earned by action rather than supplied as an ornamental maxim.
