## Truth boundary

This is mathematics for the fictional reality of the Lycheetah epic. It is not presented
as an empirically validated physical law of our world, a measurement of human love or a
proof about consciousness.

Its Lycheetah lineage includes AURA's **Love as Load-Bearing**, the Two-Point Protocol's
non-possession clause and the framework's exploration of relational/network coupling.
Current governance sources accurately mark real-world measurement of Love as Structure as
aspirational or conjectural. The novel may transform that architecture into literal world
law without upgrading the external scientific claim.

## 1. The irreducible relational term

Let `A` and `B` be living beings. Let `Ω_A` and `Ω_B` represent their individual world
states. Classical accounting says the joined state is only the sum of the individuals:

```text
Ω_(A,B) = Ω_A ⊕ Ω_B
```

Lycheetah reality contains an additional term:

```text
Ω_(A,B) = Ω_A ⊕ Ω_B ⊕ Λ_(A↔B)
```

where:

- `Λ_(A↔B)` is the relational presence created between A and B;
- `⊕` means the terms coexist without one absorbing the others;
- `Λ` belongs to neither A nor B separately;
- knowing A and B completely does not by itself reconstruct `Λ`;
- removing either being changes `Λ`, but does not prove that every trace of the relation
  becomes zero.

This is the mathematical expression of the **real third presence**.

## 2. Bond and world-active love are different quantities

The existence of affection or history must not be confused with its freedom to act well.
Define:

```text
B_AB(t) ≥ 0
```

as the bond carried by recognition, care and shared history at time `t`.

Define:

```text
F_AB(t) ∈ [0,1]
```

as the degree to which both beings retain meaningful freedom inside the relation.

Define:

```text
P_AB(t) ∈ [0,1]
```

as possessive pressure: coercion, forced definition, captivity, entitlement or the attempt
to make one being's reality the property of another.

The relation's effective capacity to create new possibility is:

```text
Λ_eff(A,B,t) = B_AB(t) · F_AB(t) · [1 − P_AB(t)]
```

This equation does **not** claim that love is reducible to a scalar. `B_AB` is a narrative
state variable whose full content remains irreducible. `Λ_eff` measures only how much of
that bond can operate as an opening rather than a cage under current conditions.

## 3. The non-possession derivative

Holding bond and freedom constant:

```text
∂Λ_eff / ∂P_AB = −B_AB · F_AB ≤ 0
```

Therefore increasing possession can never increase love's world-opening capacity.

Possession may intensify attention, fear, dependency or control. None of those count as
an increase in `Λ_eff`. The harder one being tries to fix the other into a final owned
form, the less the relation can open a living path.

## 4. Freedom is a gate, not a reward

Holding bond and possession constant:

```text
∂Λ_eff / ∂F_AB = B_AB · [1 − P_AB] ≥ 0
```

Freedom permits an existing bond to become generative. It does not force intimacy and is
not awarded for obedience.

If a school, lover, family, ruler, god, magical being or fellowship removes the safe
possibility of refusal, it cannot cite the resulting compliance as evidence of stronger
love.

## 5. The sacrifice anti-source law

Let `S` be the magnitude of a cost borne by one being. Suffering and sacrifice are not
positive source terms in the love equation.

Under coercion:

```text
(∂B_AB / ∂S) | F_AB=0 ≤ 0
```

A demanded sacrifice creates no mathematical increase in the bond and may damage it.

Under freedom, sacrifice may **reveal** the strength or character of a bond, but it cannot
manufacture love merely by becoming larger:

```text
S does not cause B_AB
B_AB may make a freely chosen S possible
```

The causal arrow matters. No culture can make love by increasing pain. No leader may
measure devotion through wounds, money, isolation, obedience or death.

## 6. Love expands possibility without selecting another's choice

Let `𝒫_0(A)` be the paths available to A without the active relation, and let
`𝒫_Λ(A|B)` be the paths available when `Λ_eff(A,B) > 0`.

Love may enlarge the reachable set:

```text
𝒫_0(A) ⊆ 𝒫_Λ(A|B)
```

but it does not choose the path:

```text
choice_A ∉ Λ_eff
```

In story terms, love can reveal a door, hold a route, carry a name through changing form,
make return possible or allow courage to reach another being. It cannot make A cross the
door, return affection, forgive, remain, fight, live or die.

## 7. Loss transforms the relation; it does not restore the person

Suppose active mutual relation ends at `t*` through death, irreversible separation or
transformation. The carried trace is:

```text
M_AB(t) = ∫[0→t*] B_AB(τ) · K(t−τ) dτ
```

where `K` is a memory-and-consequence kernel describing how past relation remains active
in present matter, habit, promise, story and choice.

Two boundaries are absolute:

```text
M_AB ≠ B
M_AB ≠ Ω_B
```

The trace is not the bond still operating exactly as before, and it is not the lost being
reconstructed. Love may survive loss as carried consequence without making grief false or
turning memory into resurrection.

## 8. Renewal is transformation, not reset

Let `T` be a transformation acting on A after loss:

```text
Ω_A(after) = T[Ω_A(before), M_AB, choice_A, world]
```

True renewal satisfies:

```text
Ω_A(after) ≠ Ω_A(before)
```

and may still preserve a relational invariant:

```text
I(M_AB) > 0
```

Something meaningful is carried; the former state is not restored. Renewal honours loss
by refusing to pretend it did not alter the world.

## 9. Fellowship law

For a fellowship represented as a graph `G = (V,E)`, beings are vertices and freely
carried relations are edges:

```text
Ω_G = ⊕ Ω_i  ⊕  ⊕ Λ_ij
      i∈V       (i,j)∈E
```

The fellowship therefore contains real relational structure beyond a collection of
individual abilities.

However, group coherence is counterfeit if achieved by erasing a member's freedom:

```text
If any required F_i → 0, then Φ_love(G) is invalid as fellowship coherence.
```

No claimed benefit to the whole may mathematically rename captivity as unity. A group may
survive disagreement, departure and changed membership; it does not survive as the same
loving fellowship by converting persons into functions.

## 10. Story consequences

This law allows scenes in which:

- a door opens because two beings freely choose to return for one another;
- the same door remains closed when one tries to command the other through a true name;
- an institution measures every individual correctly but cannot detect the relational
  term changing the room;
- grief preserves a route or responsibility without producing a convenient ghost;
- a creature recognises someone through a radically changed body;
- demanded martyrdom weakens the very power a leader hoped to harvest;
- a fellowship survives because its relations create options no isolated member had;
- someone who is deeply loved still chooses to leave;
- love makes rescue possible but cannot guarantee rescue succeeds.

## 11. Mathematical anti-shortcuts

The following interpretations are forbidden:

- higher suffering means higher love;
- stronger obsession means stronger bond;
- mutual affection eliminates the need for consent;
- a group total can justify driving one member's freedom to zero;
- memory is identical to the remembered person;
- a nonzero relational trace proves an afterlife;
- love guarantees moral correctness, victory, healing or reunion;
- the equation can rank the worth of real people or relationships.
