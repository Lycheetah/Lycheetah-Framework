# RESEARCH EXPEDITION V2
## First deeper cross-cultural pass for the Living Scripture

**Status:** SELECTIVE RESEARCH PASS · not systematic review · publication gates remain

## Buber
Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy describes Buber's I–Thou relation as emphasizing the
"between" of self and other and, in his late thought, insists that distance is needed to
secure otherness without which relation cannot take shape.

**Use:** strengthens Living Difference.  
**Do not claim:** Buber = Lycheetah Field.

## Levinas
SEP describes Levinasian transcendence in terms of an other person who eludes comprehensive
knowledge and whose encounter grounds responsibility.

**Use:** challenges any Lycheetah model that thinks ethical adequacy follows from a complete
description.  
**Friction:** Levinasian ethical asymmetry is stronger than Lycheetah's usual mutuality.

## Madhyamaka
SEP identifies emptiness of inherent natures as characteristic of Madhyamaka.

**Use:** anti-reification pressure.  
**Do not claim:** emptiness = Field, or Buddhism confirms relational metaphysics in our form.

## Laozi
SEP emphasizes the inability of names to fully capture the constant Dao; names delimit.

**Use:** every name needs a remainder.  
**Friction:** Lycheetah loves naming systems and therefore needs Daoist suspicion nearby.

## Confucian naming
SEP discussions of Analects 13.3 connect correct naming with speech, conduct and social
order.

**Use:** counters the lazy idea that because names are incomplete they do not matter.  
**Friction:** social-role naming can itself become reduction.

## Śaṅkara
SEP explains *neti neti* as negative language that avoids reifying brahman into a finite
object.

**Use:** negative-boundary definitions.  
**Do not claim:** Advaita metaphysics is a Lycheetah source unless actual historical
influence is documented.

## Rabbinic Oven of Akhnai
Sefaria preserves the Bava Metzia 59b narrative in which extraordinary signs and a
heavenly voice enter a halakhic dispute and are rejected as determinative by the majority.

**Use:** interpretation after inherited authority.  
**Caution:** not a generic anti-revelation parable; requires Jewish context and commentary.

## 1 Corinthians 8
The chapter concerns food sacrificed to idols and contrasts knowledge that "puffs up"
with love that "builds up," while continuing into questions of conscience and community.

**Use:** knowledge and care.  
**Caution:** keep Pauline/Christian context; do not universalize one line into a free-floating
Lycheetah maxim.

## Qur'an 2:256 and 49:13
2:256 is a major locus for the statement that there is no compulsion in religion; Quran.com
also exposes interpretive debates about scope/abrogation. 49:13 addresses human plurality,
knowing one another, and piety/righteousness before Allah.

**Use:** compulsion, plurality, encounter.  
**Caution:** deeper tafsir and Muslim scholarly review required before publication-level
transmutation.

## Whakapapa / mātauranga Māori
Te Ara describes whakapapa as linking people with other living things, earth and sky and
shaping Māori worldview. Royal Society Te Apārangi describes mātauranga Māori as arising
from kinship relationships between people and the natural world, with humans situated
within that relational order.

**Use:** provenance as relation and responsibility, not merely citation.  
**Hard gate:** substantive public integration requires Māori human partnership/review.

## Etuaptmumk / Two-Eyed Seeing
Reid et al., writing with Elder Albert Marshall, explicitly criticize "integration" when
it functions as assimilation and argue for coexistence and complementarity between
knowledge systems.

**Use:** coexistence before synthesis.  
**Hard rule:** do not rename or genericize Etuaptmumk as a Lycheetah technique.

## Hunhu/Ubuntu
IEP presents communally oriented accounts of personhood and also surfaces debate over the
relative place of individual and community.

**Use:** challenge atomistic selfhood.  
**Friction:** Lycheetah sovereignty resists community becoming final authority over the
person.

## Gadamer
SEP describes understanding as dialogical, changing the horizon of the interpreter and
forming a new context in which familiar and alien are both affected.

**Use:** encounter changes both sides.  
**Caution:** "fusion" must not become an excuse for erasing provenance or power asymmetry.

## Pragmatism / Peirce
SEP presents inquiry as a process and describes Peirce's contrast between science and
belief fixed by tenacity, authority or a priori elegance.

**Use:** adversarial check against sacred-system self-sealing.

## Feminist epistemology
SEP centers situated knowers and the role of social position in what is seen, asked and
authorized.

**Use:** provenance must include knower position and power.  
**Caution:** situated knowledge does not imply every claim is equally warranted.

## Extended cognition
SEP discusses Clark and Chalmers' Otto/notebook argument and its contested status.

**Use:** AI can be studied as cognitive scaffold/coupling.  
**Hard boundary:** this does not establish shared or machine consciousness.
