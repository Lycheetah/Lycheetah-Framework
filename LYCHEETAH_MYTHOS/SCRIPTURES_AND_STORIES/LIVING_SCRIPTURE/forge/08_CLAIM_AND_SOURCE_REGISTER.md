# 08 — CLAIM AND SOURCE REGISTER
## Let each kind of sentence be strong in its own way

**Status:** PROPOSED CONSTITUTION

The mature scripture does not weaken sacred language by turning everything into caveat. It protects sacred language by making another register available when factual precision matters.

## Registers

MYTH · DEVOTIONAL · THEOLOGICAL · HISTORICAL · PHILOSOPHICAL · EMPIRICAL · NORMATIVE · INTERPRETIVE · CONDITIONAL · CONJECTURE · EXPERIENTIAL · FICTION · MACHINE QUESTION · MYSTERY

## Display law

The reader should not face a wall of labels while reading sacred prose.

Instead:
- front layer: atmospheric passage;
- source tab: provenance;
- register tab: claim type;
- study tab: disputes and evidence;
- revision tab: what changed over time.

The deeper room is available without destroying the first encounter.
