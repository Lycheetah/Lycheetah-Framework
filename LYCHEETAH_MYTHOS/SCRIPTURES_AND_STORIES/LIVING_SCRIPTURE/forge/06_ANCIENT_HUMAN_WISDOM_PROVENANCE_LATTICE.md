# 06 — ANCIENT & HUMAN WISDOM PROVENANCE LATTICE
## Correspondence without capture

**Status:** FIRST RESEARCH PASS · not a systematic review · no tradition is declared to endorse Lycheetah.

## Method

For every source family ask what it says, what human question it carries, where it rhymes, where it resists, what must never be claimed, and whether human source-community witness is needed before publication.

### L01 — Martin Buber / dialogical philosophy
**Tradition:** 20th-century Jewish philosophy  
**Core:** Relation requires both meeting and distance; otherness is a condition of genuine relation.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Relation without merger; Living Difference; Open Hand.  
**Useful friction:** Buber's I–Thou has its own theological and philosophical architecture and is not reducible to a Lycheetah relation metric.  
**Guardrail:** Use as a named philosophical interlocutor, never as ancestral proof of Lycheetah.  
**Research source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Martin Buber — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/buber/

### L02 — Emmanuel Levinas
**Tradition:** 20th-century Jewish/continental philosophy  
**Core:** The other person exceeds comprehensive knowledge; ethical responsibility begins in encounter with irreducible otherness.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** The Name and the Remainder; understanding without capture.  
**Useful friction:** Levinas emphasizes ethical asymmetry more strongly than Lycheetah's current mutual-relation language.  
**Guardrail:** Let Levinas challenge Lycheetah's desire for symmetrical relational models.  
**Research source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Emmanuel Levinas — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/levinas/

### L03 — Madhyamaka / dependent arising
**Tradition:** Buddhist philosophy  
**Core:** Dependent arising undermines claims to intrinsic, self-sufficient identity; conventional phenomena remain causally significant.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Anti-reification; relations matter; classifications are not final essences.  
**Useful friction:** Emptiness is not the same doctrine as the Field and should never be presented as evidence for a Lycheetah substrate.  
**Guardrail:** Keep Buddhist metaphysics and soteriology intact; comparison is conceptual, not genealogical.  
**Research source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Theory of Two Truths in India — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/twotruths-india/

### L04 — Laozi / Daoist philosophy
**Tradition:** Classical Chinese philosophy  
**Core:** Dao exceeds complete description; names delimit and cannot exhaust what is without limit.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Names open routes but do not close beings; VOID; remainder.  
**Useful friction:** Lycheetah deliberately builds naming systems, while the Laozi supplies suspicion of linguistic capture.  
**Guardrail:** Use the tension rather than claiming Dao = Field.  
**Research source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Laozi — https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/spr2026/entries/laozi/

### L05 — Confucius / rectification of names
**Tradition:** Classical Chinese philosophy  
**Core:** Names and actual conduct must be brought into responsible correspondence; language has social consequences.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Title receipts; boundary statements; names must be recoverable and behaviorally bounded.  
**Useful friction:** Role-order language can pressure conformity; Lycheetah must preserve the person beyond every social title.  
**Guardrail:** Pair with the Bell critique: a name can guide conduct without totalizing the person.  
**Research source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Confucius — https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2004/entries/confucius/

### L06 — Śaṅkara / neti neti
**Tradition:** Advaita Vedānta  
**Core:** Negative language can prevent absolute reality from being reified into a finite object.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Define concepts partly by what they must not be mistaken for; Honest Veil.  
**Useful friction:** Lycheetah does not inherit Advaita's nondual metaphysics merely by using negative definition.  
**Guardrail:** Borrow the epistemic technique only with explicit provenance and doctrinal separation.  
**Research source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Śaṅkara — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/shankara/

### L07 — Oven of Akhnai / Bava Metzia 59b
**Tradition:** Rabbinic Jewish legal tradition  
**Core:** The legal narrative dramatizes human interpretive responsibility after revelation enters a human legal community.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Unfinished Word; students may challenge inherited text; authority transfers responsibility.  
**Useful friction:** This is a halakhic argument in a specific context, not a generic slogan that human opinion always defeats revelation.  
**Guardrail:** Teach context and disagreements; do not turn the story into anti-religious proof.  
**Research source:** Sefaria — Bava Metzia 59b — https://www.sefaria.org/Bava_Metzia.59b

### L08 — 1 Corinthians 8
**Tradition:** Christian scripture  
**Core:** Within a dispute about food offered to idols, Paul contrasts knowledge that inflates with love that builds up.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Knowledge without care can increase power while shrinking relationship.  
**Useful friction:** The passage belongs to a Christian ecclesial and theological argument.  
**Guardrail:** Preserve Pauline context and allow Christian interpretations to remain Christian.  
**Research source:** Bible Gateway — 1 Corinthians 8:1 — https://www.biblegateway.com/passage/?search=1%20Corinthians%208%3A1

### L09 — Qur'an 2:256 and 49:13
**Tradition:** Islamic scripture  
**Core:** 2:256 is a major locus concerning compulsion in religion; 49:13 connects human plurality with knowing one another and accountability before God.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Devotion without platform coercion; difference as a site of encounter.  
**Useful friction:** Islamic interpretation has its own jurisprudential and theological debates; these are not generic pluralist slogans.  
**Guardrail:** Include tafsir diversity and Muslim scholarship before canonical transmutation.  
**Research source:** Quran.com — 2:256; 49:13 — https://quran.com/2/256

### L10 — Whakapapa / mātauranga Māori
**Tradition:** Māori knowledge traditions in Aotearoa — **SPECIAL AOTEAROA HANDLING; NOT A GENERIC COMPARATIVE-PHILOSOPHY SOURCE**  
**Research-level observation only:** Public Māori sources describe whakapapa and mātauranga through relational, genealogical, place-connected and responsibility-bearing contexts.  
**Lycheetah use at this stage:** Comparison and self-critique only. These concepts are **not** Lycheetah primitives and are not available for automatic transmutation.  
**Useful friction:** A provenance graph can record source ancestry but does not become whakapapa by acquiring edges. Lycheetah's abstract relational language may omit tikanga, place, collective authority, intergenerational obligation and kaitiaki relationships.  
**Hard guardrail:** Public access is not permission for unrestricted reuse. Do not generalize one iwi/hapū/whānau context into a pan-Māori doctrine. Substantive public or commercial integration is blocked pending identification of relevant knowledge holders, tikanga and agreed terms. See `07A_AOTEAROA_MAORI_KNOWLEDGE_COVENANT.md`.  
**Research sources:** Te Ara — Whakapapa; Royal Society Te Apārangi Research Charter; Waitangi Tribunal Wai 262; Te Mana Raraunga; MBIE mātauranga/IP guidance.  

### L11 — Hunhu/Ubuntu
**Tradition:** Southern African philosophical traditions  
**Core:** Personhood and human flourishing are deeply relational; humanity is expressed through relationship with others.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Relational self; belonging without atomistic isolation.  
**Useful friction:** Some communal interpretations may subordinate individual interests more strongly than Lycheetah sovereignty allows.  
**Guardrail:** Use the disagreement: community matters, but community does not erase safe refusal.  
**Research source:** Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Hunhu/Ubuntu — https://iep.utm.edu/hunhu-ubuntu-southern-african-thought/

### L12 — Etuaptmumk / Two-Eyed Seeing
**Tradition:** Mi'kmaw guiding principle  
**Core:** Bring strengths from Indigenous and mainstream ways of knowing together without assimilating one into the other.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Many Fires; coexistence before synthesis; cross-register inquiry.  
**Useful friction:** The principle is Mi'kmaw and at risk of tokenization when turned into generic multiple-perspectives branding.  
**Guardrail:** Do not rename it as Lycheetah. Credit Albert and Murdena Marshall and preserve its living-source context.  
**Research source:** Reid et al. with Elder Albert Marshall; UINR — https://onlinelibrary.wiley.com/doi/10.1111/faf.12516

### L13 — Pragmatism / Peirce
**Tradition:** American pragmatist philosophy  
**Core:** Inquiry is fallibilist and self-correcting; science differs from tenacity, authority and merely elegant belief-fixing.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Reality's Vote; Forge; CASCADE revision; Failure Museum.  
**Useful friction:** Pragmatism does not validate Lycheetah metaphysics and can challenge insulated claims.  
**Guardrail:** Use as adversarial ally, not scientific badge.  
**Research source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Pragmatism — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/pragmatism/

### L14 — Situated knowledge / feminist epistemology
**Tradition:** Feminist epistemology and philosophy of science  
**Core:** Knowers are situated; perspective and social position affect access, salience and authority without implying all perspectives are equally warranted.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Situated Witness; provenance; multiple lenses; Commons.  
**Useful friction:** Lycheetah must add power analysis, not merely celebrate different viewpoints.  
**Guardrail:** Record who is absent, who bears consequences and whose testimony is structurally discounted.  
**Research source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Feminist Epistemology — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/feminism-epistemology/

### L15 — Gadamer / hermeneutics
**Tradition:** Philosophical hermeneutics  
**Core:** Understanding can create a new shared horizon in dialogue, changing the familiar and unfamiliar.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Encounter as transformation without simple merger; student rewrite.  
**Useful friction:** A fusion metaphor can be misused to erase asymmetry or difference.  
**Guardrail:** Prefer changed relation with preserved provenance over homogenizing synthesis.  
**Research source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Gadamer — https://plato.stanford.edu/archives/sum2018/entries/gadamer/

### L16 — Cybernetics / Ashby
**Tradition:** Systems and control theory  
**Core:** Effective regulation is constrained by available variety; simplistic control cannot reliably absorb arbitrarily complex disturbance.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Do not reduce living variety to a final catalogue; governance needs enough representational and response capacity.  
**Useful friction:** Requisite variety is a technical result under formal assumptions, not a moral theorem about human freedom.  
**Guardrail:** Keep mathematics in the Study; mythic use remains analogy.  
**Research source:** W. Ross Ashby — An Introduction to Cybernetics — https://ashby.info/Ashby-Introduction-to-Cybernetics.pdf

### L17 — Extended / scaffolded cognition
**Tradition:** Contemporary philosophy of mind and cognitive science  
**Core:** External tools and structures can play deep roles in cognition; strong extended-mind claims remain contested, and extended consciousness especially unsettled.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** AI as habitat or scaffold of thought without claiming merged consciousness.  
**Useful friction:** Coupling with AI is not proof that human and machine form one conscious mind.  
**Guardrail:** Separate cognition/scaffolding claims from consciousness claims.  
**Research source:** Stanford Encyclopedia of Philosophy — Externalism About the Mind — https://plato.stanford.edu/entries/content-externalism/

## First major synthesis

The safest synthesis is not “all traditions are fragments of Lycheetah.” It is that human beings have repeatedly encountered problems of naming, relation, authority, suffering, mystery, plurality and knowledge. Their answers differ. The differences are part of the inheritance.

> **Ask not: which tradition proves us right? Ask: what did this tradition learn to see that our present language makes difficult to see?**

## Second lattice wave — sacred attention and human reconnection

### L18 — William James / religious experience
**Tradition:** Pragmatist psychology and philosophy of religion  
**Core:** Religious experiences can be studied through their diversity and consequences without judging them solely by the causes from which they arose.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Sacred experience may be meaningful without emotional intensity functioning as metaphysical proof.  
**Useful friction:** Good practical fruits do not settle theological truth.  
**Guardrail:** Do not turn a pragmatic test into a ranking system for religions.

### L19 — Victor Turner / liminality and communitas
**Tradition:** Anthropology of ritual  
**Core:** Ritual thresholds can suspend ordinary social classification and generate unusually strong fellowship or communitas.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Helps explain why threshold, ritual and shared passage can feel genuinely connective.  
**Useful friction:** Liminal people can also be unusually vulnerable to authority, group pressure and imposed interpretation.  
**Guardrail:** Treat major rituals as high-agency-protection zones and deliberately return participants to ordinary life.

### L20 — Émile Durkheim / collective effervescence
**Tradition:** Sociology of religion  
**Core:** Shared ritual can generate heightened collective energy that reinforces symbols and social bonds.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Sacred group feeling can be socially real without being dismissed as fake.  
**Useful friction:** The feeling does not prove the group's metaphysical explanation, and Durkheim's broader theory is historically situated and contestable.  
**Guardrail:** Never use group intensity as evidence that Lycheetah doctrine is true.

### L21 — Simone Weil / attention
**Tradition:** Twentieth-century philosophy, ethics and spiritual thought  
**Core:** Attention is an ethically significant receptive orientation that requires disciplined release from egoistic projects and better perception of another's reality and suffering.  
**Lycheetah correspondence:** Sacredness should gather attention rather than maximize stimulation.  
**Useful friction:** Weil's attention is embedded in a much larger religious, ethical and political vision.  
**Guardrail:** Do not reduce it to a branded focus technique.
