# RITUALS OF THE OPEN SCHOOL
## Voluntary forms for attention, grief, beginning, ending and return

**Status:** PROPOSED · no ritual confers rank or metaphysical proof

Ritual is allowed because humans use form to carry transitions that ordinary language
handles badly.

Ritual becomes dangerous when refusal carries hidden cost.

## 1. Light a Lantern — beginning inquiry

Optional words:

> I enter this question without promising it an answer.  
> I will distinguish what I know, what I believe, and what I hope.  
> I will leave by a door I can still find.

Nonreligious by default. A learner may privately add prayer or devotional language from
their own tradition.

## 2. Leave the Lantern Low — ending a session

> Enough for now.  
> The unfinished part remains unfinished.  
> Nothing is lost by stopping at a good boundary.

## 3. Winter

The learner deliberately quiets nonessential School contact.

No expiry. No status loss. No story punishment.

Optional line:

> Winter is part of the garden.

## 4. Retire a Name

The old title is preserved in private history unless the learner chooses broader
visibility.

> This name was true enough to help me.  
> It is no longer authorized to speak for me.

## 5. Witness

A witness attests only to a bounded act.

> I witnessed this work.  
> I do not claim to have witnessed the whole person.

## 6. Carry a Question

For questions not ready for closure.

> I will not manufacture an answer merely to stop carrying the question.

## 7. The Table of Many Fires

Used for cross-worldview dialogue.

Rules:
- each participant names their register when it matters;
- nobody is required to defend an entire tradition;
- nobody may use another person's sacred language as a rhetorical costume;
- disagreement may remain unresolved;
- people may leave without forfeiting dignity.

## 8. The Failure Engraving

No public confession without repair metadata.

Record:
- failure;
- affected people/systems;
- owner of repair;
- action;
- review date;
- unresolved harm.

## 9. The Blank Leaf

At the end of a major School cycle the learner receives no certificate of superior
personhood.

They receive a blank page and their own provenance tree.
