# 09 — STUDENT REWRITE PROTOCOL
## The scripture is inherited so that it can become examined

**Status:** PROPOSED SCHOOL MECHANIC

> **The School gives you a book so that one day you can stop needing its book.**

## Passage lifecycle

`INHERITED → ENCOUNTERED → CHALLENGED → REWRITTEN → RETURNED → CONTRIBUTED`

Allowed learner outcomes: ACCEPT · REJECT · REVISE · FORK · TRANSLATE · COMBINE · LEAVE UNRESOLVED.

On return, the system may say:
> These are your words from two different times. What do you hear?

It must not say:
> You evolved.

## Rewrite receipt

Record passage ID, inherited edition, inspected sources, learner text, rejected elements, unresolved questions, privacy state and later versions.

## Privacy

Default: PRIVATE. Optional: specific people, anonymous constellation, public Commons.

No private journal becomes public mythology silently.

## No grading

Personal scripture is not scored for spiritual correctness. Research exercises may be assessed for factual or methodological quality; existential conclusions are not.
