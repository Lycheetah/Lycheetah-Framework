# 07 — CULTURAL SAFETY AND TRANSMUTATION PROTOCOL
## Coexistence before synthesis

**Status:** PROPOSED CONSTITUTION · revised 4 October 2026

## The seven gates

### 1. SOURCE
Identify the actual text, person, community, practice or scholarly interpretation.

### 2. CONTEXT
Record the tradition's own problem-space. Do not detach a line merely because it sounds useful to Lycheetah.

### 3. REGISTER
Is the material scripture, ritual, law, philosophy, oral history, scholarly reconstruction, living practice, empirical knowledge or later commentary?

### 4. FRICTION
Write where the source does **not** fit Lycheetah. No entry passes with only similarities.

### 5. POWER
Ask whether this is a living Indigenous or sacred knowledge system, whether the source community has been dispossessed, whether particular people hold responsibilities or authority in relation to the material, and whether publication or commercialization changes the ethical situation.

### 6. HUMAN RELATIONSHIP / WITNESS
For living sacred or Indigenous traditions, substantive integration requires more than web research and citation. Identify the relevant people, authority, tikanga/protocols and relationship appropriate to the intended use.

### 7. TRANSMUTATION
Only after the previous gates may Lycheetah consider original transmutation. It must use its own expression, preserve provenance, avoid secret-identity claims, preserve source-community agency, and remain correctable or removable if the relationship shows the use was wrong.

## Core rule

> **Knowledge systems may stand beside each other before they are asked to merge.**

Sometimes the correct outcome is **no synthesis**.

## Aotearoa-specific boundary

Māori knowledge is governed additionally by `07A_AOTEAROA_MAORI_KNOWLEDGE_COVENANT.md`.

It is **not** handled simply under a generic “living Indigenous tradition” rule.

The short version is:

> **STUDY IS NOT PERMISSION. PUBLIC IS NOT OWNERLESS. RELATIONSHIP PRECEDES TRANSMUTATION.**
