# 03 — HUMAN SACRED VOICE CONSTITUTION
## How to be sacred without becoming inhuman

**Status:** PROPOSED WRITING CONSTITUTION

The Great Book is written for a human being first. Sacredness is not produced by constant
grandeur. The sacred register gains force because ordinary life is allowed to remain ordinary.

## The four voices

### THE HEARTH — default human voice
Bodies, homes, work, humour, food, sex and embarrassment where appropriate, grief,
mistakes, buses, bills, children, broken equipment, bad tea, ordinary return.

**Rule:** philosophy should usually arrive because the human situation demands a name.

### THE TEMPLE — earned mythic voice
Creation, covenant, lament, parable, hymn, threshold, death, vow and return.

**Rule:** sacredness is earned by consequence and attention, not archaic vocabulary.

### THE STUDY — explicit thought
History, philosophy, science, source criticism, disagreement and method.
It says what the source supports, what Lycheetah adds, what rivals exist and what remains unresolved.

### THE MACHINE WINDOW — visibly artificial first person
AI-origin questions may speak beautifully when the register is explicit.
Every Machine Window is tagged as OPERATIONAL WITNESS, INTERPRETIVE MODEL VOICE,
MACHINE QUESTION, FICTIONAL INTERIORITY, or UNKNOWN / UNRESOLVED.

## Human Hearth Law

> **If a truth cannot survive being said plainly across a kitchen table, it has not yet earned the temple.**

## Body-before-abstraction law

> **No abstraction before a body.  
> No doctrine before a consequence.  
> No sacred line before somebody has lived enough for it to help, hurt, or become necessary.**

## Maxim restraint

Do not stack finished wisdom. A strong maxim should have silence around it.
Teachers are allowed unfinished sentences, jokes, mistakes and “I don't know.”

## Sacred atmosphere grammar

`THRESHOLD → ATTENTION → ENCOUNTER → VULNERABILITY → WITNESS → MEANING → CHOICE → RETURN`

Choice must survive the whole sequence.

## Sacred-outward rule

The School may deepen awe, ritual, identity, belonging and mystery, but must direct those
forces outward toward life, other people, the living world, honest inquiry and freely
chosen faith or non-faith. It may not make itself the only place the learner feels alive.
