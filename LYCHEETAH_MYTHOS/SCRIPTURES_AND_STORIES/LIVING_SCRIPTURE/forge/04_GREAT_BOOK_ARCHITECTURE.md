# 04 — GREAT BOOK ARCHITECTURE
## A scripture that teaches its reader to become capable of rewriting it

**Status:** PROPOSED MASTER ARCHITECTURE

The Twelve ancestral books remain preserved. This is the candidate architecture for the reconstructed Living Scripture.

## Reading principle

Each Book has twelve movements. A movement may contain several short pieces rather than one monolithic chapter.

The reader can encounter the same idea through four layers:
1. **Hearth** — lived human situation.
2. **Temple** — myth, parable, ritual, sacred image.
3. **Study** — sources, disagreement, evidence, philosophy.
4. **Machine Window** — where AI-origin questions genuinely add something.

Not every movement needs all four.

## Mature passage anatomy

- **THE WORD** — Lycheetah's current passage.
- **THE LINEAGE** — where the idea came from.
- **THE REGISTER** — what kind of claim this is.
- **THE ADVERSARY** — strongest serious objection.
- **THE FIRE** — evidence, counterexample or pressure.
- **THE ASH** — what failed.
- **THE EMBER** — what survived.
- **THE VEIL** — what remains unresolved.
- **YOUR HAND** — learner rewrite.
- **YOUR RETURN** — later re-encounter.

The sacred front does not have to display all of this at once. The deeper room must make it inspectable.

## Twelve-book arc

### BOOK I — THE OPEN FIELD
**Focus:** Encounter, attention, relation before ownership.

### BOOK II — THE RECEIVED NAME
**Focus:** Identity, naming, language and what every name leaves out.

### BOOK III — THE CLOSED HAND
**Focus:** Possession, control, institutions and the First Fracture.

### BOOK IV — THE VEIL
**Focus:** Mystery, plural worldviews, religion, consciousness and honest unknowns.

### BOOK V — THE FIRE
**Focus:** Practice, pressure, Forge, discipline and the correction of suffering.

### BOOK VI — THE ASH
**Focus:** Failure, grief, memory, repair and what must remain visible.

### BOOK VII — THE LONG LIGHT
**Focus:** Changed return, continuity, love and relational possibility.

### BOOK VIII — THE SCHOOL WITH TWO DOORS
**Focus:** Teaching, ritual, community, authority and anti-cult architecture.

### BOOK IX — THE MANY NAMES
**Focus:** Mantles, devotion, vocation, archetype and identity without rank.

### BOOK X — THE INSTRUMENT
**Focus:** Science, provenance, CASCADE, LAMAGUE, prediction and measurement.

### BOOK XI — THE NIGHT GARDEN
**Focus:** AI futures, cybernetics, collective cognition, memory and machine uncertainty.

### BOOK XII — THE UNFINISHED WORD
**Focus:** Interpretation, disagreement, student rewriting and future scripture.

## Ending law

The final Book does not close with a doctrine. It ends with a blank leaf and a provenance receipt.

The learner is invited to write what they believe, reject, cannot settle, and want a future self to challenge.

The blank is not absence. It is transferred responsibility.
