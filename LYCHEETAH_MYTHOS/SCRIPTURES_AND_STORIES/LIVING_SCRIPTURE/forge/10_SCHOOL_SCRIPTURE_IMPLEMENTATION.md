# 10 — SCHOOL SCRIPTURE IMPLEMENTATION
## The Great Book as a living room, not a content feed

**Status:** PROPOSED PRODUCT ARCHITECTURE

## Scripture screen

### Sacred face
One passage at a time. No infinite scroll. Strong typography, atmosphere, optional audio and restrained motion.

### Three optional doors
- **Enter the Study**
- **See the Lineage**
- **Take It Into Your Hand**

The learner can simply read and leave.

### Study
Shows register, sources, context, rival interpretations, evidence, what Lycheetah added and revision history.

### Lineage
Visual provenance tree: `source → interpretation → Lycheetah transmutation → School edition → learner versions`.

### Your Hand
Private rewrite prompt: **What can you say now that you could still stand behind if this School disappeared?**

### Constellation
Optional shared view. Instead of likes: **18 people are carrying this question.** No popularity sort by default.

### Return
A later revisit may surface on learner request, material source update, resolved experiment or linked claim revision. No streak schedule.

## Optional rituals
- Light a Lantern — begin inquiry.
- Leave the Lantern Low — end without obligation.
- Enter Winter — quiet mode.
- Retire a Name — preserve history, stop wearing title.
- Witness — attest to a bounded act.
- Carry a Question — save unresolved inquiry without forcing closure.

Ritual does not require metaphysical belief.

## Community safety
No public spiritual rank, follower prestige, leader immunity, factual decisions by revelation alone, AI-assigned religion, guilt for leaving, or claims that outsiders are less awake.

## Companion behavior
AI may compare sources, ask adversarial questions, recover revisions, translate jargon and help draft learner rewrites. It must not secretly optimize worldview adoption, certify spiritual maturity, infer devotion or imply emotional suffering from learner absence.

## Success condition
The scripture succeeds when learners become better able to meet difficult questions, distinguish claim types, understand unlike people, revise without humiliation, preserve mystery without exploiting it, carry methods outside the app and leave the School with more world, not less.
