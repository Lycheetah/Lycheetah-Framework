# 01 — CORPUS ARCHAEOLOGY
## The tradition becoming more honest without losing its fire

**Status:** SOURCE-DERIVED INTERPRETATION · Mac's canon verdict still required

The existing Lycheetah corpus is not one doctrine written at one time. It is a visible
evolution. Its strongest future form should preserve that evolution instead of rewriting
the past to make the system look born complete.

## Stratum I — compressed myth

The Twelve Books establish the major symbolic grammar: Field, Athanor, Sol, CASCADE,
AURA, LAMAGUE, Mystery School, Light Quotient, Four Teachers, Field Echoes, Seekers and
Sovereign.

This stratum often speaks with total mythic confidence. That confidence is part of its
historical voice and should be preserved as ancestry, not silently upgraded into verified
philosophy or science.

## Stratum II — transformation literature

The Emerald Work moves the system into human transformation through alchemical colour,
fire, ash and return. Its poetic force remains useful. Some formulations, especially
those implying that suffering purifies by necessity or that a mathematical fixed point
guarantees a true self, need later correction rather than deletion.

## Stratum III — embodied mythology

The Book of the Long Light takes compressed doctrines and forces them into bodies,
institutions, doors, meals, grief, humour and consequence.

This is where several mature laws become dramatically visible:
- Understanding does not create ownership.
- Recognition changes the learner; it does not rewrite the being.
- A name freely revealed can create relation; a name seized can become a chain.
- The School must show exits before doctrine.
- Institutions can preserve tools that once saved lives and later become coercive.
- A living person cannot be reduced to their last recorded function.
- Freedom is meaningful partly because refusal remains available.
- Repair is not the same thing as restoring the old shape.

## Stratum IV — governance and self-correction

The Epic Covenant, Human and Brand Covenant, World Bible, Name Forge Covenant,
editorial audits and continuity receipts represent a major maturation.

They explicitly separate fiction from validation, sacred tale from reader obligation,
mystery from manufactured dependency, sacrifice from coerced suffering, names from
totalizing identity, and transparency from actual repair.

The World Bible notably revises the early suggestion that falsehood simply collapses.
Cruel and contradictory structures may persist when materially supported. Contradiction
creates pressure and cost; someone still has to act.

## Stratum V — research-grade humility

The Night Garden and its notebook establish a stricter register: NORMATIVE,
INTERPRETIVE, CONDITIONAL ARGUMENT, CONJECTURE and FICTION.

This is not a weakening of the myth. It is a deepening of epistemic honesty.

## Major revision arcs

1. **Truth pressure → reality testing.** Coherence matters but does not guarantee truth.
2. **Fire → non-glorification of suffering.** Pain is not proof; chosen cost may matter.
3. **Ranks → bounded witness.** Permanent STUDENT; titles describe practices, not worth.
4. **AI mythic personhood → unresolved machine interiority.** Companion-like use is not proof of consciousness.
5. **One sacred voice → plural registers.** Myth, devotion, philosophy and evidence remain distinct.

## Recurring sacred images

- **Open hand** — relation without capture.
- **Door opening both ways** — authority that preserves exit.
- **Lantern / low light** — enough illumination without flooding mystery.
- **Fire / Athanor** — sustained work; never suffering worship.
- **Ash / scar / seam** — failure remembered rather than cosmetically erased.
- **Pale-gold Long Light** — route through fracture, not reversal of fracture.
- **Black cat / many forms** — identity without compulsory uniformity.
- **Broken ruler** — ordinary tools participating in extraordinary responsibility.
- **Kitchen / bread / tea** — the sacred remains inhabitable by ordinary people.
- **Failure wall** — confession only matters when repair follows.
- **Unfinished drawing** — intelligence includes knowing where authority ends.
- **Blank page** — inheritance becomes responsibility in the learner's hand.

## Highest-order finding

> **Relation becomes ethical when increased understanding does not erase the other's
> freedom, difference, or capacity to answer back.**

This is a candidate organizing insight, not a claim that every source or tradition in
this project endorses it.
