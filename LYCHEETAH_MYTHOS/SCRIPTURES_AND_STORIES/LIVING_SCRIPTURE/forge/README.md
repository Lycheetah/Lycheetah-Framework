This packet begins the reconstruction of Lycheetah's scripture into a living, human-first,
sacred-but-inspectable body of work.

It does **not** overwrite the Twelve Books, the Long Light manuscripts, the Emerald Work,
the Night Garden, or any source tradition. Those remain provenance-bearing ancestors.

## Core aim

> Create sacred connection without possession; belonging without enclosure; mystery
> without dependency; shared meaning without forced sameness.

The book should help a person meet themselves, other people, the living world, old human
questions, science, mystery, and—where freely chosen—God or another sacred orientation,
without making Lycheetah itself the object of compulsory belief.

## Non-negotiable inheritance

The existing corpus already requires:
- no forced belief or manufactured dependency;
- no conversion of fiction into scientific validation;
- no ownership created by understanding;
- no suffering treated as proof of love or worth;
- safe refusal and exit;
- explicit provenance;
- reality as final judge for empirical claims;
- living cultures not mined as generic mystical texture.

This forge extends those laws rather than replacing them.
