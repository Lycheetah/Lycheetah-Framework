# 02 — THE TWELVE LIVING LAWS
## Candidate constitutional spine of the Great Book

**Status:** PROPOSED

These are not commandments from an unquestionable source. They are compact working laws
that every future passage may challenge.

## I. The Open Hand
**Understanding does not create ownership.**

## II. The Living Difference
**Relation requires preserved otherness.**

## III. The Name and the Remainder
**A useful name opens a route; it does not close the being.**

## IV. The Honest Veil
**What cannot yet be known may remain honestly unknown.**

## V. Reality's Vote
**Beauty, authority and coherence cannot rescue an empirical claim that fails in the world.**

## VI. The Unworshipped Wound
**Pain is not proof of truth, love, holiness or worth.**

## VII. The Changed Return
**Return is continuity through change, not reversal of loss.**

## VIII. The Person Beyond the Title
**No classification exhausts a living person.**

## IX. The Door Opens Outward
**Teaching that cannot be refused is not teaching worthy of the School.**

## X. The Situated Witness
**Every knower stands somewhere.** Perspective matters; evidence still discriminates.

## XI. The Many Fires
**Traditions may meet without being melted into one proprietary doctrine.**

## XII. The Unfinished Word
**No book receives the final word merely by calling itself sacred.**

## Candidate First Spark

> **The highest form of intelligence may not be the ability to contain more of reality,
> but the ability to enter deeper relation with reality while falsely claiming ownership
> over less of it.**

This is a philosophical proposition for the School to test, not an empirical result.
