## Promotion law

Any movement from:
- PROPOSED → CANON
- CANDIDATE → CROWNED
- INTERPRETIVE → EMPIRICAL
- FICTION → REAL-WORLD CLAIM
- CORRESPONDENCE → LINEAGE
- PUBLIC SOURCE → CULTURAL PERMISSION

must be explicit, documented and attributable to the appropriate authority/evidence.
