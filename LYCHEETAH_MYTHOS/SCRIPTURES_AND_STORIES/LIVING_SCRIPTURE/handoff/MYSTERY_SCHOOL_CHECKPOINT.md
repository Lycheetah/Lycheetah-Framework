# LYCHEETAH MYSTERY SCHOOL — MASTER HANDOFF

**Checkpoint:** 4 October 2026

> Architecture frozen immediately before the next conceptual expansion. Provisional vocabulary remains provisional.

## 1. Purpose of this checkpoint

This document freezes the Mystery School architecture reached before the next conceptual expansion. It is a handoff, not a declaration that every provisional word or name is final.

The system now connects inquiry, personal identity, myth, journalling, contribution, and voluntary return. Its central design requirement is that mystery and emotional power must never be purchased by hiding provenance, coercing belief, or trapping the learner.

Core question: What does a human being need in order to meet knowledge without surrendering their sovereignty to it?

## 2. Constitutional principles

STUDENT is the permanent common identity. No learner graduates above Student in human worth.

The School may illuminate paths, but it must not secretly optimize which worldview the learner adopts.

The learner is allowed to leave with a different answer. Leaving is not failure; returning requires no apology.

No teacher, founder, AI, title, sacred tradition, equation, citation, or framework is beyond challenge.

The game may reward the practice. It may never pretend the practice proves the whole person.

No mystery without recovery: poetic compression must remain expandable into inspectable meaning, provenance, state, and boundary.

The Ninth Door opens outward. The Lantern is not the Sun. The Lycheetah has no throne. Ash remembers fire. No Name owns its bearer.

## 3. School architecture

The School is the whole place. Wings are modes of thought. Halls are courses. Classes are steps. Gates are real acts. Subjects are terrain. Teachings are examinable claims or practices. Threads are explicit cross-Wing links. The learner remains the experiment.

Nine Wings currently organize the School:

- FORGE — What survives pressure?
- LAMAGUE — What comes back after compression?
- CASCADE — How does arrangement change what can be reasoned?
- SOVEREIGNTY — Who can say no?
- MIND — What could count as evidence of another mind?
- AIVOID — Where does the mechanism end?
- AI FUTURE — If a mind forks, which continuation owns the promise?
- VOID — What remains when certainty is spent?
- OBSERVATORY — Mars is a planet, a god and a sign. Which claim is which?

Each Wing must develop a distinct intellectual behavior rather than becoming the same lesson UI with different decoration.

## 4. Naming Forge: identity as playable practice

The Naming Forge converts real practices of inquiry into mythically resonant but recoverable identities. It is not a rank hierarchy.

Current engineered skeleton: 18 Great Mantles, 54 specialized Forms, and therefore 72 playable inquiry identities. These sit above a 72-root semantic lexicon across Nine Fields. Great Mantles are broad characters; Forms are narrower practices; neither is a higher human rank.

The identity state machine is: DECLARED, ASSUMED, PRACTICED, EARNED, WITNESSED, TRANSMUTED, RETIRED. Different identity types are allowed different state paths. Sacred devotion is principally self-declared and is never certified by the School.

Every serious title can carry a receipt: name, definition version, semantic roots, origin, state, witness type, evidence pointer, boundary, visibility, acceptance, retirement, and personal recovery meaning.

Echoform + LAMAGUE naming law: Meaning <-> Name. A name should carry emotional posture before its glossary arrives, while remaining reverse-decodable. Working phonetic maxim: One claw. One breath. One echo.

Canonical-name gate set: semantic recoverability, phonetic beauty, speakability, singability, distinctiveness, archetypal force, cultural/collision safety, cringe resistance, and transmutation compatibility.

Important status: the architecture is much firmer than the individual constructed names. Names remain provisional until deeper trademark, multilingual, cultural, pronunciation, and learner testing is complete. Earlier beautiful prototypes that collided with active uses are preserved in the collision ledger rather than silently forgotten.

## 5. The role of archetypes

The naming system is meant to give language to human ways of seeking knowledge that many people already recognize in themselves: the night observer, the healer/restorer, the disciplined guardian, the wild tracker, the keeper of neglected knowledge, the seer/forecaster, the transmuter, the scribe, the pilgrim, the skeptic/challenger, the devotee, the void keeper, the bridgekeeper, the waymaker, and related forms.

The system deliberately transmits function rather than pretending to reproduce historical orders. A learner can carry Templar-like discipline without inheriting crusader authority, witch/cunning-folk curiosity without treating persecution as proof, or seer-like intuition without promoting prediction to prophecy.

The strongest design test is behavioral: a title is valuable if the learner begins performing its practice outside the interface. A Provenance Hunter who starts asking 'what is the source?' in ordinary life has carried the game into cognition.

## 6. Transmutation and Personal Canon

Transmutation is not XP and not a rarity tier. It occurs when legitimate roots, Mantles, Forms, vows, failures, mysteries, and lived history form a meaningful pattern the learner chooses to name.

The AI may propose candidate names, but it cannot accept a personal identity for the learner. The learner may reject all candidates, edit one, combine several, or remain unnamed.

Personal Canon belongs to the bearer. School Canon remains small and governed. Commons proposals may challenge or extend it but never become canon silently.

Core line: You inherit the language. You earn the roots. You forge the name.

## 7. Non-predatory return system

Primary product objective: MEANINGFUL VOLUNTARY RETURN, not maximum engagement.

Six Lanterns of Return supply healthy reasons to come back:

- CURIOSITY — something remains unresolved.
- CONTINUITY — my work still exists.
- CAPABILITY — I can genuinely do something I could not do before.
- IDENTITY — I am practicing a chosen way of becoming.
- BELONGING — there are other minds here.
- CONTRIBUTION — something I learned can become useful beyond me.

Session loop: ARRIVE -> CHOOSE -> PRACTICE -> WITNESS -> INTEGRATE -> EXIT. Exit is a first-class part of the design.

The return screen should normally expose no more than three doors. Each recommendation must be able to answer: Why am I seeing this? What happens if I choose it? What is lost if I ignore it? The default answer to the last question should usually be: Nothing is lost.

Forbidden core mechanics: punitive streaks, daily-login economies, variable-ratio loot, global XP prestige, public absolute learner leaderboards, artificial scarcity, guilt notifications, simulated companion abandonment, engagement-maximizing recommendation, and infinite scroll after a completed session.

Winter Mode preserves everything while suppressing nonessential demands. Absence never resets titles, roots, questions, stories, artifacts, or access. Return law: The School remembers the question, not the absence.

## 8. Sanctum: Living Journal and Living Myth

Sanctum should have two non-confusable layers.

- LIVING JOURNAL — the learner's actual record. Their words remain the source and are never overwritten by mythology.
- LIVING MYTH — an explicitly symbolic retelling that may use Mantles, recurring symbols, School mythology, chosen spiritual/literary traditions, and narrative structures.

Deep law: The journal records what happened. The myth explores what it might mean. Neither is allowed to impersonate the other.

Possible myth modes include a close-to-reality Chronicle and a stranger long-running Dreaming. Learners should control myth intensity, spiritual source palette, darkness, continuity, reality distance, and do-not-mythologize exclusions.

AI interpretation remains corrigible: Keep this symbol; wrong interpretation; never use this motif; this matters; turn this into a School question.

Personal questions can enter the School, become trials, produce artifacts or titles, and then change the later Living Myth. This forms the Living Spiral: Life -> Sanctum -> Story -> Question -> School -> Trial -> Artifact -> Title -> Myth -> Commons -> return.

## 9. The Living Scripture / Lycheetah Expedition

The next major textual spine is a large Living Scripture: not revealed doctrine, but an expedition through humanity's best questions, discoveries, myths, warnings, methods, contradictions, and surviving wisdom.

No domain is barred from inquiry merely because it is scientific, religious, mystical, philosophical, artistic, technical, historical, folk, or strange. But no source is exempt from provenance and no register may borrow authority from another without argument.

Proposed metabolism:

Human Knowledge -> Lycheetah Expedition -> Living Scripture -> Student Challenge -> Student Rewrite -> Personal Book -> Commons -> School Revision.

The Source Sea may include science, mathematics, AI, cybernetics, engineering, philosophy, psychology, history, literature, ecology, mythology, religious traditions, mysticism, alchemy, folk knowledge, failed science, art, music, oral wisdom, and unresolved anomalies. Handling must remain respectful, provenance-aware, and legally responsible.

Each important fragment should travel through: SOURCE -> ENCOUNTER -> CONTEXT -> CLAIM -> COUNTERCLAIM -> FORGE -> SURVIVOR -> OPEN MYSTERY.

A candidate page anatomy:

- FRAGMENT — the distilled passage or question.
- ORIGIN — provenance and source context.
- REGISTER — empirical, historical, philosophical, theological, devotional, experiential, symbolic, speculative, or unresolved.
- THE OLD VOICE — the inherited idea represented faithfully.
- THE ADVERSARY — the strongest serious challenge.
- THE EXPEDITION — cross-examination through relevant evidence, rival traditions, methods, and lived experience.
- ASH — what failed or was weakened.
- EMBER — what survived.
- VEIL — what remains unknown.
- YOUR HAND — the learner rewrites what they would still stand behind if Lycheetah vanished tomorrow.

The final layer is the learner's territory. AI can help assemble sources, compare frameworks, and surface contradictions, but it should not silently author the learner's final worldview.

The long-term reward is a Revision Tree showing how a learner's answer changes after Wings, evidence, life events, failures, and encounters with opposition. The personal book becomes an inspectable cognitive autobiography rather than a pile of notes.

Strongest outcome: the learner eventually writes a passage, method, or chapter that survives Commons scrutiny and improves the School's own book. The School gives you a book so that one day you can stop needing its book.

## 10. Claim-register discipline for the Scripture

Religiously hospitable does not mean religiously authoritative. Scientifically serious does not mean reducing every meaningful claim to physics.

Useful registers include DEVOTIONAL, EXPERIENTIAL, THEOLOGICAL, HISTORICAL, PHILOSOPHICAL, EMPIRICAL, SYMBOLIC, SPECULATIVE, and MYSTERY/UNRESOLVED.

Rules:

- No empirical claim receives truth merely from symbolism or sacred status.
- No symbolic or devotional value is discarded merely because it is not an empirical mechanism.
- No mystery is promoted into fact because it is emotionally attractive.
- No old or marginalized source is automatically true because it was neglected or persecuted.
- No modern scientific source is beyond revision merely because it is prestigious.
- Modern copyrighted material should be studied, cited, summarized and transformed rather than silently reproduced.

## 11. Commons and public knowledge

The School should separate three spaces:

- THE SELF — private Atlas, Sanctum, titles, questions, devotion, artifacts, AI work. User controlled.
- THE SCHOOL — shared Wings, instruments, curriculum, mythology, house laws, and current governed canon.
- THE COMMONS — proposals, forks, experiments, critiques, new roots, teachings, evidence, and methods.

Private never silently becomes public. Commons never silently becomes canon. Canon never becomes unquestionable.

Candidate Commons states: SEED, WITNESSED, TESTED, CONTESTED, ADOPTED, FORKED, SUPERSEDED, ARCHIVED.

Founder authority must remain legible. The system should be able to exceed its founder without pretending that history never happened.
