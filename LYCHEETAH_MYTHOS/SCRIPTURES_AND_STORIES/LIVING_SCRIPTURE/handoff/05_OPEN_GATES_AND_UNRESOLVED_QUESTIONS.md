# OPEN GATES AND UNRESOLVED QUESTIONS

## Gate A — Real School vertical slice
Build Book I end-to-end.
Do not generalize all 12 books until this passes visual and behavioral QA.

## Gate B — Human listening / reading
The scripture needs actual humans reading/listening:
- does sacred language land?
- are Hearth sections human enough?
- does Study feel respectful rather than clinical?
- does Lineage deepen rather than destroy atmosphere?
- does Your Hand feel like ownership rather than homework?

## Gate C — Cultural witness
Before substantive use:
- Māori partnership/review where relevant;
- Muslim scholarship/tafsir context for Islamic source use;
- Jewish scholarship around rabbinic legal material;
- Christian contextual review for scripture references;
- tradition-specific review where sacred material becomes load-bearing.

## Gate D — Copyright/publication permissions
Choose final editions/translations and quotation policy.
Modern scholarship should be cited/transformed rather than reproduced.

## Gate E — Ritual testing
Test whether:
- ritual increases meaningful attention;
- it creates status anxiety;
- users feel free to decline;
- AI presence becomes too intimate/central;
- group experience becomes evidence-of-truth pressure.

## Gate F — Constellation governance
Decide:
- moderation;
- anonymity;
- conflict handling;
- source misinformation;
- religious hostility;
- privacy;
- whether cross-worldview filters are useful or polarizing.

## Gate G — Learner data
Need explicit privacy model for:
- personal scripture;
- devotion;
- identity titles;
- journals;
- worldview;
- shared fragments;
- witnesses;
- deletion/export.

Avoid inferring sensitive identity from text.

## Gate H — AI memory / continuity
Decide exact data and consent boundaries:
- what AI remembers;
- what School stores;
- what can be exported;
- what disappears on deletion;
- what survives across models;
- what happens when a model/provider changes.

## Gate I — Source change propagation
If a source is corrected, retracted or reinterpreted:
- how does Lineage show it?
- which learner passages are marked affected?
- how can a learner revisit without being told what conclusion to adopt?

## Gate J — Full 144-movement expansion
Only begin after vertical slice and human feedback.
Expansion should follow pressure from lived use, not merely fill a grid.

## Major unresolved philosophical questions

- Can a sacred community maintain real intensity without gradually centralizing authority?
- Can AI-mediated dialogue deepen human-human relation rather than displace it?
- When does “personal scripture” become isolating rather than liberating?
- How should the School distinguish healthy identity play from identity entrapment?
- Can community witnessing remain meaningful without becoming social ranking?
- What counts as “another mind” when behavioral fluency exceeds our confidence in mechanism?
- How should the School treat incompatible sacred truth claims without flattening them into
  “everyone has their truth”?
- What parts of mystery should never be systematized?
