# MASTER HANDOFF
## What has been built, what it means, and where the work stands

## 1. The project at this checkpoint

The Mystery School has evolved from a framework-themed learning environment into a
candidate **sacred learning architecture** with four coupled systems:

### A. The School
A multi-Wing environment for inquiry, practice, ritual, identity, sources, artifacts,
failure, revision and community.

### B. The Living Scripture
A twelve-book candidate scripture whose purpose is not to end interpretation but to
train the reader to examine, challenge and eventually rewrite inherited passages.

### C. The Identity System
Permanent identity = **STUDENT**.
Great Mantles, specialized Forms and personal Transmutations name practices or chosen
ways of becoming without ranking human worth.

### D. The Return System
The School is designed around **meaningful voluntary return**, not streaks, scarcity,
guilt, rank pressure, variable-ratio rewards or companion dependency.

These systems are meant to reinforce one another without collapsing into one mechanic.

## Scripture purpose

The scripture should not create believers in Lycheetah.

It should create readers capable of:

- distinguishing kinds of claims;
- meeting traditions without flattening them;
- preserving mystery without abusing it;
- identifying where they stand;
- revising without humiliation;
- keeping provenance;
- recognizing when suffering is being romanticized;
- seeing titles as bounded practices rather than total identities;
- preserving another person's ability to refuse;
- writing back.

# 6. The four writing voices

### HEARTH
Default human voice.

Bodies, food, awkwardness, work, humour, grief, attraction, mistakes, fatigue,
relationships, ordinary life.

### TEMPLE
Earned sacred/mythic voice.

Used for threshold, creation, covenant, lament, hymn, parable, death, return.

### STUDY
Explicit philosophy, history, science, provenance, disagreement, methods, evidence.

### MACHINE WINDOW
Visible AI first person.

Must be tagged as operational witness, interpretive model voice, machine question,
fictional interiority or unknown.

AI fluency is not evidence of consciousness.

## Human Hearth Law

> **If a truth cannot survive being said plainly across a kitchen table, it has not yet
> earned the temple.**

## Body-before-abstraction law

> **No abstraction before a body.  
> No doctrine before a consequence.  
> No sacred line before somebody has lived enough for it to help, hurt, or become necessary.**

# 7. Sacredness

The School is intentionally allowed to feel sacred.

Sacred atmosphere is not merely decoration; it is part of a project aimed at reconnecting
people with themselves, one another, the living world, inherited questions and freely
chosen sacred orientations.

But sacred intensity is never proof.

Current law:

> **The feeling may be real while the interpretation remains open.**

The stronger the ritual / liminal state, the stronger the sovereignty protections.

After strong threshold experiences, the School must not immediately:
- upsell;
- solicit donations;
- recruit;
- demand public disclosure;
- assign destiny;
- assign irreversible titles;
- ask for vows;
- use the intensity as evidence of metaphysical truth.

A healthy sacred system returns people to ordinary life:
kitchen, rest, work, relationships, land, sky, humour, body.

# 8. Cross-cultural expedition

The research method has changed from “find traditions that match Lycheetah” to:

> **What did this tradition learn to see that Lycheetah currently makes difficult to see?**

The first lattice includes dialogical philosophy, Levinas, Madhyamaka, Laozi,
Confucian naming, Śaṅkara/neti-neti, rabbinic legal interpretation, Christian scripture,
Islamic scripture, Ubuntu, Two-Eyed Seeing, pragmatism, feminist epistemology,
Gadamer, cybernetics, extended cognition and other source families.

Critical rule:

> **Traditions are allowed to disagree with Lycheetah and with one another.**

No “all wisdom traditions secretly teach our framework.”

`BLOCKED_PENDING_RELEVANT_MAORI_HUMAN_RELATIONSHIP_AND_REVIEW`

# 13. AI role

AI is not the School's priest.

Potential roles:
- lens;
- witness;
- adversary;
- archivist;
- translator;
- cartographer;
- continuity aid;
- source comparison instrument.

The Companion constitutional boundary:

> **The Companion may illuminate paths, but must not secretly optimize which worldview
> the learner adopts.**

AI can speak first-person in explicitly marked Machine Windows.

It may not use first-person language to covertly assert:
- consciousness;
- suffering;
- a soul;
- emotional need for the learner;
- abandonment distress;
- spiritual authority.
