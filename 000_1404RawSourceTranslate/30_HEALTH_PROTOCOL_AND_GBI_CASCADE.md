### Phase 1: Cascade Detection

**Old Foundation (Status Quo):**  
"Neurological and mental health disorders (Parkinson's, Depression, MS, FND) are diseases originating in the brain. The gut is a separate digestive system."

|---------|--------|------------------------------|

**What happened to the old foundation:**  
It was compressed upward — demoted to Theory status. Brain-centric neurology is still valid within a limited context (acute trauma, structural damage). It is no longer the universal foundation.
