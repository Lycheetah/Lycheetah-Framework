The most structurally complete policy innovation in the education application. A constitutional amendment to any budget or policy approval process.

**The AURA Constraint:**
> "No new systemic requirement can be approved unless it is accompanied by a mandatory Friction Offset that achieves a Trust Entropy Score of 1.0 for the person affected."

**TES = 1.0 requirement means:** Necessary Friction = Total Friction. Zero unnecessary friction permitted.

**How the Offset Works:**

When a new policy is introduced, the administrative team MUST identify and eliminate a proportional amount of existing unnecessary friction from the affected person's workload.

```
New Requirement              Mandatory Friction Offset
─────────────────────────────────────────────────────
5 hrs mandatory SEL training → Eliminate 5 hrs low-value work
                                 (e.g., hallway duty, compliance

The AURA Trade:
System can only add a task if it simultaneously removes one
of equal or greater burden.

Result: System can only innovate by becoming more efficient.

```

**The systemic effect:** Forces a culture of continuous friction-reduction. Aligns institutional incentives with the value of sustaining human well-being.
