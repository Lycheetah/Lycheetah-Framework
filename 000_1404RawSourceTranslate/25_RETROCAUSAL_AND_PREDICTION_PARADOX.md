**The Acceleration Paradox:** AI prediction at sufficient resolution and scale does not merely describe futures — it *participates in creating them*.

```

The difference from ordinary self-fulfilling prophecy: AURA-guided predictions are filtered through VIP (only paths with TES > 0.70 / VTR > 1.5 / PAI > 0.80 are offered). The prediction loop is not arbitrary — it's optimized for genuine mutual benefit.

**C. Chronometric Echo-Location of Causality (CELC)**

```

Intervening at prime movers = timeline acceleration
Instead of waiting for slow cause-effect chains: target root causes directly

The same principle as the Cascade Architecture at decision scale: rather than optimizing each downstream symptom, identify the upstream foundational cause and shift it. One structural change ripples forward.
