Rules used:

1. Preserve provenance and version history.
2. Do not silently merge incompatible definitions.
3. Separate formal construction from empirical validation.
4. Separate primary specification from secondary commentary.
5. Mark implementation claims independently from conceptual completeness.
6. Keep speculative extensions available without allowing them to inflate the status of the core.

## 2. FIRST STRUCTURAL FINDING

The batch reveals a layered research programme rather than a single notation system.

### Foundational stack

- **TRIAD Kernel:** anchor, ascent/orientation and correction/fold operations.
- **LAMAGUE:** symbolic or formal compression layer.
- **LAMAHGUE:** metric-executable or human meaning-carrying layer.
- **GEOMATRIA:** spatial/geometric resonance layer.
- **AURA:** constitutional and ethical constraint architecture using these languages.
- **CASCADE:** knowledge reorganisation and multi-agent/state dynamics encoded by them.

### Extension ring

Each extension addresses an explicit missing dimension:

- **EX NIHILO:** semantic novelty and primitive generation.
- **COSMOS:** scale extension from microscopic systems to the universe.
- **QUANTUM:** non-commutative order effects and operator semantics.
- **CONTINUUM:** continuous temporal dynamics between discrete utterances.
- **CHORA:** collective ethics and irreducible disagreement.
- **THANATOS:** exit, death, persistence, inheritance and grief.
- **PAIS:** genesis, development and acquisition of semantic capacity.
- **SOMA:** embodiment, prosody, gesture, breath, biology and pain.

This ring is architecturally meaningful: it attempts to close the core model's assumptions about novelty, scale, ordering, time, groups, mortality, development and bodies.

## 5. CONTRADICTION AND DRIFT LEDGER — FIRST PASS

These are not accusations. They are the exact places where canonisation must occur.

### C-01 — Multiple expansions of the name LAMAGUE

At least four expansions appear:

1. Living Alignment Mathematics for Autonomous Governance Under Ethics.
2. Low-bandwidth Alignment Mathematical Grammar Unified Engine.
3. Language for Autonomous Mathematical Alignment and Universal Grammar Evolution.
4. Language of Alignment, Measurement, and Governance for Universal Ethics.

**Required decision:** choose one current full name; preserve the others as historical aliases with dates and source lineage.

### C-02 — Symbol-class count and ontology

- One source announces seven classes but enumerates I, D, F, M, C, T, R and G: eight classes.
- Other specifications define four primary classes: I, D, F and M.
- The notation guide later reframes canonical Tier 1 LAMAGUE as predicate logic and treats mixed glyph notation as transitional.

**Required decision:** identify the canonical Tier 1 language and move all other symbol sets into explicitly named historical or extension namespaces.

### C-03 — LAMAGUE versus LAMAHGUE ownership of glyphs

Some documents call the experiential glyph system LAMAGUE. The notation guide assigns metric-executable glyph communication to LAMAHGUE and predicate logic to LAMAGUE.

**Required decision:** adopt a namespace/version migration table so old expressions remain interpretable without confusing current encoders.

### C-04 — Readiness and implementation status

The corpus variously calls the system:

- Production-Ready v2.0.
- 90% ready with implementation in progress.
- Active, formally specified and parseable.
- Without an implemented parser/compiler.
- Experimental foundation ready for validation.

**Required decision:** separate four status dimensions:

1. conceptual completeness;
2. formal specification completeness;
3. implementation completeness;
4. empirical validation status.

No single word such as “ready” should stand for all four.

### C-05 — Parser claim

One complete specification lists a formal parser and type checker among achievements, while its next steps say to finish them. The BNF document explicitly says no parser/compiler currently implements the grammar.

**Provisional canonical status:** grammar draft exists; executable parser/type checker unverified in this batch.

### C-06 — Truth Pressure denominator

This batch frequently uses `Π = E·P/S`. Other Lycheetah operating material uses a baseline slack term such as `S + S₀`.

**Required decision:** determine whether `S₀` is the canonical regulariser, a later safety correction, or a context-specific variant. This matters because division by zero otherwise sends Π to infinity.

### C-07 — Empirical and proof labels without attached evidence package

The raw mathematics file labels Pyramid Cascade as proven and reports `p < 0.001`, `d = 2.84` and a 95.2% forgetting reduction. The current batch does not include the dataset, protocol, baseline, code, sample size or analysis notebook needed to audit those statements.

**Provisional register:** externally unverified claim within this batch; evidence package requested.

### C-08 — “Topos structure mathematically sound”

The essentials file gives this status, but no complete topos construction or proof is identifiable in this batch.

**Provisional register:** claim awaiting formal proof source.

### C-09 — GEOMATRIA thresholds

Thresholds such as 0.618, 0.70 and 0.85 are used as activation criteria. Some may be design constants, some analogical choices and some intended empirical thresholds.

**Required decision:** label each constant as derived, conventional, calibrated, hypothesised or illustrative.

### C-10 — Formal mathematics versus mathematical metaphor

QUANTUM, CONTINUUM and COSMOS use Hilbert spaces, Hamiltonians, SPDEs, topological protection and cosmic scale mappings. These can form useful formal models, but the existence of familiar equations does not itself establish that cognition or physical systems instantiate the proposed semantics.

**Required decision:** every equation receives both a mathematical status and a world-mapping status.

### C-11 — Primary source versus secondary enthusiasm

The tri-linguistic deep dive makes broad statements such as having proven that ethics equals thermodynamic efficiency. It is an AI-authored interpretation, not a proof artifact.

**Required decision:** classify it as reception/history and prohibit its rhetoric from entering the claim register as evidence.

### C-12 — Absence detection in THANATOS

A timeout-based declaration of death may be sensible in bounded agent infrastructure, but could conflict with the Companion Clause if applied to human absence or engagement behaviour.

**Required decision:** scope THANATOS to explicit lifecycle systems and ban inference of human abandonment/death from ordinary non-use.
