LAMAGUE Native-36

Experimental semantic script: ten fused native numerals and twenty-six native operation glyphs. ASCII is the debug alias. The native glyph is the body. Each mark is generated from semantic radicals rather than substituted for an ordinary letter.

Research boundary: these glyphs are independently generated, but global visual uniqueness is not yet claimed. The private-use identifiers are experimental mappings, not Unicode assignments.

What this prototype proves—and does not

It proves: the thirty-six-token codebook can be rendered as a coherent independent vector script, mapped to stable semantic IDs, transcoded from ASCII, labelled accessibly, and measured for visual separation.

It does not yet prove: semantic compression superiority, learnability, global originality, cross-model decoding, or that all twenty-six operations deserve primitive status. Those are the experiments.
