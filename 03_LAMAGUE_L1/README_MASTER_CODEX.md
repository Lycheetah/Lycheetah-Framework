## ⚠ TWO LINES SHARE THE NUMBERS v0.1 / v0.2 / v0.3

Added 2026-08-01, and it is the easiest thing in this corpus to misread:

| line | what it is | folders |
|---|---|---|
| **CORE** | the **language** — algebra, ontology, operator contracts | `12_` |
| **RUNTIME** | the **experiment harness** — does meaning survive crossing intelligences? | `05_` `06_` `07_` |

They are **not versions of each other**. CORE v0.3 and RUNTIME v0.3 share a number
and nothing else. Saying "LAMAGUE v0.3" without naming the line does not mean
anything. The numbers are stamped inside the release zips, MANIFESTs, schemas and
test suites, so neither line is renumbered to resolve this — the ambiguity lives
in the name, and `12_CORE_LANGUAGE_LINE/README.md` is where it is resolved.

The authoritative line to continue is **CORE**, toward v0.4.

Known duplicate copies carrying `(1)` or `(2)` suffixes were not included.
