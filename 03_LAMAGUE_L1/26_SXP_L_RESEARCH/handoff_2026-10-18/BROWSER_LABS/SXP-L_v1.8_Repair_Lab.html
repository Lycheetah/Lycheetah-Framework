Language, not keyboard notation

kelunaro = “Please clarify an earlier statement.” A nested proposition is a proposed interpretation, not an assertion. Inside it we use explicit names rather than ambiguous pronouns.

remakivo = “I correct an earlier statement of my own.” It adds a replacement; it never deletes history.

taba identifies turn one, inside a gonape ... dimotu reference frame. bakumo ... tesavo surrounds the proposition.
