## Critical research maturity statement
Tests show only bounded software behaviour. They do not prove fluent speech, superior AI capability, human comprehension, trustworthy permission grants, or an empirically complete human language. v2.0 has **zero independent human participants**, **zero listening studies**, and **no independent replication** of v1.8–v2.0 claims. The earlier external audit identifies genuine v0.1–v1.0 blockers; do not treat later passing tests as having repaired all of them.
