# Read the same SXP-L words from two perspectives

**Original:** Maka to Nara: `mi liva ti` — I love you.

**Nara's clarification:** `soraviko mi luma ti` — Is Maka saying that Maka misses Nara? The inner I/you follow **Maka's original viewpoint**.

**Nara's alternative clarification:** `tumerako mi luma ti` — Is Maka saying that Nara misses Maka? The inner I/you follow **Nara's current viewpoint**.

Both are *questions/proposals*, not assertions. In full syntax, the question includes `mi kelunaro`, a turn ID, scope markers and `devora` terminal. To avoid changing perspective in the middle of a conversation, never omit the frame word when you mean to shift perspective.
