# Empirical programme — designs and open measurements

**Guide updated:** 19 October 2026. This area owns study designs and
preregistration drafts. A filename containing `PREREG` does not establish that a
protocol was externally registered, executed or independently replicated.

| Document | Proposed work |
|---|---|
| [Empirical programme](E1_EMPIRICAL_PROGRAM.md) | Overall programme; its header explicitly says design, not yet preregistered or executed. |
| [E1.1 plan](E1.1_PREREGISTRATION_PLAN.md) | Initial preregistration planning and proposed measurements. |
| [K1/K4 calibration](E1A_K1K4_CALIBRATION_PREREG.md) | Calibration protocol for model parameters. |
| [AURA behaviour](E1D_AURA_BEHAVIOUR_PREREG.md) | Behavioural evaluation design. |
| [Hexie](E1F_HEXIE_PREREGISTRATION.md) | Proposed Hexie evaluation. |
| [Multi-operator](E1G_MULTI_OPERATOR_PREREGISTRATION.md) | Proposed cross-operator study. |
| [Master equation](E1H_MASTER_EQUATION_CALIBRATION.md) | Calibration and identifiability design. |

These seven documents are not seven completed experiments. Execution claims need
a fixed protocol/version, registration receipt where claimed, inputs, exclusions,
analysis, results and failures. Preserve the original protocol when recording a
change so a later result cannot silently redefine its own success criterion.

The [capability guide](../33_APPLICATIONS/README.md) routes actual dated text
evaluations and their limitations. The [empirical inventory](../29_GOVERNANCE/EMPIRICAL_INVENTORY.md)
is a separate source with its own date and scope; [research packets](../papers/README.md)
retain their own local experiments. The [current synthesis](../13_SYNTHESIS_REPORTS/CURRENT_FRAMEWORK_SYNTHESIS.md)
connects these lines without treating planned measurements as results.
