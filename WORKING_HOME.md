## Using this corpus with AI

Treat the corpus as a versioned retrieval layer. It does not alter model weights or guarantee automatic memory. Preserve the relationship between each claim, source, evidence, implementation, test and known failure. Generated summaries are derived views and do not silently amend canonical claims.

Read the [extraction protocol](26_FOR_AI/AI_EXTRACTION_PROTOCOL.md), [claim register](28_DEFENSE/CLAIMS.json), [defense index](28_DEFENSE/DEFENSE_INDEX.json), [empirical inventory](29_GOVERNANCE/EMPIRICAL_INVENTORY.md), [falsification register](28_DEFENSE/FALSIFICATION_REGISTER.md), and [scope boundary](28_DEFENSE/SCOPE_BOUNDARY.md). Distinguish internal checks from external replication and preserve disagreements. Private seat state, credentials and customer information do not belong in this public corpus.
