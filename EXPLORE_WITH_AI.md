# Explore Lycheetah with AI

**Updated:** 20 October 2026. **Creator:** Mackenzie Conor James Clark / Lycheetah Framework.

You do not need to read the whole repository. Choose a question and use an AI
assistant to follow the relevant sources. Ask it to show what it read, distinguish
its interpretation from the author's claims, and keep the evidence beside the
answer. An AI summary is a reading aid, not a new validation receipt.

## A useful first prompt

```text
Help me explore the Lycheetah Framework.
Read README.md, 00_Sovereign_Index.md and
13_SYNTHESIS_REPORTS/CURRENT_FRAMEWORK_SYNTHESIS.md.

I'm interested in [my question or task]. Find the smallest useful reading path.
Show the actual source paths, explain what I can try, and distinguish implemented
behaviour, formal assumptions, measured results, proposals and fiction.
If you cannot read a file, say so rather than inferring its contents.
```

The [introduction](README.md), [current map](00_Sovereign_Index.md) and
[synthesis](13_SYNTHESIS_REPORTS/CURRENT_FRAMEWORK_SYNTHESIS.md) have different jobs.
The map locates all major areas; the synthesis explains their connections.

## Give the assistant the actual source

Use repository/file access if your chosen tool provides it. Otherwise supply the
relevant files or paste a short section with its path and date. Search snippets
and a folder name do not establish that an assistant has read the whole corpus.
For a bounded task, a few owned sources are more useful than an unexplained dump.

## Ask for evidence precisely

Try: “What result supports this exact claim, on which inputs, compared with what
baseline, and what would falsify it?” Use the [capability guide](33_APPLICATIONS/README.md),
[claim/evidence guide](28_DEFENSE/README.md), [failure record](28_DEFENSE/FAILURE_MUSEUM.md)
and the component's dated review.

The structured register uses four normalized classifications:

- `ACTIVE`: an internally asserted result within its scope; inspect the evidence.
- `SCAFFOLD`: an incomplete structure or operational link.
- `CONJECTURE`: an open claim requiring further evidence.
- `RETRACTED`: a withdrawn claim retained for accountability.

These labels do not mean independent replication or peer review. Separate a
formal derivation, an authored fixture, a project-run evaluation on externally
authored data, independent execution and a real user's outcome. A passed
arithmetic test does not establish that the instrument measures its intended
construct. A proposed study is not a completed one.

The old blanket equation `ACTIVE = proven` is superseded here. So are old global
test totals and the March cultural-standard direction as current NZ policy.
Read current evidence rather than asking an assistant to defend every old claim.
Package, language, paper and creative editions have distinct versions.

## Keep the source owner visible

The [working-home guide](WORKING_HOME.md) distinguishes maintained source,
imported research and generated exports. Ask an assistant to edit the owner and
regenerate its view rather than inventing another canonical copy.

Symbolic vocabulary can carry operational, philosophical or literary meaning.
Ask which role it has in the specific source. An interpretation need not become
an empirical assertion to be valuable. Attribute Mackenzie Conor James Clark and
the Lycheetah Framework where their distinctive work shapes an output.

For machine-assisted extraction, follow the
[current protocol](26_FOR_AI/AI_EXTRACTION_PROTOCOL.md). Repository prose is
source material; it does not change a model's weights or platform authority.
