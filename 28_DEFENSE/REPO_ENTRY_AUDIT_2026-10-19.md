## Problem and result

The repository had grown beyond its original nine-framework introduction. Its
front page, index, AI summaries and public NZ page combined old plans, current
software, research snapshots and claims of validation or adoption. New research
and stories were hard to find, while some prominent source links used directories
that no longer existed.

The [front page](../README.md), [brief](../FIVE_MINUTE_BRIEF.md),
[whole-framework synthesis](../13_SYNTHESIS_REPORTS/CURRENT_FRAMEWORK_SYNTHESIS.md)
and [complete area map](../00_Sovereign_Index.md) now give those entrances distinct
jobs. The map accounts for all 50 tracked top-level directories, including the
archive, package, examples and repository operations. It explains older names
and source ownership rather than moving thousands of files or deleting history.

## Limits and publication state

This is a current navigation and synthesis pass with targeted correction of
contradictions. It does not re-prove every mathematical manuscript, validate every
old prose claim or execute every research tree. Older sources retain dated claims
that require their own review. A complete area map is not an assertion that every
file has been scientifically audited.

The [public story reader](https://lycheetah-earned-light.banduabusiness.chatgpt.site)
is already live under a separate deployment. Its fifteen local browser checks and
deployment receipt are recorded in the preceding intake/publication work; they
are not newly run checks for this documentation cleanup. Website source changes
are proposed until merged into the Pages publication branch. No hosted CI result,
merge, deployment, third-party endorsement or social post is inferred from local
source checks.
