# Known Limitations — v0.3

- All aggregation weights remain authored and uncalibrated.
- The development corpus influenced the implementation and is not held-out validation.
- Text triage remains English-oriented and pattern-based.
- Source locators are detected but not fetched or verified.
- Sentence-level scoping does not provide full semantic coreference or contradiction reasoning.
- Judge confidence is a model self-report unless separately calibrated.
- Review thresholds remain caller-supplied and unvalidated.
- Onion mappings are adapter assumptions, not consequences of the canonical scalar.
- No claim of universal truth measurement, objective truth probability, or universal threshold is made.
