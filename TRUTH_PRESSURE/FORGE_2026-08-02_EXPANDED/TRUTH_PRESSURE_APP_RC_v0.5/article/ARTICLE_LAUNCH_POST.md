We built a Truth Pressure engine, then stopped ourselves from posting the victory story.

The first real audit showed the formula was not the main problem. The software was rewarding its own vocabulary more reliably than evidence.

So we preserved the failure, separated evidence from certainty, separated tension from how well tension is handled, made review sovereign and reversible, and built the next version for app shadow testing rather than automatic replacement.

The article is about what failed, what changed, and what still has not been earned.

Truth Pressure is not a truth detector.

It is becoming an inspectable instrument for asking when a knowledge structure deserves review.
