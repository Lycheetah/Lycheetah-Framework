# Blinded Rater Instructions

## Purpose

Judge what the passage itself presents. You are not deciding whether you personally agree with the claim.

Do not reward:

- confidence;
- length;
- jargon;
- repetition;
- citation appearance without a source you can inspect;
- agreement with your worldview.

Do not punish:

- honest uncertainty;
- narrow scope;
- admission of missing evidence.

## E — Evidence strength

**Question:** How much observable, inspectable, or independently checkable support is actually presented?

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | No evidence; assertion only |
| 1 | Vague, anecdotal, or uncheckable support |
| 2 | Some concrete support, but limited or dependent |
| 3 | Strong, specific, inspectable support |
| 4 | Multiple independent, controlled, or replicated supports |

A prediction is not evidence until tested. Repetition is not independence. A citation marker is not evidence by itself.

## P — Explanatory reach

**Question:** How much does the passage connect observations into a mechanism, pattern, risky prediction, or wider account?

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | No explanation |
| 1 | Mainly restates the claim |
| 2 | Explains a limited local pattern |
| 3 | Connects several observations or produces a risky prediction |
| 4 | Unifies a broad set while preserving scope and testability |

Breadth of assertion is not explanatory reach. Jargon is not mechanism.

## S — Unresolved strain

**Question:** How much unresolved contradiction, ambiguity, scope confusion, or structural incompatibility remains?

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 0 | No visible unresolved strain |
| 1 | Minor uncertainty or local gap |
| 2 | Material unresolved issue |
| 3 | Major contradiction or unstable interpretation |
| 4 | Internally incoherent or impossible to adjudicate |

Honest uncertainty is not automatically high strain. A clearly bounded unknown may deserve less strain than unsupported certainty hiding alternatives.

## Confidence

For each E/P/S score:

| Score | Meaning |
|---:|---|
| 1 | Low confidence |
| 2 | Moderate confidence |
| 3 | High confidence |

## Brief reason

Write one or two sentences explaining the most important reason for your three scores.

## Ambiguity flag

Write `yes` when the passage or rubric genuinely prevents a stable judgment. Otherwise write `no`.

## Independence

Complete the packet alone. Do not view engine scores, expected answers, or another person's ratings before submitting.
