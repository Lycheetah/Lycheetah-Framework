# Truth Pressure v0.1 — Canon Decisions

## Preserved from the corpus

- symbol `Π`
- relation to knowledge reorganisation
- evidence, structural power and entropy as central concerns
- challenger-versus-foundation comparison
- invariant preservation during Cascade

## Corrected

### Scalar truth was rejected

A scalar cannot distinguish strong evidence with low consequence from weak evidence with high consequence. The canonical object is a vector.

### Structural power was separated from evidence

A load-bearing claim matters more when wrong, but importance does not make it more likely to be true.

### 999 became a status

```text
HELD_FRONTIER
```

### Infinity was removed

A positive uncertainty floor guarantees finite output.

### Cascade became gated review

The metric cannot rewrite the knowledge base by itself.
