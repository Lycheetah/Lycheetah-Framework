## Immutable v0.1 boundaries

1. Truth Pressure is revision pressure, not truth probability.
2. The canonical object is vector-valued.
3. Structural impact never substitutes for evidence.
4. Conviction is not an input.
5. `999` is replaced by `HELD_FRONTIER`.
6. Infinity is impossible.
7. Cascade requires foundation comparison.
8. A candidate opens review; it does not rewrite automatically.
9. Defaults are synthetic and must be calibrated.
10. LAMAGUE Core remains independent.
