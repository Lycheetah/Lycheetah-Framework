# Calibration Plan

The v0.1 defaults are test scaffolding.

## Stage 1 — Synthetic calibration

Freeze cases covering strong replicated contradiction, single-source novelty, high-impact weak evidence, compatible evidence, provenance failure, zero uncertainty, ties, local corrections and adversarial score inflation.

## Stage 2 — Historical retrospective

Use domains with documented paradigm change. Freeze only the evidence available at each historical date.

## Stage 3 — Inter-rater reliability

Independent reviewers score each dimension. Measure agreement, disagreement by dimension, domain-expertise effects and institutional-position effects.

## Stage 4 — Predictive validity

Test whether higher pre-resolution Truth Pressure predicts later replication, foundation revision, narrowed surviving claims, false alarms and retractions.

## Stage 5 — Threshold fitting

Fit thresholds on a training split and evaluate on a held-out split. Report false-positive and false-negative costs.
