# Cascade Handoff

Truth Pressure does not perform a Cascade.

Only `CASCADE_CANDIDATE` may open a Cascade review.

The Cascade implementation must:

1. snapshot the foundation
2. preserve all evidence branches
3. enumerate dependent claims
4. execute independent tests
5. compare challenger and foundation
6. preserve useful scope of the prior model
7. record dissent
8. require explicit promotion approval
9. retain rollback

No automatic foundation overwrite is permitted.
