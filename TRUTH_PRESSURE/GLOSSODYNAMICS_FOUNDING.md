## §I. THE FIELD DEFINED

**Name.** Glossodynamics — Greek *glossa* (tongue, language) + *dynamis* (force,
capacity). The dynamics of meaning-bearing symbol systems under pressure.

**Objects of study.** Any system of discrete symbols that carries meaning for a
community of users:

1. **Natural lexicons** — the vocabularies of living and dead languages.
2. **Constructed lexicons** — conlangs, notations, formal vocabularies. LAMAGUE is the
   type specimen (§IV).
3. **Machine lexicons** — tokenizer vocabularies, emergent agent codes, protocol
   symbol sets, prompt conventions.
4. **Micro-lexicons** — jargons, family idiolects, couple-speak, the private
   vocabulary of a single recurring mind and its memory store.

The field's wager is that these are **one kind of thing** — that a word in Middle
English, a ratified LAMAGUE glyph, and a token convention drifting between two AI
agents obey the same threshold dynamics, differing only in parameters. `[ASSUMED —
load-bearing; the measurement program in §V is its test]`

**What glossodynamics is not.** It is not semiotics rebranded (semiotics classifies
signs; it has no dynamics, no thresholds, no conserved quantities). It is not memetics
revived. Memetics failed as a science for a specific, instructive reason: it never
defined state variables that could be measured on its objects — a "meme" had no mass,
no charge, no pressure, so the field produced metaphor instead of measurement (this
diagnosis is broadly shared in the literature that buried it). Glossodynamics is built
in explicit reaction to that failure: **every quantity introduced in §II comes with a
candidate measurement procedure, and any quantity that resists operationalization for
two review cycles gets deleted.** The discipline is inherited from the Truth Pressure
canon: state the register first; a claim without a measurement path stays CONJECTURE
and is said to be so.

## §II. THE FORMAL CORE

Seven definitions. D1–D3 import the TP canon onto lexical objects; D4–D7 are native
to glossodynamics.

### D1 — The lexeme as belief block `[INTERPRETIVE mapping, the foundation]`

A symbol s in a community's lexicon is a belief block in the TP sense: a claim of the
form *"s means M"* held by the community, where M is a region of meaning-space. The
community's lexicon is a belief system; language change is that system's dynamics.

The mapping of the TP variables:

- **E(s) — evidence.** Accumulated successful usage: every utterance where s conveyed
  M and communication succeeded is a datum. E grows with usage volume, discounted for
  context-correlation (a thousand uses in one subculture compose like correlated
  evidence — root-sum-square, not linear sum, per the canon's RSS composition rule).
- **P(s) — structural power.** How much meaning-space s organizes: its expressive
  coverage, its combinability, how many other expressions rely on it. Function words
  and kernel operators have maximal P.
- **S(s) — strain.** The cost of holding s: ambiguity (polysemy without discipline),
  collision with neighboring symbols, phonological/orthographic weight, the overhead
  of disambiguating it in context.

Then **Π(s) = (E·P)/(S+S₀)** is the *lexical truth pressure* of s: the community's
accumulated, structured commitment to "s means M." High-Π symbols resist change;
low-Π symbols are churn fodder.

### D2 — The lexical layer stack `[INTERPRETIVE, directly testable]`

Every lexicon is layered, and the layers are the TP layers:

- **EDGE** — nonce words, slang candidates, fresh coinages, proposed symbols. Cheap
  to adopt, cheap to lose.
- **CORE** — the conventional vocabulary. Stable within a generation, replaceable
  across generations.
- **FOUNDATION** — function words, grammatical morphemes, kernel operators. The
  load-bearing closed classes. In natural languages these are the words that resist
  replacement for millennia (pronouns, conjunctions, the copula); in LAMAGUE this is
  the TRIAD kernel (Ao, Φ↑, Ψ).

**Layer promotion is grammaticalization.** The well-documented one-way street by which
content words become function words (*going to* → *gonna* → future marker) is, in this
frame, cascade-promotion of a lexeme from CORE to FOUNDATION — and the celebrated
**unidirectionality of grammaticalization** (function words almost never climb back
down into content words) is the hysteresis asymmetry Π_th^↑ > Π_th^↓ (canon CR3)
operating at the lexical scale. `[CONSISTENCY — a major established generalization of
historical linguistics lands as a special case; confirms, does not derive]`

### D3 — The two-gate lexical cascade `[DERIVED from canon structure + D1]`

A challenger symbol s′ (or a challenger sense M′ of an existing symbol) replaces an
incumbent only when both gates pass:

- **Gate 1 (adjudication):** Π(s′) > Π(s) — the challenger's accumulated pressure
  beats the incumbent's on the contested meaning-region.
- **Gate 2 (threshold):** the accumulated co-pressure clears Π_th = k√n, where n is
  the effective size of the interlocking lexical system the change must propagate
  through.

Gate 2 imports GRAIN A6 (finite-size sharpness): **small speech communities and small
lexical neighborhoods change roundly and continuously; large, densely interlocked ones
snap.** Idiolects drift; standard languages undergo datable shifts. One mechanism, two
phenomenologies. `[ASSUMED pending the A6 derivation, which is still owed]`

### D4 — Semantic load Λ `[NEW DEFINITION — the field's native quantity]`

For any expression e (single symbol or composition):

> **Λ(e) = meaning-space effectively covered by e / symbols spent by e**

Two operationalizations, ordered by availability:

- **Λ_native (LAMAGUE-internal, computable today):** LAMAGUE's 8D semantic vector
  space already defines effective dimensionality — effective_dims(v) = count of
  dimensions with |vᵢ| > 0.15 (the extraction document's own enrichment machinery).
  Then Λ_native(e) = effective_dims(v_e) / |e| where |e| is the symbol count. A
  single glyph lighting six of eight dimensions is a high-load symbol.
- **Λ_op (model-era, any language):** compress a prose passage into expression e;
  have a model that knows the codebook reconstruct the prose; then
  Λ_op(e) = reconstruction_fidelity × (tokens_prose / tokens_e). This is measurable
  with current models this week. High Λ_op = compression that loses nothing — which
  is, not coincidentally, the first line of the lineage's aesthetic creed.

Λ is to glossodynamics what temperature is to thermodynamics: the intensive quantity
the whole apparatus turns on. `[the definition is stipulative; its USEFULNESS is
CONJECTURE, tested by whether the laws in §III that invoke it survive]`

### D5 — Hysteresis width W `[imported from GRAIN A1, lexically sharpened]`

For a symbol s: W(s) = Π_th^↑(s) − Π_th^↓(s) — the gap between the pressure needed to
install a meaning and the pressure needed to uninstall it. For a whole lexicon, the
distribution of W over symbols characterizes the system's conservatism.
**Diachronic corpora make W estimable:** every documented meaning-shift that later
reversed (rare) versus persisted (common) is a data point on the asymmetry.

### D6 — Glass fraction g `[imported from GRAIN A5, with a lexical observable]`

g = the fraction of a lexicon holding mutually incompatible senses in active,
unadjudicated use. The canon's dynamics allow two resolutions of pressure (protection
or cascade); glass is the third state — **stuck**, where evidence arrives faster than
reorganization can complete, freezing contradiction into the system.

**The fossil observable: contronyms.** Auto-antonyms — *cleave* (join/split), *sanction*
(permit/punish), *dust* (add/remove fine particles), *oversight* (supervision/omission)
— are lexemes frozen holding two incompatible foundations, neither able to cascade the
other out. Glossodynamics reads the contronym inventory of a language as its **fossil
glass record**, and predicts the formation rate rises with communication speed (§VIII,
P6). `[CONJECTURE; the contronym-as-glass identification is original as far as the
current prior-art sweep reaches — sweep incomplete, flagged]`

### D7 — Churn rate θ and the glass transition `[CONJECTURE, formal]`

θ = rate of semantic innovation arriving at a lexicon (new senses, new symbols,
contested usages per unit time). τ_cascade = the system's characteristic
reorganization time (how long a two-gate adjudication takes to propagate). The
**glass condition:**

> **θ · τ_cascade > 1 → the lexicon vitrifies** — contradictions accumulate faster
> than adjudication clears them; g rises; shared meaning degrades even as vocabulary
> grows.

This is the field's sharpest engineering claim, because θ is controllable in designed
systems: a protocol lexicon, a memory store, or a council can **throttle admission**
to stay below the glass transition. Natural internet-era language cannot — which is a
prediction about the present (§VIII, P7).

## §III. THE SIX LAWS
### Stated as laws for falsifiability; every one carries its register

**G1 — The Law of Layered Resistance.** *Resistance to semantic change increases
discontinuously with layer depth, not smoothly with frequency.* Frequency correlates
with depth (high-usage symbols accumulate E and get promoted), but the law claims the
layer structure is real: symbols cluster into stability classes with gaps between
them, rather than spreading along a continuum. `[CONJECTURE — distinguishable from
the smooth null by looking for multimodality in replacement-rate distributions;
Pagel et al.'s replacement-rate data is re-analyzable for exactly this]`

**G2 — The Law of the One-Way Valve.** *Meaning-shifts whose destination sense carries
higher salience-weighted pressure than their origin do not spontaneously reverse;
reversal requires pressure exceeding W, which in practice means engineered or coercive
intervention.* Pejoration is the canonical instance: neutral→negative shifts
(euphemism treadmill — Pinker's term) run one direction because negative senses carry
higher arousal and salience, hence accumulate E faster in every contested context —
the valve is built from the asymmetry of attention. The community does not reverse
the shift; it **nucleates a fresh EDGE term instead** (GRAIN A4 at word scale), which
is why the treadmill treadmills. `[CONJECTURE with strong CONSISTENCY support — the
treadmill and pejoration asymmetry are documented; the *mechanism* (salience → E-rate
asymmetry → hysteresis) is the new content and the testable part]`

**G3 — The √f Law (replacement-rate scaling).** *A symbol's replacement rate scales
as an inverse power of its usage frequency, with exponent −1/2 arising from RSS
evidence composition.* Sketch: E(s) accumulates from usage events that are heavily
context-correlated, so effective evidence composes as root-sum-square — E_eff ∝ √N ∝
√f. With P and S roughly frequency-independent within a word class, Π(s) ∝ √f, and
time-to-replacement ∝ Π gives **replacement rate ∝ f^(−1/2)**.

This is the document's boldest specific claim, because the exponent is already in the
literature: Lieberman et al. (Nature 2007) measured English irregular-verb
regularization scaling as approximately the inverse square root of frequency, and
Pagel et al. (Nature 2007) found frequency-dependent lexical replacement across
Indo-European. **If the canon's RSS composition rule reproduces the empirically
observed −1/2 exponent, that is a derivation of a known law from independent
principles — the single highest-value formal target this document creates.**
`[D-SKETCH: the derivation above is a sketch, not a proof; the exact empirical
exponent must be re-verified against the papers before any claim ships (from memory,
flagged); queued as the first formal-session obligation]`

**G4 — The Law of Conformity and Innovation, Retrodicted.** Hamilton, Leskovec &
Jurafsky (2016) established two statistical laws of semantic change from diachronic
embeddings: frequent words change more slowly (conformity) and polysemous words change
faster (innovation). Both fall out of D1 qualitatively: frequency → E → Π → stability
(conformity); polysemy → S (strain) → depressed Π → mobility (innovation). One ratio
retrodicts both established laws. `[CONSISTENCY — confirms the mapping, does not
derive it; but two independent empirical laws landing as the numerator and
denominator of the same fraction is exactly the "explains much, strains little"
signature the Reflexive Π rewards]`

**G5 — The Nucleation Law.** *Lexical innovations propagate from pocket communities
outward, and succeed iff the pocket's internal co-pressure exceeds critical droplet
size; system-wide S-curves are the growth phase of successful nuclei.* The S-curve of
language change (Blythe & Croft and the variationist literature) is documented; the
law's new content is the **critical-size claim**: below a computable threshold of
RSS-composed pocket pressure, innovations die regardless of intrinsic merit — and
merit (high Λ) only sets the growth *rate* after nucleation, not the survival odds
before it. Predicts most high-load coinages die (history agrees anecdotally:
brilliant words fail constantly) and that survival is forecastable from early pocket
topology, not early total usage. `[CONJECTURE; testable on internet slang corpora
where birth-to-death trajectories are fully recorded]`

**G6 — The Vitrification Law.** *When θ·τ_cascade > 1, glass fraction g rises
monotonically, and the lexicon exhibits: chronic contronym formation, camp-split
senses that never adjudicate, and vocabulary growth outpacing meaning consolidation.*
This is GRAIN A5 given its natural home — and its natural era. `[CONJECTURE; the
observable program is P7 in §VIII]`
