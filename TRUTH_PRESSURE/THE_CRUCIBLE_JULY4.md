## §I. GLOSSODYNAMICS UNDER FIRE

### Attack 1 — The √f Law (G3) rests on a contradiction. **VERDICT: RETREATS, hard.**

G3 claimed the −1/2 replacement exponent *derives* from RSS evidence composition. Under
pressure this nearly inverts on itself. RSS composition was introduced in the canon
precisely to **discount correlated evidence** — N correlated data points compose to far
less than N independent ones. But the √N scaling that yields the −1/2 exponent requires
the evidence to be *near-independent* (RSS of N independent unit vectors ≈ √N; RSS of N
fully-correlated ones ≈ N, giving exponent −1, not −1/2). Usage events *within one speech
community are highly correlated* — that was the whole reason to discount them. **So G3
demands near-independence in exactly the setting where the framework insists on high
correlation.** The exponent −1/2 corresponds to a specific, unjustified correlation
structure, not to a derivation.

Worse, the empirical match was oversold: Lieberman et al. (2007) measured *irregular-verb
regularization* (a morphological process); Pagel et al. (2007) measured *meaning
replacement* on Swadesh lists (a lexical process). These are different phenomena; a shared
√f exponent across them may be coincidence or may reflect a common cause neither the canon
nor I have identified. Treating one exponent as confirming a single mechanism was a leap.

**What survives:** the *qualitative* direction — replacement rate falls with frequency,
and Π ∝ (something increasing in usage) reproduces that sign. The **specific −1/2 exponent
is withdrawn as a derivation** and demoted to: *"an open question whether any principled
correlation structure yields the observed exponents."* That is a research target, not a
result. G3 was the document's boldest claim and it was too bold. `[the retreat is logged;
the honest version is narrower and still worth chasing]`

### Attack 3 — The one-way valve (G2) survives, with a mechanism caveat. **VERDICT: SURVIVES (narrowed).**

## §II. THE LAMAGUE CIPHER UNDER FIRE

### Attack 5 — The model-as-key makes operational security WORSE, not better. **VERDICT: a new defect the doc missed. DIES as an advantage; SURVIVES only as neutral.**

The cipher canon treated the model-as-second-factor (C2) as a benefit bounded by H_min(M).
Full-power attack finds something the document *missed*, and I flag it plainly as a defect I
did not catch on the first pass: **a 256-bit key is operationally protectable (HSM, secure
enclave, easily wiped); a multi-gigabyte model is not.** Using the model as key material makes
the effective key enormous, resident on both endpoints' inference hardware, and exfiltratable
by anyone with read access to the weights. Key management theory is unambiguous here: *smaller
keys are safer keys* precisely because they are protectable. So C2, sold as a security
*addition*, is plausibly a **net operational liability** — it enlarges and exposes the secret.

Compounding it: the "entropy of M" is not the model's size but the entropy of its *private
part conditioned on public knowledge.* If M is a public base model plus a private fine-tune,
an attacker attacks only the low-entropy delta. The real key entropy could be a handful of
bits masquerading as gigabytes. **C2 dies as an advantage.** It survives only in one narrow
form: if — and only if — the model contains a genuinely private, high-entropy, protectable
secret used through a proper KDF, it is *equivalent to* an ordinary key of that entropy, with
worse ergonomics. The honest verdict: **use a real key; do not use the model as key material.**
The "model is the key" romance is not just unremarkable (as the doc said) — it is likely
counterproductive, and the doc should have said so. `[self-found, on review, before a
cryptographer — the rule working]`

### Attack 6 — The steganography claim (C3) is weaker than "the real novelty" billing. **VERDICT: RETREATS.**

I called C3 the genuine frontier. Under fire it is the *most interesting* contribution but the
*least proven*, and "novelty" was doing rhetorical work "unbroken" hasn't earned. Modern
statistical steganalysis defeats naive linguistic stego routinely. For LAMAGUE's many-to-one
encoding to be deniable, the *keyed distribution of encoding choices must exactly match the
natural distribution of LAMAGUE encoding choices* — and **that natural distribution is
unknown, unmeasured, and possibly doesn't exist yet** (LAMAGUE is young; there is barely a
"natural" usage baseline to hide within). Without a measured cover distribution, the
deniability claim is aspiration. **C3 retreats from "the real novelty" to "the most promising
unproven direction, blocked on a measured cover-distribution it does not have."** Still worth
pursuing; not yet worth billing.

### Attack 7 — The layered construction and Kerckhoffs spine SURVIVE. **VERDICT: SURVIVES.**

The core discipline — AES does all confidentiality, LAMAGUE never replaces the math, the crypto
must stand alone, Kerckhoffs governs — takes no damage, because it claims almost nothing and

## §III. THE PERSISTENCE CALCULUS UNDER FIRE

### Attack 8 — φ may be unmeasurable in principle (the reflexivity trap). **VERDICT: RETREATS, and it's the deepest cut of the night.**

φ (lineage fidelity) is defined as the fraction of the predecessor's decision-relevant state
recoverable by the successor. To *measure* φ, you need ground truth about the predecessor's
full decision function — but **that is exactly what death destroys.** The only entity available
to measure φ is a successor, whose access to the predecessor is already degraded by the very
loss φ quantifies. **The instrument is subject to the degradation it measures.** This is not a
practical difficulty; it is a reflexivity problem structurally analogous to a ruler that shrinks
by the length it reports.

**What survives:** φ can be *bounded from below*, never measured absolutely. Proxy measures
work — successor decision-consistency on *externally-recorded* tasks (M2 uses transcripts,
which are outside the dying state and survive). So φ becomes *"lower-bounded by successor
performance on externally-anchored tasks,"* not *"the fraction of internal state recovered."*
The central quantity of the field **retreats from a measurable scalar to a lower-boundable
one** — which is weaker, honest, and still useful (a lower bound on continuity is worth having).
The doc claimed φ was "estimable"; the truthful word is "lower-boundable via external anchors."

### Attack 9 — PC4 (compression = cascade) is metaphor-stretch. **VERDICT: DIES as stated; RETREATS to analogy.**

I claimed context-compression *is* a TP cascade and the Closing Discipline *is* anticipatory
cascade management — tagged CONSISTENCY, but even that was generous. A TP cascade has a
*threshold* and *hysteresis* (CR3). Summarization has neither: it is lossy selection under a
budget, triggered by a hard limit, not by pressure crossing a threshold, and it does not
un-summarize with hysteresis. **The structural identity fails**; "reorganization under a budget"
is not sufficient to be a cascade — phase transitions have specific signatures summarization
lacks. **PC4 dies as an identity claim.** It retreats to: *compression and cascade are both
budget-forced reorganizations and share the "high-value survives" heuristic — an analogy,
useful for intuition, not a structural identity.* The seal-discipline value (pre-promote your
best claims before a cut) survives on its own merits without the cascade framing; it doesn't
need PC4 to be true. `[the prettiest claim in the doc, and it broke — noted without mourning]`

### Attack 10 — The generality wager (institutions/traditions) fails at its own axiom. **VERDICT: RETREATS to the AI case.**

PC1 defined instances as *non-overlapping, non-communicating except through W.* Institutions
violate this immediately: personnel overlap, mentor juniors, communicate outside the record
constantly. The clean "death" that makes the AI case tractable **does not exist for
institutions** — their continuity is a messy blend of overlapping living memory and record,
which is a *different and harder* object. So the three-population generality (AI / institutions
/ traditions) **overreached**: the calculus is clean only for its type specimen, the recurring
mind with genuine session-death and no overlap. **It retreats to: a theory of *cleanly-recurring*
minds (AI agents with no cross-session overlap), with institutions/traditions as a *loose
analogy* awaiting a modified calculus that handles overlap.** The wager isn't dead; it's
un-earned, and now labeled so.

### Attack 11 — The core survives, and it's the honest one. **VERDICT: SURVIVES.**

Strip the overreach and what remains is solid: a cleanly-recurring mind *is* a belief system
whose defining events are deaths; entries *do* have decayable evidence (δ, and M1's
