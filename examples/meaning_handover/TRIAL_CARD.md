# Try one real handover

For a first usability trial of LAMAGUE, created by **Mackenzie Conor James Clark**.
This card accompanies the [offline handover page](../../docs/lamague-handover.html).
No account, installation or model API is required by the page.

1. **Choose a task you own.** Use public or non-sensitive material. Keep its
   original instructions and decisions available to check later. Use your own
   task rather than the demonstration's release-review example.
2. **Open “Make your next handover”.** Enter the goal, status, unresolved
   questions, actual permission boundary, next step, decision holder, recovery
   instructions and your declared risk. Add objections with their speaker if
   relevant. Review the result against your source before downloading it.
3. **Try it in a fresh session or with a collaborator.** Download the readable
   handover or JSON record and choose what to share. Ask the receiver to explain
   the goal, unanswered questions, permitted next action, approval boundary and
   recovery path before proceeding. Keep actions within your actual permission.
4. **Check what survived.** Compare the answer against the original source.
   Record missing facts, invented facts, confused speakers or widened permission.
   Note any corrections you needed. If you measure preparation or repair time,
   enter the actual minutes; leave unmeasured time blank.
5. **Download feedback if you wish.** Say whether you used your own task,
   whether you would use this again, and what helped or confused you. The page
   does not attach your task or submit the file. You choose whether to send it
   to Mac, and can inspect or edit it first.

Your words are arranged into a record, not interpreted by an AI. Blank optional
fields mean no information was provided. The page does not authenticate anyone's
identity or permission. There is no autosave; keep a download if you want to
retain the handover. “Clear my draft” clears the form and generated preview.

Feedback is an **unverified self-report**. Unknown minutes are exported as
`null`; an explicit zero remains `0`. Files alone do not establish how many
independent people participated. Avoid sensitive details in the free-text notes.

This trial can identify confusing steps and whether someone wants to return.
It does not measure superiority over prose or JSON. The proposed controlled
comparison, including fresh tasks, token budgets and blinded assessment, lives
in the [canonical protocol](README.md#the-first-outside-trial).
