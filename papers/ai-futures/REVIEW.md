## Remaining substantive limits

This is a selected-literature synthesis. It does not supply a comprehensive
review of cooperative game theory, political philosophy, teaching, machine
unlearning, or consciousness. It proposes no universal sovereignty metric.
The normative commitments remain discussable; observed performance alone cannot
decide them. Source ancestry, community legitimacy, workable correction, useful
learning, and the welfare indicators all require more than this document.
