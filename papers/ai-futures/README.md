# AI Futures: Persistent and Collective Intelligence

**Working research draft v0.2 · 3 October 2026 · Not empirically validated.**

**Framework creator and research owner:** Mackenzie Conor James Clark.
Research and drafting assistance: Caelorynth, an AI coding assistant.

[Human Sovereignty in Persistent and Collective AI](PAPER.md) develops the
Framework's memory, cooperation, learning, community-governance, and AI-welfare
questions into one connected research specification. It complements the
[agent-controls working paper](../agent-safety/README.md); that paper's simulator
does not validate these additional proposals.

Read:

1. [Paper](PAPER.md): proposed contracts, three elementary arguments, eight research tracks, and three study designs.
2. [Claims](CLAIMS.json): normative choices, deductions, literature observations, and untested hypotheses kept distinct.
3. [Sources](SOURCES.json): pinned local revisions, selected primary literature, inspection depths, and scope conflicts.
4. [Review and verification](REVIEW.md): the authoring seat's challenges and the checks actually performed.
5. [Local verification receipt](VERIFICATION.json): source, claim and link checks, with empirical limits.

This packet is research, not a second implementation board. No study has run;
there are no new model benchmarks, participant outcomes, clinical results,
consciousness findings, independent reviews, or established novelty claims.

The [experimental role-comparison kit](../../18_EXPERIMENTAL/agent-role-hybridisation/study/README.md)
is a local offline implementation supporting the role-identity question in §6.3.
It prepares prompts and review records without executing models. Its software
integrity checks are separate from the paper's untested research hypotheses.
