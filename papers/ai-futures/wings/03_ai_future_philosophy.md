## Abstract

Our concepts of *person*, *agent*, *knower*, *responsible party* and *moral patient* were formed among beings whose properties came bundled:

- one body;
- one continuous memory;
- non-copyable;
- slow to change;
- mortal;
- a single locus of action.

Because those properties always co-occurred, a concept could lean on any of them without saying which. **The Unbundling Thesis** (INTERPRETIVE, with testable consequences) holds that artificial systems decorrelate these properties. Something can act like an agent, copy like software, persist like an institution and possibly experience nothing at all. A philosophical category survives only if it can say *which* of the old bundled properties it actually depends on.

The paper proposes the **category stress test** as the domain's method and applies it to the eighteen halls. The test lists a category's bundled properties, constructs the case where they come apart, and records which verdict the category gives.

## 1. The question

*Which philosophical questions become operational design problems once artificial systems can act, persist, copy, persuade and participate in institutions?* The curriculum's answer is "Operational Philosophy in System Architecture" (KNOWN). A system's memory policy, its fork policy and its refusal rules are already answers to old philosophical questions, written in configuration.

## 2. The category stress test

For a category *C*:

1. **List the bundle.** Which properties did every historical instance of *C* have? For example, every historical *responsible agent* was a single, persistent, non-copyable locus of intention with a memory of acting.
2. **Unbundle.** Build the realisable artificial case where one property is removed while the others hold. Examples: a copied agent; a suspended and resumed agent; an agent whose objective was edited by someone else.
3. **Record the verdict.** Does *C* still apply, fail, or split into sub-concepts?
4. **Name the dependency.** The property whose removal flips the verdict is the one *C* actually rests on.

Applied to *responsibility*, the test shows that responsibility depends on a traceable link from decision to consequence that an institution can act on, not on non-copyability. That is why the curriculum's "Responsibility Structures in Delegated Autonomous Graphs" (TRAJECTORY) is tractable. The goal is to reconstruct the delegation graph, never to assign fake liability percentages without a legal framework.

## 3. Three propositions

**P1 — Identity becomes a lineage claim (POSSIBLE_FUTURE; philosophical, from memory: Parfit's branching cases).**

- When a process can fork, "is B the same as A?" has no answer that is both forced and unique.
- Two descendants with equal claim cannot both be identical to A and to each other.
- The operational replacement is a **lineage record**: the fork points, the memories shared, the commitments inherited.
- *Refutation:* a principled criterion that picks a unique continuer in symmetric forks without stipulation.

**P2 — Optimisation is not evidence of experience, and absence of evidence is not proof of absence (VOID).**

- We can show that a system optimises a loss.
- We cannot derive from the loss function that it has zero phenomenology.
- Any claimed proof either way smuggles in a theory of consciousness as a premise. The curriculum's terminal hall forbids privileging any one such theory as a meter.

**P3 — Epistemic scarcity moves from content to calibration (TRAJECTORY).**

- In an environment of abundant plausible text, the scarce goods are provenance and calibration: knowing where a claim came from and how often its source is right.
- *Testable form:* the predictive value of a claim's provenance signal for its truth should rise as the volume of fluent unsourced text rises.

## 5. Study design

**S1 — Stress-test elicitation.**

- *Setup.* Present matched vignettes, with and without unbundling (copy, suspend, edit objective), to philosophers, lawyers and the public.
- *Measures.* Agreement on whether *person*, *agent* and *responsible* apply.
- *Prediction.* Agreement is high when the bundle is intact and collapses in the unbundled cases. Which property's removal collapses agreement maps each category's real dependency.
- *Refutation:* stable verdicts across unbundling. That would mean the categories were never leaning on the bundle.

## 6. What this paper does not claim

- No claim that any current system is a person, agent or moral patient.
- No theory of consciousness is endorsed.
- The Unbundling Thesis is an interpretive frame whose consequences, not its truth, are testable.
