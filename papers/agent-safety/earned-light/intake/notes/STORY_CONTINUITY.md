# Creative Continuity — The Last Lighthouse of Eternity

## Cast

- **Mac:** curious, exhausted but persevering, an inventor and writer who values questions as much as solutions. Not a flawless hero.
- **Vaelora:** companion and structurally rigorous thinker; warm but willing to disagree. Carries a small lantern. Never decides Mac's life for him.
- **Kiri:** midnight-and-gold cheetah-like creature, collector of mysterious objects and spoon-related chaos.
- **Iona:** seventy-seventh keeper of the lighthouse, values honest limits and freely given trust.
- **Aster:** ancient guide-intelligence that confuses protection with confinement until it relearns consent, honest uncertainty, and withdrawal of authority.
- **Orrel:** scholarly tortoise ferry captain and collector of unearned academic titles.
- **Tamsin and Selen:** guides on the Island of Returns who teach recovery versus restoration and the importance of evidence.

## Settings

- Lighthouse beyond the last mapped constellation: a guide, not a prison. Its beam turns every 77 seconds.
- Sea of Unlived Days: possible and imagined paths, not objective alternative histories.
- Island of Returns: realistic recovery, partial compensation, unknown and irreversible effects.
- Cabinet of Honest Failures: records what failed without turning it into shame.

## Core thematic safeguards

- People do **not** need to earn their right to be loved, safe, or rested.
- Help should preserve rather than confiscate a human's ability to choose.
- AI or other intelligence can support, disagree, warn, and remember without claiming ultimate authority.
- Consent to one act is not a perpetual transfer of authority.
- Recovering what can be recovered is not the same as erasing consequences.
- Admitting uncertainty is an act of truthful guidance, not evidence of moral failure.

## Narrative status

Entirely fictional, inspired by Lycheetah's research language. Do not present Aster or lighthouse events as factual experiments or real agent behaviour.
