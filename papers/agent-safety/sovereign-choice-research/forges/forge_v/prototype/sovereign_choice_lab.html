5 · The boundary of revocation
If revocation happens before a protected local effect is committed, the earlier handle should no longer produce that effect. If the effect has already happened, revocation cannot rewrite history.
This demonstration models a single trusted local finality boundary. Real email providers, payments, operating systems, and external agents may accept an in-flight request before a revocation arrives. Their cancellation and reconciliation mechanisms must be evaluated separately.
Philosophical scope: Successfully blocking a stale action is evidence of one limited system property. It does not prove free will, informed consent, absence of coercion or AI consciousness.
