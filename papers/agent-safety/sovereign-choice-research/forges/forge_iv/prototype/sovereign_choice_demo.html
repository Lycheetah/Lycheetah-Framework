    This is an educational interface, not a secure authorisation service or connected AI. All events are held only in this page’s memory. “Send” records a simulated local effect; it sends no email. Once a simulated effect commits, later revocation cannot undo it. An actual system needs authenticated identity, independent policy checks, permission fencing at the real effect boundary, and provider reconciliation. No claims about consciousness or free will are inferred from the interface.
