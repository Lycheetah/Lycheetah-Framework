What this comparison does—and does not—show

In the original fixture set, the conventional goal-aware baseline ties the Lycheetah proposal on all 18 authored examples. That is the correct result to report. The additional sovereignty vocabulary has not demonstrated independent superiority.

Forced independence fails when someone wants accessibility support, repeated business administration or a continuing creative partnership. Equally, engagement optimisation can displace skill transfer or pressure someone who has explicitly declined.

The meaningful future study is not “which policy has the most appealing name?” It is whether a user can learn independently when they want to, receive ongoing help when they choose, contest a decision, change their mind, and leave without artificial penalties.
