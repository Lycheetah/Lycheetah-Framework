# New Zealand AI governance

**Creator:** Mackenzie Conor James Clark / Lycheetah Framework.  
**Current guide:** 19 October 2026. **Status:** [PROPOSAL] Proposed rights and experimental supporting research.

## Start here: one proposal, the same rights

[Data Sovereignty for New Zealanders](DATA_SOVEREIGNTY_FOR_NEW_ZEALANDERS.md) proposes six rights for every New Zealander when AI touches their data: know when AI is used, keep a receipt, access/correct and withdraw specified future uses, reach a human reviewer, know where data lives, and receive equal treatment.

Ancestry, faith, region and politics do not determine access to that baseline.
This is the current direction. It is a proposal, not law or an adopted national
standard. No iwi, agency, ministry or community endorsement is claimed.

## What exists, and what remains open

The package contains local receipt/policy and permission-control prototypes.
It does not yet implement an end-to-end citizen service, an independently
validated group-fairness test, universal data withdrawal or location attestation.
The six-rights paper identifies these gaps beside the relevant source tools.

[Anon Politician](anon_politician/README.md) is a separate experimental policy
analysis workstream: citizen input, evidence tracing, challenge and outcome
tracking. It is not an elected representative or an authorised government service.
