# Draft Question for a Monthly Citizen Signal

**Status:** Proposed wording for research and comprehension testing. Not a live poll. Not approved as a formal consultation instrument.

> In designing how infrastructure needed for new housing is funded, what should decision-makers give most weight to? Please identify your main priorities, explain the trade-off you are most concerned about, and describe what evidence could change your view.
>
> Examples of affected interests include people seeking homes, current residents and ratepayers, developers, councils, central government and future residents. This list does not imply a preferred answer.

## Before asking the public

Pilot the wording with people from different backgrounds to identify confusing or leading language. Decide whether the aim is exploratory input, a representative survey, deliberation or formal consultation; these require different methods. Publish answer choices, sample plan, eligibility, privacy terms, how responses will be analysed, and how the institution will respond.

## Optional follow-up questions to test in research

1. Which outcomes matter most to you: timely delivery of needed infrastructure, housing costs, transparent charges, fair cost allocation, fiscal sustainability, local impacts, intergenerational effects, or something else? Allow participants to rank or explain in their own words; state that the list is not exhaustive.
2. Who do you believe should pay for different kinds of growth-related infrastructure, and why?
3. What possible harm or trade-off worries you most?
4. What information would help you decide?
5. After reading a balanced, source-linked explanation of the proposal and alternatives, has your view changed, stayed the same, or become less certain? Why?
6. What important perspective or option is missing?

Do not treat results as population-representative unless the sampling and analysis support that conclusion. Report changes of mind, uncertainty and minority concerns rather than only a headline percentage.
