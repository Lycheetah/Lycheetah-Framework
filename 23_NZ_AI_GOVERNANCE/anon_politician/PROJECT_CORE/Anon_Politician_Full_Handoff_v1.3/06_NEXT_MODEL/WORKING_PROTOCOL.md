# Working protocol for the next model

## What to preserve

- AI assists politicians; AI does not make the political decision.
- Citizens have a recurring opportunity to provide input on policy questions and should receive a clear account of how their input was considered.
- A monthly cadence is the founder's starting concept, to be researched and tested rather than assumed ideal.
- CASCADE preserves claims, evidence, dependencies, contradictions, and history; upstream change triggers a review queue, never automatic truth changes.
- Adversarial models should help different perspectives challenge and improve ideas; party-informed models are a proposal requiring independent safeguards.
- Forecasting should compare plausible futures, policy options, and the status quo, with uncertainty and later calibration checks.
- The intended incentive is to make listening, explaining trade-offs, revising policies, and delivering outcomes a source of competitive leadership credibility.

## How to work

1. Inspect the original material and existing code before redesigning.
2. Label facts, source claims, hypotheses, normative commitments, external research, and unknowns.
3. For fresh New Zealand policy/law/institutional claims, use dated primary sources.
4. Challenge the founder's assumptions as rigorously as government or party assumptions.
5. Compare with existing approaches and simpler baselines before claiming novelty.
6. Avoid single unvalidated scores, partisan ranking, political persuasion, and false claims of scientific validation.
7. Build only what the current experiment needs; add tests and record assumptions.
8. Keep source fidelity and privacy in view, especially when preparing public outputs.

## Definition of done for the first demonstrator

- One precisely scoped policy question and rationale for selection.
- Complete evidence ledger with provenance and limitations.
- Public-input representation and explicit sampling caveat.
- At least two perspective-specific adversarial reviews and a fair response process.
- Scenario or forecast analysis that states when available evidence is inadequate.
- CASCADE dependency/contradiction review queue and version history.
- Comparison against ordinary analysis and a static evidence ledger.
- Independently reviewed evaluation rubric, explicit failure criteria, and published limitations.
- A human-readable report connecting public input, evidence, objections, revisions, decision rationale, and planned outcome monitoring.

## Civic signal design (added v1.3)

Use the Civic Signal Integrity Kit under `08_CIVIC_SIGNAL_v0.3/`. Keep open public voice, probability-sample population measurement, deliberative panels, stakeholder submissions and statutory consultation distinct. Do not let an opt-in poll become national consensus through wording, visualisation or AI summarisation. Ask pre/post information questions only when the exposure and question wording are recorded. Preserve minority and high-severity concerns. Require a public decision-response ledger showing what was heard, what changed or did not change, why, who owns follow-up, and what triggers reconsideration. Validator tests check declared metadata only and do not certify representativeness.
