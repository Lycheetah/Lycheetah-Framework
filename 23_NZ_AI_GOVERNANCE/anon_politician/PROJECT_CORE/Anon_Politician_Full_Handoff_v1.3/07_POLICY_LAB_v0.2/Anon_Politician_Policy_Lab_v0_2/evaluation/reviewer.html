      3. Score each candidate by dimension

      Use 0 for materially deficient, 1 for mixed/incomplete, 2 for adequate for the stated task, or N/A where the dimension truly cannot be assessed. Ratings are a structured record of judgment, not objective truth. Cite snippets or source passages in notes when possible.

      Select every issue observed for each candidate. A critical issue must be investigated separately and must not be averaged away by other ratings.
